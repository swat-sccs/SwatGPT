# Teaching Remotely

Whether holding a fully online class or teaching a few class sessions remotely, your Academic Technologist can provide recommendations for tools to use and how to structure your course. 

## Overall Guidance for Online Teaching

  * [Best Practices for Teaching Remotely](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Best_Practices_for_Teaching_Remotely "Best Practices for Teaching Remotely") \- a quick read that covers the major topics to consider when setting up an online course.
  * [Accessibility and Online Teaching](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Accessibility_and_Online_Teaching "Accessibility and Online Teaching") \- a set of recommendations focused on making online courses more accessible to all students



## Technology for Teaching

  * [Tools for Online Teaching](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Tools_for_Online_Teaching "Tools for Online Teaching") \- a summary of common teaching needs and how to provide them for an online class.
  * [Online Teaching Resources](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Online_Teaching_Resources "Online Teaching Resources") \- a collection of links to information for online teaching, including general and discipline specific recommendations

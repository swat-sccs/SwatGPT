## Description

Padlet is a easy-to-use service to create bulletin boards to collect, organize, and present information. Often used in student projects to share images, maps, audio, and video. Faculty can create a shared board for students to post information. Its strength is simplicity. 

[![Digital Padlet board titled Favorite Literary Quotes with multiple text notes displaying quotes from various books against a background of book spines](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/b/b5/Padlet_1777660448824.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:Padlet_1777660448824.png)

  
The Padlet Sandbox feature is a “collaborative digital canvas with tools for drawing, writing, and adding media.” 

## Login

Anyone with a Swarthmore account can log into the paid version of Padlet at [swarthmorecollege.padlet.org/auth/login](https://swarthmorecollege.padlet.org/auth/login). Use Google to complete the login process. 

Faculty can set up [a link from Moodle to automatically enroll students in a Padlet](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Add_Padlet_to_a_Moodle_Course "Add Padlet to a Moodle Course").

## About

This space features a microphone and audio interface that can be used to record voiceover, sound effects, and musical instruments. 

It also features an audio mixer, 4 microphones and headphones, and a computer to record and edit your podcasts or conversational recordings through [Adobe Audition](https://www.adobe.com/products/audition/podcast-recording-software.html), [Garageband](https://support.apple.com/guide/garageband/welcome/mac), or [Audacity](https://manual.audacityteam.org/). You may also export your recordings to the computer, save them on an external hard drive or cloud storage such as Google Drive, and edit them on your own computer or at the [Language Center and Creative Media Commons](https://www.swarthmore.edu/language-center-and-creative-media-commons).

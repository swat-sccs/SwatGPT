# Sign Into Zoom for Swarthmore Users

## Sign In With the Zoom Application

  1. Open your Zoom application.  _(If you need to download and install the application, go to<https://swarthmore.zoom.us>. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click "**Download Zoom Client** ".)_
  2. Select "**Sign In with SSO** ".  
[![Zoom login window with Single Sign-On \(SSO\) option highlighted for Swarthmore users](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/8/8a/Sign_Into_Zoom_for_Swarthmore_Users_1778803121147.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:Sign_Into_Zoom_for_Swarthmore_Users_1778803121147.png)
  3. Enter "**Swarthmore** " as the company domain. This will take you to the Swarthmore login page. Enter your Swarthmore username and password > then go through the Duo process.
  4. You can also sign in via a web browser to schedule or start a meeting. Go to <https://swarthmore.zoom.us> and sign in with Swarthmore College username and password and go through the Duo process.

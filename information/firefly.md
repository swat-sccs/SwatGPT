## Getting Started

  1. Go to [firefly.adobe.com](http://firefly.adobe.com) (VPN not required).
  2. Sign in with your Swarthmore Google account.
  3. Enter a description of the image you would like to see.
  4. Firefly will provide several options. From here, click on an image to see additional refinements or use the tools to adjust the style of the image.

[![Adobe Firefly text-to-image interface with settings for model, aspect ratio, content type, and composition, plus generated images of a college campus in fall](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/c/c2/Firefly_1777490250613.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:Firefly_1777490250613.png)

  


## Video

As of Summer 2026, Firefly has a video generation option, but Adobe is not offering it to educational accounts. Each user has a limited number of credits per day. Typically this can be used to generate hundreds of images, but there is a limit.

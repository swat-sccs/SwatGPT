# iPad Jamf Self Service

ITS uses an Apple Device Management system called [Jamf Self Service](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jamf-self-service/id718509958) to manage the installation of all apps on College-owned iPad devices. It shows up on your iPad as an app that looks like a colorful square. 

[![Jamf Self Service app icon with four rounded squares in green, blue, yellow, and red surrounding four smaller colored dots](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/b/b5/jamf_icon.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:jamf_icon.png)

You should see the Self Service app already on your iPad after you complete the initial iPad setup. In order to install any College-supported app on your iPad, follow the instructions below. 

  1. Select the Self Service app. Here, you will see several apps for you to install.
  2. Select **Install** next to the apps you'd like to have on your iPad.

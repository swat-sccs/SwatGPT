## How do I print a poster?

Follow the instructions below. Laminated copies are available in the Media Commons itself, and electronic pdfs with screenshots are available for download at the bottom of this page. 

  1. Download your pdf and open it in the Preview application. _Locate your download, right click, and select Open With > Preview_
  2. Send your file to the large format printer using the 48x35 inch paper size. _Click File > Print..._ _In the Printer drop-down menu, select option B) Print Standard Large Format Poster_ _In the Paper Size drop-down menu, select Standard Poster 48x35_ Ensure _scale to fit_ is selected, and that scale reads at 110% or below. Scaling above 110% may yeild pixelated images. If you created your poster at the appropriate size (48x35 inches) this will not be a problem. _Click Print._ You will be prompted with two pop-up messages. One asks to confirm that your project is for curricular purposes, and the second will notify you that your print has been held in a queue. Read and click through both messages.
  3. Release your print job from the computer station in the adjoining printer room. Unlike other printers on campus, you cannot swipe your OneCard to release a print. You must sign in to the print release station on top of the filing cabinets using your normal Swarthmore credentials. You will then see the option to print your file.



### _Additional Notes - Posters that do not adhere to the 48x35 standard_

If your poster is reasonably close to standard size, consider following directions on the opposite side of this document as they are written. Excess paper from smaller posters can be trimmed using the large paper cutter in this room, and larger posters can be scaled down to 48x35 without distortion. 

If you need to print a poster that is significantly smaller or larger than the 48x35 standard, please use the tips below to easily modify instructions on the opposite side of this document. You may also ask one of our student consultants for assistance. 

_**Undersized Posters:**_ Complete step 1. Complete step 2 as written, but modify it by selecting 

"Manage Custom Sizes" at the very bottom of the **__Paper Size__** drop-down menu. When prompted, edit the "Custom Poster" size according to your needs. Please do NOT edit the _Standard Poster 48x35_ preset. 

_**Oversized Posters:**_ Complete step 1. In step 2, select option _C) Print Oversized Large Format Poster._

Further modify step 2 by selecting "Manage Custom Sizes" at the very bottom of the **__Paper Size__** drop-down menu. When prompted, edit the "Custom Poster" size according to your needs. One dimension _must be 43" or less_. It can be either dimension; the other can be larger. 

Please do NOT edit the _Standard Poster 48x35 preset_. 

Please ask the person on duty to load a 44-inch roll of paper into the printer. 

### _Common Troubleshooting Steps_

  * Problem: Poster is too small or pixelated Solution: Check page setup dimensions in your original document and in your pdf. Adjust the original document as needed and confirm the PDF has retained the correct dimensions on export.
  * Problem: Poster will not show up in release station Solution: Confirm these laminated directions were followed precisely. If yes, and if problem persists, try printing from a different computer.

## A Note on Standard Poster Dimensions

Most posters are 35”x48” (Sigma Psi), but some are larger (35” x 60” or even bigger).  You always need to make sure that your print size is 1" smaller than the width of the paper roll you intend to use.  So for a 44" roll, you should set one dimension to 43" or less; for a 36" roll, set one dimension to 35" or less. The print processing system will automatically rotate your image to use the least paper possible.  Feel free to check with a Creative Media Commons student employee if you're worried about how something will print out. For additional details about setting up posters correctly, [see the FAQ.](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Large_Format_Poster_Settings_\(Page_Setup\)_%26_FAQ "Large Format Poster Settings \(Page Setup\) & FAQ")

## Installation Instructions

To install Mathematica, log into the [Wolfram Products Page](http://www.wolfram.com/siteinfo) with your Swarthmore email address. You will be prompted to log in with your Swarthmore credentials. From there you can download the installer package. 

You will also use your Swarthmore email address to log into Mathematica once it is installed. 

## Mathematica Online

Go to [mathematica.wolframcloud.com](http://mathematica.wolframcloud.com) to use Mathematica online. Log in with your Swarthmore credentials. 

## High Performance Computing

See [Running Mathematica on Firebird](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Running_Mathematica_on_Firebird "Running Mathematica on Firebird") for instructions on running Mathematica on Swarthmore's computing cluster.

## What is JMP Student Edition?

JMP Student Edition is no-cost FREE statistical analysis software for class use. 

For additional information please see <http://jmp.com/student>

## Download the JMP Student Edition

To download the JMP Student Edition installer: 

  1. [Verify via SSO with your Swarthmore email address](https://my.jmp.com/jmpportal/MyJmpLogin?ec=302&startURL=%2Fjmpportal%2Fs%2Fjmp-student-edition) to access the installer.
  2. After completing the steps, proceed with downloading the JMP Student Edition installer.
  3. [Sign in to My JMP](https://community.jmp.com/t5/Administrator-Knowledge-Base/How-to-Sign-in-to-My-JMP/ta-p/744059).
  4. Click the download icon for the software to download.
  5. Below the installation instructions, click the download icon for the desired operating system.

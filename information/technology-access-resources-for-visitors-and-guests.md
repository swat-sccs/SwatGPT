## Visitors

  * **Wireless Access:** All visitors can connect to campus Wi-Fi using the [**SwatGuest**](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Self-Service_Guest_Wireless "Self-Service Guest Wireless") network. 
    * Visitors at **101 S. Chester** will need an authorization code to join SwatGuest.
  * **Printing:** SwatGuest users cannot print across campus. 
    * Visitors may print only at **McCabe Library** using public computers.
    * Ask the **Circulation Desk** for assistance.
  * **Library Computers:** McCabe Library offers a guest login for use on public computers.



## Sponsored Guests

### Short-term Guests

  * Use [**SwatGuest**](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Self-Service_Guest_Wireless "Self-Service Guest Wireless") for wireless access.
  * To log in to a College computer, the sponsoring department or faculty member must sign in on the guest’s behalf.
  * If a guest needs an adapter/dongle for a presentation, these can be requested through [**Classroom and Conferencing Technologies**](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Classroom_and_Conferencing_Technology_Support "Classroom and Conferencing Technology Support").



### Printing

  * Printing is not available through SwatGuest; sponsored guests should work with their sponsoring department for printing.
  * Guests can print from the **Libraries** via the [ _public computers_](https://www.swarthmore.edu/libraries/visitor-information).



### Moodle Access (Guests Not Registered in a Course)

  * Faculty may grant Moodle **guest access** : [Logging into Moodle at Swarthmore](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Log_in_to_Moodle_at_Swarthmore#Special-Swarthmore-Users-and-Guests "Log in to Moodle at Swarthmore")


  * Guests who want **course credit** must be [officially registered through the Registrar](https://www.swarthmore.edu/registrar/registration).



### Extended Guest Access

Faculty and staff may request longer-term accounts for guests who need more than basic SwatGuest access. 

  * **Extending SwatGuest**
    * SwatGuest accounts expire after 4 days.
    * Sponsors can request an extension before expiration by emailing the Help Desk: [support@swarthmore.edu](mailto:support@swarthmore.edu)
  * **Temporary Accounts** (for research or similar needs) 
    * Faculty or staff can request a temporary account for a sponsored guest through the Help Desk: [support@swarthmore.edu](mailto:support@swarthmore.edu)
    * Available options include: 
      * Wireless (internet only)
      * Network account (login to [eduroam](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Wireless#eduroam "Wireless") and computers)
      * Google account (email and Google apps)



### Support for Presentations

Classroom and Conferencing Technologies provides AV support in classrooms. For available equipment, see [HD classroom details](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/HD_Classrooms "HD Classrooms"). 

### Access Extensions for Graduating Seniors

  * Accounts may be extended for research projects.
  * The request must come from the sponsoring faculty member and include the project purpose and end date.
  * Graduating seniors cannot continue as student workers. If they will be hired, the hiring must go through HR.



## Tri-Co Guests

  * Users from **Haverford** or **Bryn Mawr** should connect using [**eduroam**](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Wireless#\[inlineExtension\]eduroam-for-Swarthmore,-Haverford,-Bryn-Mawr,-and-other-eduroam-institutions "Wireless"), the College’s authenticated wireless network.
  * Tri-Co students who are **registered** for a Swarthmore course can [log into **Moodle**](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Log_in_to_Moodle_at_Swarthmore#Bryn-Mawr-College,-Haverford-College,-University-of-Pennsylvania,-and-Strath-Haven-High-School-students "Log in to Moodle at Swarthmore") using their Swarthmore credentials.
  * Students who are **not** registered but need course access may request Moodle guest access directly from the instructor.

[Preparing For Graduation](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Preparing_For_Graduation "Preparing For Graduation")

    * [**Swarthmore Alumni Online Community**](https://www.swarthmore.edu/alumni-resources-events/alumni-portal)

<https://www.swarthmore.edu/libraries/alumni-borrowing>

## Parents

  * Due to **FERPA** , ITS cannot provide parents access to their student’s accounts: <https://www.swarthmore.edu/parents/student-records-and-parental-notification>


  * For billing questions or issues with E-Bills, contact the **Student Accounts Office** : <https://www.swarthmore.edu/student-accounts-office/e-bill>



## Vendors

  * Vendors may receive limited access depending on project requirements.
  * The request must come from the Swarthmore department working with the vendor.
  * Submit requests to the Help Desk and include the access type and purpose: [support@swarthmore.edu](mailto:support@swarthmore.edu)

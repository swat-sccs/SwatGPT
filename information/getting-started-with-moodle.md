# Getting Started with Moodle

All courses and enrollments have been entered into Moodle, so instructors can log in and get started on their courses right away. 

  1. Go to [https://moodle.swarthmore.edu](https://moodle.swarthmore.edu/).
  2. Log in with your Swarthmore username and password.
  3. If you have courses available, they will be shown in the middle of the screen. Select a course to enter it.
  4. In a course, toggle the **Edit Mode** button (top right) to begin adding content.



See more Moodle resources by visiting our [Moodle Help pages](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Moodle "Moodle").

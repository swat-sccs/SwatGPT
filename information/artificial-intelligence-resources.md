# Artificial Intelligence Resources

Swarthmore provides Artificial intelligence (AI) tools to support teaching, learning, and research. Other tools not provided by Swarthmore may use your data to train their models, potentially creating a critical data security risk.

## Artificial Intelligence Tools

All listed tools are private and do not train on user data. 

### Text-Based

[Google Gemini](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Google_Gemini "Google Gemini"): Google’s AI chatbot and mobile app 

[Gemini Notebook](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/NotebookLM "NotebookLM") (formerly NotebookLM): Google’s service that uses your specific documents as a knowledge base to answer questions, summarize content, and generate ideas 

[LibreChat](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/LibreChat "LibreChat"): multi-model AI chatbot including access to ChatGPT and Claude and open source models. 

### Image, Video, and Music

[Firefly](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Firefly "Firefly"): generate images from a text description 

[Google Gemini](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Google_Gemini "Google Gemini"): generate images, videos, and music from a text description 

[Google Gemini Alt Text Gem](https://gemini.google.com/gem/1uPih6mM0MKIX-zG6HmwF2IJpBbAiRssT?usp=sharing): Generate alternative text and image descriptions for your visuals. 

### Zoom

[Zoom AI Companion](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Zoom_AI_Companion "Zoom AI Companion"): automatically generate meeting summaries and allow participants to questions about the meeting. 

### API Access

ITS provides API access to a number of different large language models. Get in touch with ITS to learn more about how to incorporate AI into your coding projects.

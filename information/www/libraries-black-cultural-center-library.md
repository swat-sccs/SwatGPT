# Black Cultural Center Library

The Libraries support the research of the community served by the Black Cultural Center, which holds it's own library collection and study space devoted to materials about the African American experience and the civil rights movement. Students use this library as both a reading room and collaborative space; there is ample table space and comfortable seating.

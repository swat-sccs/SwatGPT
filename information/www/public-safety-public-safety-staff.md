# Leadership Team

## Colin Quinn

Executive Director, Public Safety

Public Safety

### Contact

Colin has 12 years of experience in public safety and emergency management. Almost 10 years of that experience is within higher education, including at Drexel University. Prior to Swarthmore, Colin worked in the global emergency travel assistance field conducting medical and security evacuations. He is an experienced public safety communication center instructor, and has worked collaboratively with many colleges, universities, and agencies to develop programs that empower community members to feel safe on campus. Colin earned a B.A. from La Salle University and an M.S. from Drexel University. (Joined 2022)

## John Bera

Associate Director, Emergency Preparedness, Compliance, & Engagement

Public Safety

### Contact

John began his career with the District of Columbia Fire and EMS Department, and later served in the Haverford Township, Pa., Paramedic Department. He earned a B.A. in political science from Ursinus College and an M.S. in disaster medicine and emergency management from Philadelphia University. Prior to Swarthmore, he served as the director of campus safety and dean on duty at Ursinus, and served as a member of the student affairs senior leadership team. (He/Him/His) (Joined 2020)

## Candis Evans

Community Resource Manager

Public Safety

### Contact

Candis brings more than 14 years of public safety experience to Swarthmore. Prior to joining the College, she served as a public safety lieutenant at La Salle University, where she studied criminal justice and human services and earned an A.A. in liberal arts. Her devotion to serving the community underscores her enthusiasm for mentoring students. In addition to her work at the College, Candis is currently pursuing her B.A. and is developing a nonprofit organization to support a safe and healthy environments for youths. (She/Her/Hers) (Joined 2020)

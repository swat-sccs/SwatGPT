# Faculty Services

The libraries support the research and teaching of all faculty, regardless of tenure status or terms of employment. Each department and center has a dedicated subject specialist librarian, with expertise in the resources and methodologies of that field, who can support you with your research and collaborate on instruction.

The Libraries' Head of Scholarly Communications, Maria Aghazarian, supports faculty throughout the publishing process and Head of Digital Scholarship Strategies, Amanda Licastro, facilitates the critical use of digital networked technologies in research and teaching.

Email specialcollections@swarthmore.edu for more information about teaching with primary resources and Special Collections materials.

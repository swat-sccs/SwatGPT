# News & Events

#### Lang Center Newsletter

During the academic year, the Lang Center publishes a weekly newsletter to help keep campus and community members informed about news and announcements, upcoming events, and opportunities. We invite you to sign-up to receive our newsletter for weekly updates.

If you would like to submit a publicity request for the newsletter, please refer to our submission guidelines and email the Lang Center Newsletter team.

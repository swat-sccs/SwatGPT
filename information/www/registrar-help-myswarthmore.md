# Help with MySwarthmore

*my*Swarthmore access

Effective Wednesday, August 1, 2018, mySwarthmore will only be available to current Students, Faculty, Staff, and Emeriti.

Please take note of the information below:

Only constituents with active accounts will be permitted to log into mySwarthmore using their network credentials via our two-factor authentication provider (DUO).

If you do not have or do not remember your password, you may reset your password at: password.swarthmore.edu

If you do not remember your network username, please contact the helpdesk via phone x4357 (on campus) or 610-328-8513 (off campus) or via email: support@swarthmore.edu

Former Faculty (non-Emeriti), Staff and Graduated Student Employees: Should a copy of tax statements (i.e. W2, 1095C) or paystubs be needed after leaving the College, please send a request in writing to the Human Resources Office 


Alumni (including the class of 2018): you will not have access to mySwarthmore after you graduate. Please join the Alumni Online Community:

(https://www.swarthmore.edu/alumni/swarthmore-alumni-online-community) 


Alumni wishing to order transcripts, please visit our transcript request page:

(https://www.swarthmore.edu/registrar/transcript-request)

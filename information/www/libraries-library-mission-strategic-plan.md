# Library Mission & Strategic Plan

## **Mission**

Cultivating scholarship and intellectual curiosity in our community through access to expertise, collections, and spaces.

## Strategic Plan

Updated July 2026

The strategic plan for the libraries describes our goals for the evolution of library expertise, collections, and spaces from 2025-2027. The plan is deliberately ambitious and includes objectives that we hope to achieve in each area, along with specific actions which may change from year to year.

### Expertise

#### Advance the educational mission of the College

##### Objectives:

- Ensure that all students graduate with a strong knowledge of the research process and are literate in the use of information, digital and emerging technologies, and primary sources.
- Build library staff capacity for, and communication about, responsible use of emerging technologies across campus.
- Develop sustainable and accessible professional practices.
- Provide meaningful opportunities and engaging library experiences for students to prepare them for future employment and develop professional skills.

### **Collections**

#### Adapt collections and discovery practices to our evolving information landscape.

##### Objectives:

- Adapt collections and discovery practices to our evolving information landscape.
- Elevate the visibility and accessibility of our Special Collections and the College Art Collection, maximizing their impact both within the Swarthmore community and across the globe.
- Improve the discoverability, sustainability, usability, and cross-compatibility of our digital spaces and infrastructure ecosystem.

### **Spaces**

#### Maximize the potential of library spaces to strengthen teaching, learning, and research at the College.

##### Objectives:

- Maximize the potential of library spaces to strengthen teaching, learning, research, and well-being at the College.
- Develop existing library space to better meet the needs of current students and explore potential innovative uses.
- Engage faculty, students, staff, researchers, and alumni in sustained and lively conversation around library planning.

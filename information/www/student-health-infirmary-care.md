# Infirmary Care

Infirmary care is provided for students who may need additional health care by experienced nursing staff. There are a limited number of beds for infirmary care. This unique service is available to Swarthmore students that are admitted to the Student Health Service by a nurse or a physician for health care that does not require hospitalization or emergency room services.

# Dining Locations & Hours

- Faculty, staff, and guests may dine in the Dining Center.
- Take out for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Reusable container programs are available. Exact change, checks or credit cards are accepted. The prices are:
- Breakfast: $9.50
- Lunch: $13.50
- Dinner: $17.50
- Children 5 and under are half price.
- The Dining Center is a tray-less facility, which will help save an average of 22,000 gallons of water per year and reduce the amount of chemicals going into the waste stream. Once you have finished dining with us, please take your items to the dish drop. Simply put your silverware on the conveyor below and leave everything other than single-use packaging on your plate, which will travel to our composting area.
- The Dining Center has been certified by the Green Restaurant Association! Learn more about the process.

### Essie's Corner

**Essie’s Corner located in the Dining and Community Commons.**

Essie’s Corner offers a quick and seamless dining experience, with a fresh-to-order menu of healthy sandwiches and grain bowls and a marketplace of snacks and prepared items. Dining Dollars, Swat Points, Garnet Cash, and credit cards are accepted at Essie's Corner. **Dispatch Goods,** reusable take-out containers instead of single-use compostable containers will be available. You will see the bright orange reusable containers at Essie's that you can easily return to any of the bright orange bins located in the Science Center Café, Clothier Hall, Dining Center and at the Food Recovery Fridge in the lobby outside Science Center Rom 199 .

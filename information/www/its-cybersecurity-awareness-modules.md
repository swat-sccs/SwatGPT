# Cybersecurity Awareness Modules

A cybersecurity eLearning system from KnowBe4 is available to all Faculty and Staff here at Swarthmore College. There is a library of modules that range in topics from how to spot and avoid phishing attempts to protecting your mobile devices. Within some modules, there may be questions to help you, and a quiz at the end to test what you have learned. You can even pause videos whenever you need to and come right back to where you left off later. Completing a video course gets you a printable certificate, too!

To take advantage of these courses:

1. Visit swarthmore.edu/safeonline
2. Enter your full email address into the KnowBe4 login page and then login with your Swarthmore credentials on the following screen - You will need your second-factor device handy
3. Click on "Training" (if you've been assigned any modules) or on "Library" and browse for whatever courses look interesting to you


There are also some additional details at ITS Knowledge Base > KnowBe4.

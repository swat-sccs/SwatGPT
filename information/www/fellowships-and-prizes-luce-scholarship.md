# Luce Scholarship

### Description

Provides stipends and internships for fifteen to eighteen young Americans to live and work in Asia for a year.

### Selection Criteria

- U.S. citizenship
- Record of high achievement
- Outstanding leadership ability
- Clearly defined career interest with evidence of potential for professional accomplishment
- Must be under 32 years old.

### Internal Deadline

No longer requires internal nomination.

### How to Apply

Visit the Luce website below to access an online application and to review the selection process. National deadline is in October 2024.

# Academic Advice for Students

The following advice guides were developed collaboratively by Student Academic Mentors, the Teaching and Learning Commons and the Office of Academic Success staff:

For advice on approaching your natural sciences, engineering and math courses, visit the NSE (Natural Sciences and Engineering) Success Toolkit.

For advice on how to use Artificial Intelligence, check out the Library and ITS Guidance for Students on Artificial Intelligence (AI).

Want to develop more efficient computer skills? Try the Computer Skills Tutorials from the Social Science Quantitative Laboratory.

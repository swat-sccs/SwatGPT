# Interlibrary Loan Policies for Outside Libraries

- Shipping Information
- Average turnaround time is 2-5 working days (excluding shipping time.) We use UPS/IDS for member libraries. For non-members we ship most books via Library Mail, and all media (CDs, DVDs, LPs, VHS, Microforms) via First Class.
- Billing
- We prefer to bill via IFM; we will invoice.
- Loans to Libraries outside the US
- If possible, we will provide copies and loans to libraries outside the United States. Fees will depend on the borrowing library's status. In most cases, we charge at least the cost of postage. All items are shipped via United States Postal Service, air mail.
- Fees
  - We will lend to non E-Z Borrow libraries at no charge if you reciprocate!
  - Non-reciprocal academic and public libraries: $15 per loan or copy
  - Corporate libraries: $25 per loan or copy.
- Loan periods and packing requirements
- Items may be borrowed for 60 days and many are renewable for 30 days 
  - Videos and DVDs: must be returned in a box. Some CDs and LPs require replacement cost insurance and return via FedEx or UPS.
  - Microforms: We will lend up to six reels at a time per request. We generally do not provide copies from microforms, but will do so in special circumstances.
  - CDs and LPs: must be returned in a box. Some CDs and LPs require replacement cost insurance and return via FedEx or UPS.
  - Journals: We do not lend unbound journal issues. Bound volumes may be loaned during the summer months, for a maximum of two weeks.
  - Multiple copies: We will copy only one article/chapter per request. Multiple articles/chapters require multiple requests.
  - 
		Multiple volumes: We will lend up to five volumes per request for multi-volume loan requests.

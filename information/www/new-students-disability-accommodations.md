# Disability Accommodations

Swarthmore's Student Disability Services is pleased to work with students with disabilities to identify accommodations that will allow equal access to college programs and services. If you are a student with a documented disability and have not already done so, submit an accessibility request through the Disability Accommodations section in mySwarthmore (via Accommodate) and send your supporting clinical documentation by June 1. Some accommodations can take many weeks to arrange, and we want to be sure that your needs are met in time for classes.

To learn about the Student Disability Service and the accommodations process, please visit our Student Disability Service website.

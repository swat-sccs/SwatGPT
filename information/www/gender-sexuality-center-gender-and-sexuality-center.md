# Gender and Sexuality Center

Through programming, education, and encouraging activism, the GSC fosters a welcoming community where students can thrive, connect, and celebrate their authentic selves. Although all students are welcome in the GSC, the mission of the center is dedicated to cultivating an inclusive space for addressing issues relating to all marginalized genders and sexualities.

Our safe space ensures that all students can seek support and explore their experiences without judgment. Together, we strive to build a transformative campus environment that celebrates diversity, promotes understanding, and advocates for a more equitable and inclusive future for all students, including women, gender non-conforming, trans, and queer students.

The GSC is located in the previous Women's Resource Center on Sharples Lane, behind the Faulkner Tennis Courts.

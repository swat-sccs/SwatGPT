# Postgraduate Opportunities

### **External Fellowships**

Collaborate with the Office of Fellowships and Prizes to find and apply for world-class postgraduate opportunities that align with your goals. The impact of these fellowships extends far beyond financial support. By becoming a recipient, you join a lifelong community of dedicated scholars and thinkers, providing you with a network of peers and mentors that spans the globe. Learn more about the unique benefits of opportunities such as: Churchill, Fulbright, Gather, Marshall, Rhodes, Schwarzman and Watson. Learn more from the Office of Fellowships and Prizes

### 

**Swarthmore Fellowships**

Partial stipends available to support the initial year of advanced graduate work at global institutions abroad or in the U.S. These fellowships are open to any alum of Swarthmore College no matter their age or number of years since graduation. Learn more from the Office of Fellowships and Prizes

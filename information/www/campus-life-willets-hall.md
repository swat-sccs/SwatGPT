# Willets Hall

Willets is in the perpetual heart of campus. A short walk from McCabe Library, Public Safety, Worth Health Center, Parrish Hall, and about a five-minute walk to the Dining Center, Willets is close to many of the resources at Swarthmore. Housing mostly first-year students, with some sophomores and juniors, Willets is a lively place to be. Willets on its own houses almost a quarter of the entering class and can be an excellent place to meet fellow incoming students. Willets’ rooms are mostly doubles with a scattering of singles and a couple of two-room triples. Most rooms have large windows providing a lot of natural light. Boasting a large lounge on each floor, with kitchens and communal bathrooms, Willets provides a lot of space to build community. With Mephistos lounge just steps away on the 1st floor of Willets, residents will enjoy many programming opportunities and quite a few late-night events.

# Borrow Beyond Trico

- EZ-Borrow: Start here for books
- Interlibrary Loan: articles. chapters, and books (not available from EZ-Borrow).

Articles and book chapters will be delivered electronically.

Should Swarthmore add your request to our collections?

Email interlibraryloan@swarthmore.edu with any questions.

## FAQs:

- **EZ-Borrow** : Use your Swarthmore credentials (email address) to place requests.
- **Interlibrary Loan** : First-time users need to create an account using their Swarthmore credentials before placing a request.
- **Return** materials at any circulation desk or via outdoor drop boxes.

# Mission & Values

## TLC Core Values

The TLC aims to be responsive, inclusive, asset-oriented, collaborative, and holistic in all of its activities. As such, its programs:

- Are developed in response to student, faculty, and staff interests and needs;
- Include offerings for all members of our campus community (staff, students, faculty, and instructors);
- Develop collective capacity to meet the varied needs of all of our students;
- Build upon the myriad strengths of our community members;
- Model community-building and cooperation;
- Create opportunities for reciprocal teaching and learning; and
- Support the professional well-being of all our community members.

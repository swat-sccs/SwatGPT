# Mission Statement

Information Technology Services (ITS) works in partnership with the campus community to provide leadership and assistance in information technology matters to achieve their goals and objectives. We are a highly service-oriented organization that provides robust, reliable, adaptable, and innovative technological resources through which the College community is empowered to accomplish the College’s mission and strategic plan.

## Vision Statement

We will provide technological leadership by:

- Effective stewardship, management, development, and integration of academic and administrative technology services.
- Developing, facilitating, and promoting the use of technology in physical and virtual spaces to enhance teaching, learning, research, and creative production.
- Providing universal access, training, and support for the campus community to enable and sustain effective use of technology.
- Continually improving the performance, security, privacy, ease of use, accessibility, and reliability of technology services.
- Developing close partnerships with departments to enable effective collaboration.
- Exploring and advocating new opportunities using current and emerging technologies.
- Empowering our College community to embrace data governance, retention, and privacy standards to provide consistent and reliable data.
- Supporting the many ways of teaching, learning, and working, whether in-person, remote, or hybrid.
- Continually improving sustainability practices through the use of technology.

## Core Values

Our core values drive and guide us as we serve the College community. As members of ITS, we are committed to:

- **Accessibility** : We consciously keep accessibility in mind in an effort to create and promote an inclusive experience for faculty, staff, and students.
- **Collaboration** : We are dedicated to a constructive, team-oriented environment, gathering varied perspectives, sharing knowledge, and building effective partnerships with our community by fostering meaningful relationships.
- **Diversity, Equity, Inclusion** : We embrace all facets of DEI, including the intersectionality of race/ethnicity, gender, class, sexual orientation, gender identity and expression, ability, age, national origin, and religious/spiritual identities, as we strive to understand the different experiences of all people and the ways in which those differences move us to greater connection and communication.
- **Growth Mindset** :  We embrace continuous professional and personal development by our staff and the College as a whole.
- **Innovation** : We encourage creative and critical thinking, in the support of new approaches to problem-solving, risk-taking, and creative expression in the development of technology services and solutions.
- **People** : We value joy, positivity, thoughtfulness, empathy, and social responsibility through respect, active listening, and care for all, both professionally and personally.
- **Service** : We strive for excellence by providing secure, reliable, accessible, agile, and consistent service to all.
- **Transparency** : We foster open communications and thoughtful business processes to ensure our commitment, accountability, and equitable treatment to all.

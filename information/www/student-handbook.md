# Student Handbook

Together, we all share the responsibility of creating an inclusive community that values you and every member of the academic environment in which we live, learn, and work. This handbook explains our community expectations and the resources and services that are here to support you. Please contact your student dean or any Student Affairs staff member if you have questions or need advice about what follows.

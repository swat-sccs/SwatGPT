# Vending Machines

There are over 35 snack and beverage vending machines across campus offering a wide range of snacks and beverages. There is also a health vending machine, with a selection of health and wellness products, in the Dining and Community Commons (DCC).

All machines accept payment in cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and OneCard. OneCard payment is in the form of Swat Points, Student Garnet Cash, and Faculty/Staff Garnet Cash.

#### Health Vending Machine

The DCC has a health vending machine located behind the upper level welcome desk and accessible to our community members any time the DCC is open. The vending machine is stocked with a variety of useful products, including:

- Over-the-counter medicines like Advil, Tylenol, Pamprin, Tums, Pepto Bismol, Imodium, antibiotic ointment, antihistamines, and hydrocortisone cream.
- Cough and cold relief, cough drops, and Emergen-C.
- Daily use items like Band-aids, tissues, lip balm, eye drops, and contact lens solution.
- Emergency contraception, pregnancy tests, and digital thermometers.

#### **USING YOUR ONECARD**

To make a vending purchase with your OneCard, swipe the card on the reader, then wait for the reader say "Authorized." Once authorized, make your selection and enjoy your snack or beverage.

**REFUNDS**

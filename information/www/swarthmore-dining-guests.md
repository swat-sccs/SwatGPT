# Guests

### **Sharples Dining Hall** 

Invite your family and friends to dine with you. With our great variety and unlimited seconds, they won't be able to beat our prices. The rates in Sharples Dining Hall are:


- Breakfast - $5.75
- Lunch - $10.00
- Dinner - $12.50



### **Essie's Corner**

Essie's Corner is open to the general public, selling a wide variety of light fare for reasonable prices. Guests may pay in cash or be treated by current students using meals or points.


Please also consider our coffee bars, which are open to the general public.

# JobX and Student Employment

JobX is Swarthmore’s student employment portal and the College’s centralized hub for posting and applying to jobs on campus. Swarthmore is committed to its mission of inclusion, and to preparing students to be civic leaders by giving them the tools to develop their skills within and beyond the classroom. JobX supports this mission by bringing ease, transparency, and equity to student employment at the College.

**If you have questions about using JobX**, please contact Meg Wherrity, Director of Student Employment, mwherri1@swarthmore.edu. We also welcome your input as you use JobX! Please share feedback on your experience through our feedback form.

**Need help posting your jobs to JobX?** Faculty and staff may contact Meg Wherrity, Director of Student Employment, mwherri1@swarthmore.edu for information on upcoming training sessions.

**If you are a Faculty or Staff member** involved in the management and hiring of student employees within the College, explore these pages to learn how to use JobX to:

- Add, Edit, List, or Close a Position
- Manage Candidates (Review, Contact for an Interview, Reject)
- Hire an Employee
- Job Posting Timeline

# Copies & Permissions

## Copyright in Special Collections

When you use the collections, you agree to bear responsibility for determining whether the intended use of these materials is permitted under U.S. law or if it is necessary to secure additional permission to use them, particularly in publication.

The collections' finding aids have additional details about conditions governing use and legal status. You may wish to refer to this guide from the Society of American Archivists as you determine whether you may claim fair use or otherwise use this material without locating rights holders. If you have additional questions, please contact the specific collection that holds the materials:

- Friends Historical Library: friends@swarthmore.edu
- Swarthmore College Archives: archives@swarthmore.edu
- Swarthmore College Peace Collection: peacecollection@swarthmore.edu
- Rare Book Room: amccoll1@swarthmore.edu

Swarthmore College Special Collections make materials available to patrons without claiming to control the copyright to all materials in our collections. Material may be protected by copyright law (Title 17 U.S. Code).

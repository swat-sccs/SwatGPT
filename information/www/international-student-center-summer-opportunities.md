# Summer Opportunities

Are you wondering what to do this summer? Swarthmore College provides students with meaningful, learning opportunities for the summer. Check out this page for common guidelines.

Here is a list of opportunities open to students:

1. SWATWorks Micro-internships - independent contract opportunities
2. SFEP - Funding to offset costs associated with professional development with local Philadelphia-based startups
3. Lang Center - Social Impact Summer Scholarship, Pilot Project Grant, Lang Opportunity Scholarship Program (and many others) but NOT PSRF
4. Center for Innovation & Leadership - CIL Summer Fund & CIL Venture Fund
5. Rubin Mentoring Program - Supports a variety of summer endeavors
6. Career Services Summer Stipend- Students can apply for a stipend to support living expenses associated with pursuing an unpaid opportunity that they have been offered and intend to accept
7. Academic Division - Supports academic endeavors such as research, intensive language study, thesis/independent research. Check this list of examples.

# Health Vending Machine

Located on the Upper Level of the DCC

The addition of this vending machine to campus reflects feedback from students about needing a more convenient, accessible way to get health and wellness supplies in a space they use every day.

Located behind the upper level welcome desk and accessible to our community members any time the DCC is open, the vending machine will be stocked with a variety of useful products, including:

- Over-the-counter medicines like Advil, Tylenol, Pamprin, Tums, Pepto Bismol, Imodium, antibiotic ointment, antihistamines, and hydrocortisone cream.
- Cough and cold relief, cough drops, and Emergen-C.
- Daily use items like Band-aids, tissues, lip balm, eye drops, and contact lens solution.
- Emergency contraception, pregnancy tests, and digital thermometers.

This resource was established in partnership between Student Health and Wellness, Be Well, and the OneCard Office. If you have any questions, you can reach out to health@swarthmore.edu.

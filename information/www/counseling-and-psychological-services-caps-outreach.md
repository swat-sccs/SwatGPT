# CAPS Outreach

### Fall 2026 Outreach

CAPS aims to support faculty, students, and staff in developing and/or facilitating outreach (trainings, workshops, and other events). If you are interested in partnering with us for outreach, please let us know via our Outreach Request Form. You don't need to have every detail to submit a request -- we'll help with that! Should you have any other questions, please email us at CAPS Outreach.

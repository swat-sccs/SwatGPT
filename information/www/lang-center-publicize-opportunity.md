# Publicize an Opportunity

#### Share to Our Newsletter

The Lang Center publishes a weekly newsletter during the academic year to help keep campus and community members informed about news, announcements, events, and opportunities.

If you would like to share an opportunity or event with the Swarthmore College community through our newsletter, please refer to our submission guidelines and email the Lang Center Newsletter team.

#### Share to Our Instagram

If you would like to share an Instagram post featuring an exciting opportunity or event for our students, please send the post to us @thelangcenter through Instagram messages, or email the Lang Center Newsletter team.

# Nutritionist

Nutrition consults at the student health service are available by appointment for any student who may be interested or referred by a physician, nurse practitioner, nurse, or counselor.

Brittany Pizio, Registered Dietitian, specializes in eating disorders and weight management, as well as medical nutrition therapy for a variety of concerns including diabetes, GI issues, anemia, and issues frequently affecting young adults. Please email bpizio1@swarthmore.edu to schedule an appointment.


Questions, concerns, or needed support related to eating and/or body image can be discussed confidentially with any staff member in this group:

- Brittany Pizio, Registered Dietitian, Student Health and Wellness Service: bpizio1@swarthmore.edu
- Any medical staff at the health center
- Counseling & Psychological Services (CAPS) at Worth Health Center: caps@swarthmore.edu

# Emergencies

In case of emergency or when the health center is closed, call Public Safety by dialing "0" or (610) 328-8000.

The officer will make a decision to either arrange transportation to the Health Center or hospital.

Usually students will be taken to Riddle Hospital Emergency Room. However, students requiring urgent care that is not critical or which is not expected to end in a hospital admission, will be sent/taken to Patient First Urgent Care in Springfield, PA.

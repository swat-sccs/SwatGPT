# Health Care

The Student Health Service (SHS) offers acute and chronic health care services. Preventive health services are also offered with a focus on issues that impact college students. Physicians and nurse practitioners are available for assessment, diagnosis, treatment, and referrals as needed.

### For appointments call 610-328-8058

Health care providers are available Monday through Friday 9-5 for scheduled appointments and there is a Registered Nurse available at the health center until 7:45pm weekdays. A health care provider is available after hours to speak with students while school is in session by calling 610-328-8548. The SHS closes between 12-1p Monday-Friday but is available for emergencies.

Walk-in service is provided by a registered nurse on a first come first serve basis Monday - Friday 8 am - 7:45 pm. Services that require appointments include: lab work, STI screens, physician appointments, gynecologic services, vaccinations, referrals and travel consults.

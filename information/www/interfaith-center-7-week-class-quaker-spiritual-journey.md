# 7-Week Class on the Quaker Spiritual Journey

This fall, under the care of the Worship and Ministry Committee, Swarthmore Meeting is offering a 7-week Tuesday night class on the Quaker Spiritual Journey. The class will be taught by meeting member Marcelle Martin, using her book Our Life is Love as the text. We will examine and discuss ten elements of the Quaker spiritual journey as experienced by the first Quakers, by contemporary Friends, and by ourselves. The first class is Tuesday, September 13 in Whittier Room at 7:15 PM. Reading for the first session is available online at InnerLightBooks.com.

A donation to Swarthmore Meeting is requested: $50 (or whatever you can afford). For more information, email Marcelle at FriendMarcelle@aol.com.

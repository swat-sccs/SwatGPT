# Faculty-led Study Trips

The Provost’s Office hold an annual call for proposals from faculty that is designed to promote high-impact learning experiences for students through the development of courses that have a short-term domestic or international travel component. These trips typically take place over a break and connect to the content covered in courses. GEO is happy to provide advice and guidance on leading international travel abroad with groups.

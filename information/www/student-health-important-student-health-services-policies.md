# Important Student Health Services Policies

The Student Health and Wellness Service strives to provide comprehensive health care while respecting the individual rights and dignity of all students.

SHWS policies regarding Student Rights and Responsibilities reflect our commitment to care that is effective and considerate, within our capacity, goals, and philosophy of providing quality college health services.

FERPA rules and regulations govern campus health center treatment records. Health Records are kept securely for 7 years per state law.

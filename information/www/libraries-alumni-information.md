# Alumni Information

## Congratulations - you're an alum!

Whether you are nearby or far away, you'll always have special status at the libraries.

Swarthmore alumni have ongoing access to selected electronic resources such as JSTOR, SAGE journals and videos, Project Muse, and Mango Languages. Create an account in the Alumni Portal and register for access.

Many other online resources are available - no credentials needed!

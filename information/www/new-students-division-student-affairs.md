# Division of Student Affairs

The Division of Student Affairs is comprised of many offices that are here to support you or your student! Learn more about all the division can offer you on their website.

Swarthmore College has named a new Vice President of Student Affairs, Stephanie Ives. For more information about Stephanie, check out the official college announcement. She will be here in time to welcoming you at Orientation!

There will be more opportunities to meet members of this Student Affairs team through zoom sessions later this summer. In the meantime, do not hesitate to reach out with questions.

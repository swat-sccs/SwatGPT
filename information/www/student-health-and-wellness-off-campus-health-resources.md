# Off-Campus Health Resources

#### Resource Information of Local Health and Dental Providers off-campus who are not affiliated with Swarthmore College.

*Please be aware of your individual insurance coverage policies and how they would pertain to service "out of network plan" or "out of state". Student health is not considered a primary care office and will not be able to provide a referral for HMO insurance purposes.* 

*If you are seeking care from a specialist not listed here, please call the health center during normal business hours at 610-328-8058. Alternatively you can email* *health@swarthmore.edu* *to request a specialist referral list.*

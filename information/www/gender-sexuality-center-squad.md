# SQUAD

Swarthmore QUeer Advocate Development

### **Join the SQUAD**

The Intercultural Center team offers a collection of LGBTQ+ training and educational opportunities for faculty, staff, and students centered around LGBTQ+ students on campus. In Spring 2018, these trainings began with Pronoun 101, which was offered at a variety of levels including open community discussions, peer-led student trainings, and faculty and staff department trainings.

SQUAD delivers skills-based, educational opportunities for the Swarthmore community, while centering the philosophy that advocacy is a continuous process that looks different for everyone.

Departments or other groups may request a training by emailing Paige Jennings at pjennin1@swarthmore.edu

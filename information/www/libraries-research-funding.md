# Research Funding

- Award
- A stipend in the amount of $1,500 per week, for a minimum of one week and a maximum of four weeks, will be distributed to 2-6 researchers. Fellows are expected to travel to Swarthmore to conduct their research on-campus between July 1 and the following June 30. It is our expectation that each research trip will be for a one-week minimum increment (for example, a researcher granted $3,000 for two weeks may choose to schedule two separate one-week trips). If alternative arrangements are preferred, they must be requested.
- Eligibility
- Academics, creatives, and researchers at all levels are welcome to apply! The fellowship is open to faculty, graduate students, or scholars at all levels from outside the Swarthmore College community, as well as Swarthmore College faculty and staff. Preference is given to projects that make significant use of materials available only on-site at Swarthmore College. Fellows will be required to give a brief, informal presentation about their research to library staff on the last day of their research trip.
- How to Apply
- Applications are due on March 31 of the award year. Applicants must: 
  - Complete the application form
  - Submit the following four components combined into a **single PDF.**  The filename must**include your name.**  Email the file to specialcollections@swarthmore.edu.
 
  1. A description of your project and its importance to the field, 2-5 pages long (double-spaced). The description should address how you will use the resources at Friends Historical Library (FHL), the Swarthmore College Peace Collection (SCPC), and/or Swarthmore College Archives (SCA) to address your research question. Applicants may wish to consult with collection Curators about available resources before submitting their application. The project description should state the anticipated publication or dissemination of research results.
  2. A work plan that states the number of weeks that the applicant wishes to spend on site, and includes a list of the specific resources (with collection/call numbers and box numbers if applicable), that the applicant wishes to consult. Please note that staff are typically unable to process requests larger than 30 boxes per week. Funding decisions prioritize projects using materials that are only available on-site; requests for resources that are available on microfilm, in digital format, or published edition should include a rationale.
  3. A current vita or resume.
  4. A short writing sample (an article or book chapter).
 Notifications of award winners will be distributed in May of the award year.

# Research Funding

The Margaret W. Moore and John M. Moore Research Fellowship provides a stipend to promote research that uses the resources of the Friends Historical Library, the Swarthmore College Peace Collection, or Swarthmore College Archives.

Academics, creatives, and researchers at all levels are welcome to apply! (Although current Swarthmore College *students* may learn more about their opportunity here.)

Questions about the application process may be directed to specialcollections@swarthmore.edu.

# Student Opportunities

The Libraries provide holistic student support. Explore this page to learn about job openings and career paths, opportunities for your club or student group, and our prestigious book collecting prize.

The Edward awards cash prizes to three Swarthmore students who submit outstanding entries about their book collections. Winners are also invited to install their collections in a McCabe Library exhibition.

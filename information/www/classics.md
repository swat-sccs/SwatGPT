# Classics

## What is Classics?

The field of Classics has traditionally been defined as the study of the cultures of the ancient Greeks and Romans. At Swarthmore our curriculum includes training in Greek and Latin. In addition, the department offers a range of courses on the history, literature, philosophy, religion, and cultural life of antiquity, including classes that explore the reception of the ancient world in later periods up to the present day. The rigorous training in Greek and Latin that is the hallmark of Swarthmore's Classics program has meant that the department enjoys remarkable success in producing students who go on to become leaders in the field. But because it is a truly interdisciplinary field, Classics also appeals to students with a wide variety of interests and career goals.

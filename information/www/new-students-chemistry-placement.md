# Chemistry Placement

Any students interested in taking the honors version of CHEM 010 (CHEM 010 HN) in Fall 2022 should participate in our online placement process in July and August. If you are not interested in the honors section, there is no additional work that you need to do. In July, you will receive instructions on the online placement process.

Accommodations Information: If you anticipate that you might need testing accommodations due to a disability, please contact Student Disability Services (studentdisabilityservices@swarthmore.edu) as soon as possible. We are happy to work with you.

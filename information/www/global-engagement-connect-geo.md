# Connect with GEO

The Global Engagement Committee is a campus-wide committee of faculty and administrators that serves as advisors to the Global Engagement Office on matters of policy and practice.

The International Travel Risk Management Committee (ITRMC) develops policies and procedures for Swarthmore Global Travel and disseminates appropriate information and resources.

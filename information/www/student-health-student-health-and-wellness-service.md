# Student Health and Wellness Services

The Student Health and Wellness Center is open Monday through Thursday 9:00 a.m. - 4:45 p.m. and Friday 9:00 a.m. - 4:00 p.m. during the fall and spring semesters.

Appointments are available by calling 610-328-8058. No walk in appointments.

There is a registered nurse on call (not affiliated with Swarthmore College) who is available to students during periods that the health center is closed. The nurse on call can be reached at 610-328-8548.

The health center closes daily between 12:00 p.m. and 1:00 p.m. for professional development and lunch meetings. Health care staff are in the building and available for urgent health issues and emergencies.

**For life threatening emergencies always call Public Safety at 610-328-8333 and 911.** 

The Student Health and Wellness Service (SHWS) offers acute and chronic health care services. Preventive health services are also offered with a focus on issues that impact college students. Providers are available for assessment, diagnosis, treatment, and referrals as needed.

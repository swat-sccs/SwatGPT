# SPAs (Speaking Associates)

Speaking Associates are Writing Associates (WAs) who are trained to help students with their presentations. SPAs work with students on the structure of their ideas and arguments, their oral delivery, and their visual aids in a variety of capacities, including preparing final presentations, leading seminar discussions, presenting research in progress, or pitching a grant proposal. SPAs have worked with students to present their research at Senior Showcase, SWAT Tank, and class presentations.

Speaking Associates are available for conferences in the Writing Center or by special request. To make an appointment via the Writing Center, go to swarthmore.mywconline.com, or go to “MySwat” > “Student Information” tab > “Schedule an Appointment with the Writing Program”.

Once on the scheduling platform “WCOnline”- click the link for Speaking Associates (SPAs). Click on an open pair of white boxes (each pair is an appointment slot) and complete the appointment form. A confirmation email will be sent once the appointment form is submitted.

To get more information on the Program, please contact the Writing Program Director, Professor Alba Newmann Holmes at aholmes2@swarthmore.edu.

# Voices Matter

Intercultural Center's Speaker Program

In order to amplify the voices and perspectives of underrepresented communities, it is imperative that the Intercultural Center bring in outside speakers who will bring their lived experiences, expertise, and subject matter expertise to our community. Voices Matter is a signature program under the leadership of the Intercultural Center that aims to provide the college and surrounding community with world-class speakers and lectures that specialize in enhancing global stewardship, humanity, and cultural competency. They will be formatted in a keynote lecture style or a moderated Q&A program.

### Program Objectives

- Educate the Swarthmore College Community to various perspectives of underrepresented communities
- Foster an experience that will enhance individual cultural competency about a particular topic
- Participants expressed positive communal interactions while attending one of the Voices Matter Programs

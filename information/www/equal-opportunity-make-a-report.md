# Make a Report

## Reporting Allegations of Violations

Swarthmore College, as an educational community, will promptly and equitably respond to all reports of alleged discrimination and harassment based on a protected class.

**You may report allegations of violations to this policy in any of the following ways:**


## On Campus:

Report to Swarthmore College using the Assess, Respond, Connect (ARC) Reporting Form

## Off Campus:

External Hotline (Formerly known as the "Whistleblower Hotline) (866) 292-7713

This toll-free line is offers a live reporting option 24/7. It is managed by an outside company that will listen to your concern and direct it to the appropriate college official for prompt review. You may remain anonymous or you may choose to identify yourself*. Regardless of your decision, your information will be documented and addressed appropriately.

*If you chose to remain anonymous, this option does not fulfill any mandated reporting obligations you may hold.

## More Information

More information about the College’s resolution process of complaints implicating this notice can be found in the Swarthmore College Policy Prohibiting Discrimination, Harassment, and Retaliation and Procedures for Resolving Allegations of Discrimination, Harassment and Retaliation. To make inquiries or file a complaint, please contact the Swarthmore College Equal Opportunity Officer at eoo@swarthmore.edu.

# Dining Center Stations and Maps

The new Dining Center is home to nine food stations serving an array of fresh and local dining choices. Ranging from “The Classics” to “Verdant and Vegan,” there is something for everyone.

### Lower Level

**Daily Kneads:** Freshly made cookies, cakes, pies, and many more delicious sweets are served here daily. A self-serve station that includes an assortment of ice creams brought to you by Hershey’s®.

**World of Flavor**: Let’s take your taste buds on a world tour at this station. Here you will explore cuisines from all over the world such as American, Asian, Italian, Greek, Indian, and Caribbean. Also, if you have a famous family recipe that you want to share with our staff, feel free to meet with the chef and we will explore it at this station.

**Field of Greens**: A build-your-own salad bar with a multitude of fresh toppings, accompanied by a selection of seasonal vegetables and your favorite salad dressings. You will also find specialty salads here.

**The Classics**: Your traditional homestyle meals are served at this station. Usually referred to as the “meat and potatoes” of the meal, here you will find dishes such as meatloaf, baked chicken, mashed potatoes, macaroni and cheese, and many more classic entrées.

**Free Zone**: Do you have a food allergy? No problem! Free of all of the top nine allergens, including sesame, the Free Zone assures you have a safe and tasty meal to enjoy. Open for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and late at night to make your dining experience as worry-free as possible.

**Fired Up**: The centerpiece of The Dining Center, the hearth will showcase your daily pizza, soup, and deli options. A selection of meat lovers, vegetarian, and vegan pizza options are available daily. Deli options will include an assortment of lunch meats and cheeses and condiments. All of our soups are made from scratch.

### Upper Level

**Verdant & Vegan**: If you are a vegetable-lover, this is the station for you. You will find a selection of fresh vegetables as well as local vegan protein options. This is where you will find fresh and flavorful vegan and vegetarian options.

**Spice of Life**: Here you will find a flavorful selection of Mediterranean and Middle Eastern cuisines.

**Grillin' Out**: This station is where you will get your daily dose of grilled burgers, fried and grilled chicken sandwiches, golden fries, grilled cheese sandwiches, hot dogs, and specialty sandwiches. You can also get eggs made to order.

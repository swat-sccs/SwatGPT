# Teaching Consultations & Facilitated Mid-Course Evaluations

The TLC currently offers two individualized services for faculty and staff seeking feedback on their teaching.  **Confidential consultations** can be scheduled to discuss anything from syllabi and assignment design to in-class activities and class dynamics. Reach out via the booking link below to schedule.  **Facilitated Mid-Course Evaluations** can also be scheduled for mid-semester. These dynamic, in-person class visits offer an alternative mode of soliciting formative feedback from students on in-progress courses. Contact Caroline Cheung at ccheung1 to learn more.

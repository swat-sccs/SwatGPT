# Resources for Faculty & Instructors

The TLC aims to connect faculty and instructional staff to resources that support teaching excellence and professional well-being. As the Center grows, expect to see programming that encourages collaborative reflection around teaching and learning, including individual consultations, drop-in lunches, learning communities, intensive pedagogy seminars, and more.

# Get Connected to CAPS

**In a mental health emergency, __do not complete this form.__ Call:**

- CAPS On-Call at 610-328-7768 or Public Safety at 610-328-8333
- The National Suicide Prevention Hotline: 800-273-8255
- 988 - National Suicide and Crisis Lifeline
- 911

If you have never been to CAPS or it has been **longer than 3 months** since your last appointment, please select NEW CLIENT and then "Access Appointment." If you have seen a CAPS provider for counseling within the last 3 months, you may choose Returning Client. 

Early in fall and spring semesters, please keep checking back on the web booking portal below if you do not see any available access appointments. More access appointment slots are added every day. You may have more success earlier in the day before appointments are only visible two weeks out.

**Please note: You must be on the campus internet** **or using VPN** **in order to access the New Clients scheduling page.**

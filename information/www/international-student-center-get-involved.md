# Get Involved

As part of the celebration, departments, offices, and individual community members are invited to highlight the impact of international students and engage with international alumni as part of the celebration.

Here are some ideas for how you might want to get involved:

- Highlighting international students and alumni in your newsletter, outreach and events
- Asking your international alumni to share their story to be featured in The 1875 online magazine or send us ideas for alumni to feature
- Incorporating the milestone theme into your existing events or collaborating with the ISC
- Hosting a celebration for current international students in your area
- Using the hashtag #intl150swat to highlight international students and alumni on social media

We welcome collaboration! If you have an idea for an event, please contact internationalcenter@swarthmore.edu.

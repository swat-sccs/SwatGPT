# Services

The College Post Office is here to help you send your mail and packages. If you are paying out of pocket, you may do so by cash or credit card. For mailings commissioned by departments, a departmental charge number must be provided.

Pricing for mail and packages varies according to the weight, dimensions, choice of carrier, and level of service selected. Please visit us for more information.

### Amazon Locker

There is an Amazon Locker in Parrish Hall, at the rear of the building alongside the Post Office loading dock. College students, faculty, and staff members may ship most Amazon orders to the locker, as well as place return packages there for daily pickup. There are some restrictions on the size and weight of packages that can fit in the locker; learn more about using the Amazon Locker to receive and return your Amazon purchases here. Please visit us for additional details.

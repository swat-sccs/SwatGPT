# Student Advisory Committee

Students on the advisory board inform the TLC of current and emerging educational and campus needs of students and give the TLC feedback on how well it is meeting those needs through its programs, support systems, and other offerings. The board meets on a monthly basis to help guide, develop, and review student-facing programs through the TLC.

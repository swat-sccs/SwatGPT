# Student Mailing Information

All mail is delivered to a central mail room on campus. Students are not assigned individual mailboxes, but are alerted via email when a piece of mail or a package is available for pick up.

Correspondence to your student should include their class year as follows:

Student Name ’28

Swarthmore College

500 College Ave.

Swarthmore, PA 19081

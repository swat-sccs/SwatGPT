# About Us

The Student Disability Services staff includes Assistant Director Traci Huppman, Administrative Assistant Christine Jackson and Director Andrea Vassar.

Student Disability Services is located on the first floor in the West wing of Parrish Hall: Parrish West 113, 119 and 123.

Student Disability Services is part of the Office of Academic Success. Director Vassar's supervisor is Associate Dean of Academic Success Liz Derickson. Dean Derickson's supervisor is Dean of Academic Success Eric Jensen.

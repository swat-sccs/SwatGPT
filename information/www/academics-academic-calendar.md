# Academic Calendar

2026 Fall Semester

| Date | Event | 
|---|---|
| August 25 | Residence halls open for new students | 
| August 21–24 | International Student Orientation | 
| August 25–30 | New Student Orientation. | 
| August 27 | Advising begins. All-advisor meeting in morning; individual advising begins in afternoon. | 
| August 28 | Residence halls open for returning students. Computer preregistration for first-year and transfer students only. | 
| August 30 | Meal plan starts at dinner. | 
| August 31 | Classes begin. | 
| September 7 | Labor Day. No classes. | 
| September 14 | Drop/add ends. Last day to delete a course from or add to permanent registration. Last day to uncover a shadow grade for a CR course from the previous semester. | 
| October 1 | Final examination schedule available online. | 
| October 5 | All outstanding incompletes from Spring semester must be complete and graded. | 
| October 9 | Fall Break begins after last class. | 
| October 19 | Fall classes resume at 8:30 a.m. | 
| October 28 | Schedule of courses and seminars for next semester available online. | 
| November 2- 13 | Advising period. | 
| November 6 | Last day to declare CR/NC grading option. Last day to withdraw from a course and receive the grade notation "W." | 
| November 17-19 | Pre-enrollment for spring semester. | 
| November 19 | Pre-enrollment ends at 4 p.m. | 
| November 25 | Thanksgiving Break begins after last class. | 
| November 30 | Fall classes resume at 8:30 a.m. | 
| December 1 | Note: All accounts must show a zero or positive balance to enroll or select a room for spring semester. | 
| December 7-9 | December 7 follows a Monday schedule to make up for Labor Day Monday. December 8 follows a Thursday schedule to make up for Thanksgiving Thursday. December 9 follows a Friday schedule to make up for Thanksgiving Friday. | 
| December 9 | Classes end | 
| December 13-19 | Finals exam period | 
| December 18 | 100-level seminars end. The fourteenth meetings of Fall 100-level seminars may meet in either the seminar format or for a final exam, up to the discretion of the professor. | 
| December 19 | Finals end at noon. Residence halls close at 6 p.m. Meal plan ends at lunch. | 
| Januray 8, 2027 | Final grades for Fall due (noon). |

# New Students

Fall 2026

Dear Incoming Student,

Welcome to Swarthmore College. I am delighted to have you as a member of the Swarthmore community.

Our mission is to uplift and guide the whole student along their health journey with compassion, respect and connection.

The health and wellness team supports the needs of our diverse student body by providing individualized holistic care and campus-wide education. The Health & Wellness Center offers myriad wellness promotion, counseling, acute care, and prevention services.

Students may schedule appointments at the Student Health and Wellness Center and can be seen by a registered nurse, nurse practitioner, physician, dietitian, alcohol and other drug counselor, and health and wellness educator. Our physician is a member of the Main Line Health System, a full-service teaching hospital. The Student Health & Wellness Center provides acute care, alcohol and other drug counseling, health and wellness promotion, nutrition counseling, allergy injections, first aid treatment, reproductive health services, simple diagnostic screenings, travel health consultations, vaccinations, and wellness visits. Our operational hours are based on when the need is highest, and are supplemented by an after-hours on call system that provides students with access to a registered nurse.

All visits to the Health & Wellness Center are free of cost. A fee is applied for simple diagnostic tests and most medications dispensed at the Health & Wellness Center. Laboratory specimens are sent to LabCorp and are billed by the lab to the student’s health insurance.

The Swarthmore College Student Health Portal is available for managing your on-campus health needs and required forms. You can access your Student Health Portal through your New Student Checklist. In your New Student Checklist select Student Health Portal, choose Swarthmore College from the drop-down menu, then sign in with your Swarthmore username and password.

All students are enrolled by default in the Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP).  To waive out of the SHIP, follow these steps **by June 15th**: 

1. Log into New Student Checklist
2. Click Health Insurance Waiver

**As a new student, you are required to submit the following health information prior to June 15th.** (The following forms are also found on your Student Health Portal.)   For items 2, 3, and 4, print the form to be completed by your primary care clinician. You are required to then enter your immunizations dates and upload your completed forms to your student health portal for review. 

1. Health Certificate, FERPA form, and treatment consent form are completed by students online in the Student Health Portal
2. Immunization Record
3. Tuberculosis (TB) Screening Questionnaire
4. Physical Form - Must be completed within the past year

- __Hepatitis B vaccine__ .  Three doses or two doses of adult vaccine, or a positive Hepatitis B surface antibody is required.
- __Measles, Mumps, Rubella__ (MMR).  Two doses are required at least 28 days apart after 12 months of age.
- __Meningococcal__ vaccine (MenACYW).  One dose is required at age 16 or older.
- __Polio__ .  Three doses of oral or four doses of injectable vaccine are required.
- __Tetanus-Diptheria-Pertussis__ .  Four doses and tetanus booster within the last ten years is required.
- __Varicella__ vaccine.  Two doses or healthcare provider documentation of proof of Chicken Pox disease (date) is required.
- **Unvaccinated students must complete a** waiver form. (located in your student health portal under forms)

Health insurance is required for all Swarthmore students. Please read the Important Annual Health Insurance Requirement information carefully. If you are insured by another plan, please be certain that your private health insurance plan will adequately cover a student away from home. Services away from home, such as blood tests, MRI’s, x-rays, and care from specialists are often not covered under a private insurance plan.

**The full amount of the student health insurance plan premium will be charged to your tuition bill if you do not complete the online waiver process**.  Failure to comply with this deadline will result in automatic enrollment into the Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP).  This fee will be added to the first tuition bill. I invite you to stop by and visit your health and wellness team. Please don’t hesitate to contact us at health@swarthmore.edu regarding any questions you may have about this information or if the Health & Wellness Center can be of assistance in managing a health concern.

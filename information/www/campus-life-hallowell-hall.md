# Hallowell Hall

Hallowell is a modern residence hall set just on the edge of the Crum woods connected to Danawell and Dana, a nearly identical hall. Together, the "Danawell" complex share a joint lounge in Danawell referred to as the Danawell Connector that hosts activities from TV-watching to group meetings to registered parties. The connector lounge also features an updated kitchen and attached patio with a grill for barbecues. The space is reservable by students to host these various events. Doubles, home to mostly first-years and some sophomores, are spacious and offer lots of storage. Singles are home to mostly juniors and some seniors. Some rooms have dressers that are built into the closets. Walls are white cement block. The large bay windows have a storage under the seat and are ideal for studying or lounging.

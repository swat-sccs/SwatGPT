# Services

Our services are intended to be both preventive and therapeutic. We have extensive experience counseling students with a broad range of concerns, including:

### CAPS On Call

As always, CAPS On-Call is also available for consultation 24 hours a day, every day of the year at 610-328-7768. There are multiple types of services available for urgent mental health needs. View available emergency resources.

### Other Services

#### TIMELYCARE

Swarthmore College is partnering with TimelyCare. Visit the TimelyCare page to learn more. TimelyCare is available to all Swarthmore students.

#### PSYCHIATRY

Medication management is available to students engaged in ongoing individual or group psychotherapy. Consultation is provided by licensed psychiatrists. A counselor must meet with a student before their first psychiatry appointment.

#### REFERRALS TO OTHER PROVIDERS

Students who require additional resources can read our off-campus counseling referral process or work with a counselor to locate the services outside the College.

We also recommend that you contact your insurance company for additional information about your benefits and coverage.

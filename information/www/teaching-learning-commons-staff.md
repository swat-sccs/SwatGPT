# Staff

Recognizing the integral role that staff play in making Swarthmore a wonderful place to live, work, and study, the TLC aims to connect staff with ongoing learning opportunities. The following directories include long-time programs, like Learning for Life, and funding sources to support continuing education, campus resources open to all members of the community, and new offerings from the TLC.

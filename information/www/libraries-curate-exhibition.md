# Curate an Exhibition

Do you have an idea for a library event or exhibit? We invite students to propose and curate Pop-Up Exhibitions in particular. Pop-Ups are intended to open up conversation about relevant social issues or to serve as promotion for campus programming.

Interested? Email Visual Initiatives and Exhibition Librarian Susan Dreher: sdreher1@swarthmore.edu

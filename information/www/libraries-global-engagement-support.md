# Global Engagement Support

- Research help
- Llibrarians can help you find sources and build research skills. Connect with us through chat, Zoom, or email.
- Language learning
- Mango Languages offers target-language tutorials for real-life situations and conversations.
- Access to digital materials
- You can access digital books, articles, streaming video, JSTOR, and other library resources through Tripod. Sign in to the proxy server for Off Campus Access or use the VPN. You may request journal articles and book chapters via Interlibrary Loan.

We hope you’ll take some time to explore the libraries where you’re studying! Visiting the local libraries at your host institution can be a valuable part of your off-campus experience. Feel free to ask Swarthmore’s librarians for help finding your way.

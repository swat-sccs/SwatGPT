# Need Help Now?

### Urgent Clinical Care

If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, urgent care is available 24 hours a day.

- 
	**When classes are in session, you can attend a crisis hour at CAPS at 11 am and 3 pm Monday through Friday. Call ahead if possible 610-328-8059**
- 
	**Call CAPS On Call anytime to speak to a licensed counselor:  610-328-7768**
- 
	**Contact Public Safety: 610-328-8333**
- **Call the National Suicide Prevention Hotline: 800-273-8255**
- **Call the National Mental Health Crisis and Suicide Hotline: 988**

### Swarthmore Public Safety

- **Emergency:** 610-328-8333
- **Non emergency:** 610-328-8281

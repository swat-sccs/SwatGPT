# GPA Calculator

Related Link: Swarthmore GPA Policy

Only accepts ABCD+/- grades. (Don't enter CR or NC, etc.)

An additional course prompt will be provided after entering a valid **# of Units** and a course **Letter Grade**.

Click the **X** to remove a course from the calculation.

This is a simple, browser-side calculator; nothing you enter or calculate is saved.

Questions and comments to registrar@swarthmore.edu

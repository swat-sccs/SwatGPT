# Wednesday Worship

An Interfaith Service

The Interfaith Center will offer a mid-week interfaith service every Wednesday during the Spring semester from 3:15-3:45pm. It will be an inclusive and ecumenical service open to people of all faiths (or no defined faith). Religious advisors will rotate leading the service from a basis of their own faith traditions and offer an open time for reflections. Join us to renew your mind, revive your spirit, and replenish your soul!

At this time, the Wednesday Worship is entirely virtual. Please contact the Interfaith Center for the Zoom link.

# For Community Partners

We regard community members’ insights and experiences as integral components of our co-created knowledge, and recognize that social and political solutions must consider the perspectives of those most directly affected. We are grateful to our partners in this important work—both on and off campus—who steadfastly collaborate in developing, implementing, and assessing these promising initiatives. Collectively, these partnerships, programs, and initiatives exemplify how the Lang Center has facilitated Swarthmore College’s commitment to intellectual rigor, ethical engagement, and social responsibility by connecting the curriculum, campus, and communities creatively.

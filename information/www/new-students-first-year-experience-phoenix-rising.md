# First-Year Experience: Phoenix Rising

- Pre-Orientation
- Before you arrive on campus, you will **accept your admissions offer** , complete all tasks on your**New Student Dashboard** , check your**academic tasks on Moodle** , and then learn about our exciting pre-orientation experiences. Options include our FLI-POP, International Student Orientation, and Summer Social Justice Institute programs.
- Orientation
- Your official welcome! During this time, you will follow the **Orientation schedule** on the Swarthmore app. It is designed to help you navigate the physical space of campus for move-in, get to know your orientation leader and cohort, learn more about our community values and processes, and*have fun!*
- Preparing for Classes
- As Orientation progresses, academic responsibilities begin. You will meet with your **academic advisor** and have your Moodle tasks complete. The academic fair is a bustling opportunity to take in all that Swarthmore has to offer intellectually.
- Building Community
- **What does it mean to be a Swattie?** This is an essential question to guide your time here. Engage with our community values and open dialogue, and participate in reflection with the Queries as you build your place on our campus.

Your roadmap to the Swarthmore Experience

A true Swarthmore Experience is one that educates the whole student, and to begin this journey, we support your whole transition throughout the first year at Swarthmore. Here, you will find everything in one place for living and learning in our community.

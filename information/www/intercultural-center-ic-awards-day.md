# IC Awards Day

Please join the Intercultural Center for an afternoon of celebrating and recognizing community members who are committed to promoting culture, inclusivity, and global stewardship. These awards are given to individual faculty, staff, students, as well as university offices, departments, centers, and divisions. Award recipients are recognized for their deep investment to inclusive excellence and furthering Swarthmore College’s commitment to an inclusive and welcoming community for all.

The ceremony will recognize 7 distinct categories, which are the following:

- Emerging Leaders Award
- Legacy Leaver for Community Leadership, Commitment, and Contribution Award
- Artistic Activism Award
- Social Justice Activism Award
- Intercultural Center Intern Award
- Staff and Faculty Award
- The Napoli-Kochavi Disability Advocacy Award

Please Nominate Someone from Our Community by filling out the 2027 IC Awards Nomination Form.

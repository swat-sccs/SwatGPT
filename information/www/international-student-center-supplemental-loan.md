# Supplemental Loan

### Application Guidelines

International Students who are on financial aid must apply for the supplemental loan through the ISC Portal. Requests can be for up to $1500.

Along with with the online application, students are also required to review and fill out a promissory note. The promissory note must also be submitted to the International Students Office for processing via the application in the ISC Portal.

Please keep a copy of the promissory note for your records.

# FLI-POP Pre-Orientation

This program, offered from August 20 to August 25, is designed to offer incoming first-generation low income (FLI) and undocumented students a unique opportunity to engage in various discussions and workshops focused on academics, social life, career readiness, and the FLI & undocumented experience at Swarthmore. The FLI Peer-Orientation Program (FLI-POP) is hosted by the Teaching and Learning Commons (TLC) and was conceptualized and facilitated by current FLI and undocumented Swarthmore students. The online application will be live and shared with new students in May.

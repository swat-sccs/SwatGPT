# Visit Us & Get Involved

**Our office is located on the first floor of McCabe Library (to the right when you first walk in)**

As Swarthmore’s center for teaching and learning, the TLC is a central hub that helps students, faculty, and staff to find resources to support their academic success, enhance their teaching, and to develop new skills and expertise. This happens most effectively with collaboration and feedback from you. Get involved with the TLC by submitting an idea, scheduling a teaching consultation, contributing to our programs, attending our events, or reading our newsletter. We also invite faculty and staff to drop-in to our offices and enjoy the lounge during McCabe library open hours.

Thanks for taking the time to share your perspectives. We hope to see you at future TLC events!

# The Lodges

The lodges are individual cottage rowhomes located between Worth residence hall and Bond Memorial Hall. Each Lodge is comprised of a 1st floor triple and a 2nd floor double. The residents of each Lodge share a bathroom on the 2nd floor. The first floor of the Lodges offers scenic views of the Worth courtyard. With only 5 occupants per building, Lodges are available as a block where students have the option to live with their friends in beautiful cottages.

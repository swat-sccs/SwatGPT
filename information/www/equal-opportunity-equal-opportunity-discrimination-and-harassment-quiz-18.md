# THE EQUAL OPPORTUNITY DISCRIMINATION AND HARASSMENT QUIZ

The following ten questions will test your knowledge of various issues in equal opportunity, discrimination and harassment. Select the best answer for each question and you will be given the correct response as well as a brief explanation.

### QUESTION #1

Leading a respectful work and academic environment means that each member of the Swarthmore College Community is responsible for ensuring that our community is free from discrimination and harassment.

# How to give feedback to the Writing Associates Program

We are committed to centering the needs of writers in Program practices and the Writing Center itself. We want to ensure that all writers and Writing Associates Fellows have the support and security necessary to engage in helpful conferences.

Please use our anonymous feedback form to provide anonymous feedback to the Writing Associate Program. Any feedback given through this form will be acknowledged and addressed to the best of our abilities. Your responses will only be shared with Program staff. If you have any questions or concerns you would prefer to share directly with the Program Director, please contact Alba Newmann Holmes (aholmes2@swarthmore.edu).

Within Course WA pairings and Writing Center appointments, the Program provides a specific feedback form pertaining to the individual Course Fellow for writers. For writers working with Course WAs, this feedback form is distributed at the end of the semester. For writers using the Writing Center, this feedback form is distributed at the end of each appointment.

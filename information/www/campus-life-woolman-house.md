# Woolman House

Nestled in the residential community right behind campus, Woolman offers the feel of off-campus living with the convenience of on-campus amenities and support. Just a short five-minute walk from the Science Center, Woolman is an upper-class only residential community that houses 22 residents. Giving the feel of a home away from campus, sophomores, juniors, and seniors can opt to block rooms together with their friends. Each floor in Woolman has several rooms and shared bathrooms. All rooms have hardwood floors, and most boast large windows. Residents will also have access to a kitchen, laundry facility, and communal lounge with a piano, fireplace, and a computer station.

# What are Negotiated Resolutions?

What are Negotiated Resolutions?

Negotiated Resolutions are processes guided and supported by the College that are meant to either (a) offer an pathway for resolving allegations of Discrimination, Harassment or Retaliation (when appropriate) and/or (b) address issues of culture or climate related to identity, inclusion and belonging.

# Conversations of Care

Intercultural Center (IC) (located in the Hormel-Nguyen Intercultural Center; Sproul Hall) Swarthmore College 500 College Avenue Swarthmore, PA 19081

IC Big Room (located in Clothier Hall, entrance in the Fragrance Garden) Swarthmore College 500 College Avenue Swarthmore, PA 19081

Hours

IC Big Room Hours: 10 AM - 8 PM M-F

By OneCard access only. Reserve through 25Live.

The Intercultural Center welcomes all visitors and is an accessible building. The IC has an elevator which reaches all floors and the front entrance (closest to Clothier Hall) is wheelchair accessible. There are accessible restrooms on the first and second floors. Additionally, there are accessible parking spaces outside of Clothier Hall that provide easy access to our center.

The Clothier Cloisters, (building adjacent to the Hormel Nguyen IC) and the IC Big Room include a ramped walkway and wheelchair lift, but no accessible restroom. Please contact space@swarthmore.edu for assistance with information and accommodations for your event in the Old IC Big Room. The space is reservable through 25Live.

The Intercultural Center is guided by the Swarthmore College Strategic Plan of building a community prepared for life in a multicultural society and world while educating the whole student. In order to bring that vision to life, IC will provide educational workshops to help our community build upon cultural appreciation, cultural competency, and the foundations of the ideals that fall under inclusion. These workshops provide opportunities to gain knowledge, build community, and demonstrate skills alongside your peers to advance your cultural competency practice and grow both personally and professionally.

Faculty, Staff, and Student Organizations, please use this form to request a staff member from the Intercultural Center to come to your class or group meeting.

What You Will Learn:

Address diversity issues and create educational pathways towards learning for students, faculty, and staff from diverse backgrounds

Develop education presentations workshops for our community members on diversity, equity, and inclusion ideals

Engage in educational dialogue to foster the development of intercultural community (re)alignment towards understanding, inclusion, cultural humility, and appreciation of diversity

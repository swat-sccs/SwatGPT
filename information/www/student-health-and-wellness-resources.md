# Resources

Swarthmore Student Health and Wellness service as an access point to care both on and off campus. Many resources are included in the sub-menu. If you do not find the information you are looking for, please call the health center at 610-328-8058 during normal business hours or send an email to health@swarthmore.edu and we will be happy to assist.

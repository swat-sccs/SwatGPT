# Acalog

Acalog is the College's catalog system. Anyone who is responsible for maintaining catalog content for their office, program or department should have an account.

The public catalog is available online at http://catalog.swarthmore.edu

## Login

To edit catalog content, login to Acalog Publisher at https://swarthmore.acalogadmin.com

1. Click the button for Swarthmore SSO Login.
2. Enter your regular Swarthmore username and password.

To request an account, please send an email to registrar@swarthmore.edu indicating which administrative chapters or academic programs you are responsible for maintaining.

## Select the Correct Catalog

To begin, select the catalog entitled **College Bulletin - Course Catalog [P]** from the drop-down box at the top of the screen.


## Edit Content

From the vertical left-hand menu, choose the type of content to be edited.

### For Administrative Chapters

- Choose **Gateway & Content.**
- Next step: Learn to edit administrative chapters >

### For Academic Programs

- Choose **Programs & Cores.**
- Next step: Learn to edit academic programs >

### For Courses

- Choose **Courses**
- Next step: Learn to edit courses >

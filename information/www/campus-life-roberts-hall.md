# Roberts Hall

Roberts is the third residence hall in the PPR trio, though it differs significantly from its neighbors in layout and atmosphere. Residents of PPR and PPRA share a courtyard, complete with a grill that often hosts barbeques. Roberts consists of singles, doubles, and/or triples arranged into suites, accommodating up to five people sharing a semi-private bathroom. Most rooms are arranged in blocks making this a nice place to share with close friends. A spacious and complete kitchen flows into a very large common area- the center of hall life in a residence hall of private suites. Each room has its own unique character, but they all are generally spacious with plenty of windows and high ceilings. Similar to its neighbors, Roberts does not house first year students.

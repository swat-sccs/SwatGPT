# Find Summer Opportunities

List of offices and departments that offer summer funding/stipends/opportunities. Some offer multiple TYPES of stipends, so click on their link to learn more.

| **Department or Program (see links for full list of opportunities)** | **Supports** | **Eligible Class Years (Current)** | 
| Academic Division (Arts and Humanities, Social Sciences, NSE) | Academic opportunities such as research, intensive language study, thesis/independent research | FYs, Sophs, Juniors | 
| __Career Services__ | SFEP is various roles at Philadelphia-based start-ups. Stipends for industries that are typically unpaid or under-paid. CSSS is for opportunities in unpaid industries. | FYs, Sophs, Juniors | 
| Center for Innovation & Leadership | The CIL will provide stipends for an internship, research opportunity, etc. in innovation, management, entrepreneurship, or leadership. | FYs, Sophs, Juniors | 
| Lang Center for Civic & Social Responsibility | Social impact internships, projects, or research | FYs, Sophs, Juniors, Seniors | 
| Mellon Mays Undergraduate Fellowship | Mentoring and academic research opportunities for students interested in Ph.D programs in the humanities and interpretive social sciences | Sophs, Juniors | 
| Rubin Mentoring Program | Mentorship and access to internships and research opportunities for first gen and/or low income students | FYs, Sophs, Juniors | 
| Swarthmore Summer Scholars (S3P) Mentors | Mentorship and leadership opportunities supporting incoming first-generation and low income students | FYs, Sophs, Juniors | 

Search by *Project Type, Class Year*, or *Awarding Office* by using the Funding Sources page. 

For Undocumented Students, there are Non-Employment Based Opportunities (NEBO) summer-based opportunities. To check eligibility, please visit the Sanctuary Committee's Policy on Experiential Learning Opportunities.

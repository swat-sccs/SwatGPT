# Graduation Information

## Graduation Date

Commencement will take place on Sunday, May 24, 2026.

## Senior Deadlines

- All PE requirements must be fulfilled by the last day of classes on Friday, May 1, 2026.
- By mid-April look for an email from the Registrar asking you to verify the correct listing of your name, major(s) and minor(s) for your diploma and the graduation list. See more about names, below.
- All financial obligations must be met by noon on Friday, May 15, 2026.
- All academic work and academic requirements (except Honors orals) must be fulfilled by the last day of finals on Thursday, May 14, 2026.
- All transfer credit must be received by Friday, May 1, 2026.
- All library materials must be returned to the Libraries by Tuesday, May 19, 2026.
- Seniors grades are due Tuesday, May19, 2026.

## Graduation Rule

By Swarthmore policy, to graduate seniors must be completely finished with degree requirements and approved for graduation by vote of the faculty (Catalog 8.9). Swarthmore does not permit seniors to walk in Commencement without being completely finished with degree requirements, nor are seniors allowed to participate without actually graduating if they defer their graduation year.

## Verify Graduation Name Listing and Diploma Information

In April, the Registrar's Office sends seniors an email requesting students verify the name, major(s) and minor(s) for their diploma. Students may email the Registrar now to make any needed changes; see more options below.

**NAMES**

Full legal names are printed on the diploma and graduation list, unless otherwise requested.

- Students intending to use the diploma as an international document (for working abroad) or as a legal document should use their legal name.
- The graduation list is not a legal document and names need not be a legal name.
- Information on chosen names may be found here.

**ACCENT MARKS**

To include accent marks on the diploma or Graduation List, please email registrar@swarthmore.edu and we will try to include these markings. Students are also welcome to send a photo of hand-written names with markings included.

**MAJORS AND MINORS**

Majors and minors may be confirmed by checking the degree audit in mySwarthmore. Any change of major or minor needs departmental approval.

**DEGREES AND DIPLOMAS**

The Swarthmore diploma includes a student's name, degree, level of Honors earned (if applicable), major(s) and minor(s).

Swarthmore awards the Bachelor of Arts degree for all its majors except the Engineering major, which earns a Bachelor of Science. Swarthmore students earning majors in Engineering and another field earn two degrees and two diplomas.

The Honors designation is awarded by the College, not by a particular department. Honors listed on the diploma will not be attributed to a particular major. The diploma lists the level of Honors earned on one line, followed by major(s) and minor(s) on separate lines.

**PARTICIPATION**

Participation in the graduation ceremony is encouraged but not required. After the faculty have approved the candidates for graduation and degree requirements have been reviewed, student degrees will be recorded and students will be graduated.

# Important Changes for Fall 2025

- A.R.E. Crum Ledge / NPPR Events
- Events and Parties policy: 
  - Developed an Alcohol Registered Event (A.R.E.) Party Permit policy for events to be hosted in both NPPR apartments and Crum Ledge.
- Appeals | Major (CJC) misconduct and Title IX
- Appeals procedures for Major (CJC) misconduct and Title IX: 
  - Creating a new Appellate Board to review all Major Misconduct and Title IX / College Defined Sexual Misconduct.
  - Each Appellate Board will be composed of three (3) members: a chair and two (2) other board members selected from a pool of specialty-trained board members appointed by the President's Office, Provost's Office, and Division of Student Affairs.
  - Minor Misconduct appeal process remains unchanged.
- Discrimination, Including Harassment (Protected Class)
- "Discrimination, Including Harassment (Protected Class)" policy: 
  - Updated the Discrimination, Including Harassment (Protected Class) policy to reflect the College’s new Notice of Non-Discrimination policy.
- Endangerment or Infliction of Physical Harm
- "Endangerment or Infliction of Physical Harm" policy: 
  - Formerly titled “Assault, Endangerment or Infliction of Physical Harm.”
  - Renamed to help clarify College expectations regarding violations associated with physical harm and violence.
  - Help clarify the College policy definition from the legal definition.
- Major Misconduct (CJC) Procedures
  - The College will create an audio recording of all Major Misconduct (CJC) and Title IX and College-Defined Sexual Misconduct student conduct procedures, whether they are administrative or CJC proceedings.
- New Student Handbook Website
- Student Handbook: 
  - Redesigned the entire Student Handbook in an effort to make the entire website more intuitive and easier to navigate.
- Records Retention and Reporting
- "Records Retention and Reporting" policy: 
  - Updated this section to more clearly identify that all student conduct records will be kept for seven (7) years after a student’s graduation from Swarthmore; and
  - Created an Outcome Reporting Details chart that outlines the reporting policy and timeline for various student conduct outcomes.
- Student Conduct Administrator
- "Student Conduct Administrator" definition: 
  - Created a section in the Student Conduct Policies and Procedures section of the Student Handbook to compile the various aspects of the role of the Student Conduct Administrator into one section.
- Warning Sanction
- "Warning" sanction definition: 
  - The sanction of Warning has been redefined.
  - “Warnings are recorded for internal purposes only and are not considered part of a student’s permanent student conduct record”.
  - The sanction of Warning is no longer eligible for appeal review.

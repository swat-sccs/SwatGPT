# Swarthmore Community Celebrates Eid

The Eid Banquet on Friday, September 23 packed Upper Tarble with students from across campus. A wonderful model of interfaith collaboration, the event was co-sponsored by Deshi, interfaith center, intercultural center, Islamic studies, Kehilah, Muslim Student Association, Newman, and Swarthmore Progressive Christians.

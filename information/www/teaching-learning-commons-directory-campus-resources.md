# Directory of Campus Resources

One of the roles of the TLC is to help community members access the teaching and learning resources they need. This comprehensive directory includes academic support programs, pedagogical and curricular development resources, and lifelong learning opportunities for students, faculty, and staff. Please let us know at tlc@swarthmore.edu if there is something you'd like us to add.

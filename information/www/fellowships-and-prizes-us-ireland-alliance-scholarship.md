# US-Ireland Alliance Scholarship

### Description

Up to twelve scholarships are awarded annually to support one year of graduate study in any discipline offered by an institution of higher learning in Ireland or Northern Ireland. An institutional endorsement is required for *graduating seniors only*. Alumni of the College can apply directly to the US-Ireland Alliance Scholarship.

### Selection Criteria

- U.S. citizenship
- Must be 18 years of age or older but NOT yet 30 on October 1 of the year of application
- Hold a bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university before beginning study under the US-Ireland Alliance Scholarship
- Evidence of academic achievement, leadership, and public service

### How to Apply

Follow the link below to register on the web site and gain access to an official application. Complete the application on-line and turn in all the following to mmandos1@swarthmore.edu:

- A copy of your application including your 1,000 word personal statement
- Four letters of recommendation. Recommenders must complete their letters on-line. In addition, please ask them to email their letters to mmandos1@swarthmore.edu.
- Unofficial Transcript
- Do not submit your application online until you have completed the college's internal nomination process.

### Selection Process

- After submitting your application materials, you will be interviewed in person (or remotely if you are unavailable in person) by members of the Fellowships and Prizes Committee.
- When all interviews are completed, the committee will select Swarthmore's nominees and notify you of your status.
- Within several weeks, the Scholarship will notify you to tell you if you have been selected to interview. Shortly after the regional interviews, you will be told whether or not you have won a US-Ireland Alliance Scholarship.

# Tripod: Get the Most out of the catalog

Tripod is the catalog of the TriCollege Libraries, a database of all the library materials at Bryn Mawr, Haverford and Swarthmore.

## Sign in:

- for seamless access to online resources
- to save searches and create lists of favorites
- to check your account online and renew items
- to request items from Bryn Mawr and Haverford

## Search by:

- **Books + Media + Articles** : for broad searches exploring topics and ideas. Results may include all types of library materials.
- **Books + Media:** in both physical and digital formats (ebooks, streaming movies, vinyl records, and much more).
- **Articles:**  to find full-text articles online.
- **Course Reserves:**  using the course title or professor's name to see what's on reserve for your class.

## Refine your results

Use the options on the left of your results to refine by options including resource type, library, language, date.

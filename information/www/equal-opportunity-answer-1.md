# Answer 1

### The correct response to Question #1 is : TRUE

All members of the Swarthmore College Community are responsible for ensuring that the work and academic environment is free from discrimination, including assault, harassment and other forms of discrimination based on sex, race, color, age, religion, national or ethnic origin, sexual orientation, gender identify or expression, pregnancy, marital status, medical condition, veteran status or disability.

# Holistic Support

### Identities Abroad

Whether students are researching their study abroad options or preparing for their semester overseas, it's important to consider how their identities may shape their experience.

Guidance on Identities Abroad
### Cultural Adjustment

All students will experience some degree of “culture shock” during their time abroad as they navigate a new culture. Returning to Swarthmore can also bring “reverse culture shock” as students struggle to readjust to familiar routines. Both experiences offer opportunities for personal growth and self-reflection.

Guidance on Cultural Adjustment
### Making the Most of your Experience

Unlock the full potential of the study abroad experience! Study abroad is an opportunity to develop valuable intercultural skills, refine career goals, and build a global network while making meaningful connections.

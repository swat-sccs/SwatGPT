# Transportation

If transportation is needed to the health center because you are too ill or injured to walk to the health center, please call Public Safety by dialing (610) 328-8333. Emergency medical transportation is provided as needed. An ambulance may be called for transport to the emergency room as needed.

### Transportation to medical appointments is the responsibility of students.

For those who need to take a cab, the Health Center has Philadelphia Taxi Cab Service vouchers that allow students to be billed at a later date to their student account. This will help students who don't have cash in hand or don't want to use a credit card. Please consider tipping the drivers as usual.

- Philadelphia Taxi Cab Service - 215-666-6666

### Other options:

Check SEPTA for Bus 109 and 110 schedules as well as train schedules.

# Fall 2026 Meal Plan Information

- Where will students on campus be able to use Dining Dollars?
- Dining Dollars can be used at Essie Mae’s, the Science Center Coffee Bar, campus vending machines, and the Swarthmore Campus and Community Store (for food, beverage, and convenience products only), beginning on the first day of classes.
- Will students be able to eat at any campus dining location this fall semester?
- Most campus dining locations will be open for food pick-up, take-out, or dining-in, but hours of operation will be modified. Please see our Dining Locations and Hours page for detailed information.
- Will students on campus be able to use Student Garnet Cash in the Ville?
- Student Garnet Cash will be available for use in the Ville during the fall semester.
- Where will students be able to use their meal equivalency?
- Science Center Coffee Bar, Monday-Friday, 9 p.m.- 11 p.m. and Saturday & Sunday, 7 p.m. - 11 p.m.

# Fall 2026 Meal Plan Information

The deadline extension after 8/15/26 email OneCard.

Swarthmore Dining is proud to provide you with your campus dining experience. Our staff have been working hard to ensure that those students who are returning to campus for the FALL 2026 semester can enjoy fresh, quality food as safely as possible.

### FALL 2026 Meal Plan

- **Swat Plan** - Unlimited meals, $175 Dining Dollars and $175 Swat Points
- **Garnet Plan** - 275 Meals, $350 Dining Dollars and $235 Swat Points
- **Phoenix Plan** - 225 Meals, $465 Dining Dollars and $350 Swat Points
- **Parrish Plan** - 160 Meals, $580 Dining Dollars and $465 Swat Points
- **Apartment Plan** - 160 Meals, $235 Dining Dollars and $805 Swat Points (PPR and Crum Ledge Residents only - more information to follow)
  - Confirmed PPR and Crumb Ledge residents will be given instructions to select the Apartments plan during the housing selection process.

Please be aware that the meal plans will begin at dinner the night before classes begin. Dining Dollars, will be available starting the first day of classes.

**Off Campus** students may purchase a Commuter Plan at the OneCard Office when you return to campus. - 100 meals and $115 Dining Dollars for $1,625.

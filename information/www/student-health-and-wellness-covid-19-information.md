# COVID-19 Information

Throughout the pandemic, Swarthmore College leadership sought the most up to date guidance from nationally recognized health groups and consultation from infectious disease specialists to steer the decisions regarding policies and procedures to foster health and safety and to mitigate risks on campus. Going forward, the College continues to base its decisions on the expert guidance from evidence-based guidelines and leaders in medicine and public health. Please refer to the Recommendations for COVID-19 and Other Respiratory Illnesses for more detailed information and guidance regarding COVID-19 and other viral respiratory illnesses.

For the most up-to-date information on COVID-19, please visit the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) website.

# Summer Opportunities

## Swarthmore Funded Summer Opportunities

Swarthmore College provides students with meaningful learning opportunities for the summer. This website centralizes participating programs/departments and common requirements. Generally they share:

- Similar application timelines
- Application method
- Each program/dept has its own contact person
- $6000 is the definition of a fully-funded stipend maximum for a given summer ($6000 for up to 10 weeks of full-time at ~35-40 hours per week)

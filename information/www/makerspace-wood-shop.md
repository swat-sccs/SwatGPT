# Wood Shop

#### Wood Shop Machines

Wood Shop machines require training and certification before use.

1. Drill Press
2. Sliding Compound Miter Saw
3. Bandsaws
4. Combination Sander
5. Spindle Sander
6. Panel Saw
7. Table Saw
8. Jointer
9. Planer

#### Wood Shop Hand Tools

Wood Shop hand tools are open for use during shop hours.

1. Drills and Drivers
2. Hand Sanders
3. Brad Nailer
4. Hammers
5. Screw Drivers
6. Hand Saws
7. Chisels
8. Dremmels
9. Measuring tools and more.

# The Commons Newsletter

News from the TLC

In our monthly newsletter, The TLC shares updates on our recent and upcoming events, links to partner programming, and other regular features including Accessbility Corner, with tips from the Accessibility Working Group and Teaching Spotlight, which highlights great pedagogy on campus. Our newsletter should arrive in your email, but if you can't find it, links to the web-version can be found below.

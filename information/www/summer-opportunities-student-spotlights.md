# Student Spotlights

### Anuujin Bayarjargal ‘27

*Center for Innovation & Leadership*

 *Tomyo Edtech, Web Design Intern* 

"I had the incredible opportunity to contribute to projects by translating and editing website content, creating video tutorials, and conducting usability tests.[...] Currently, as part of the LibUX Internship Program, I’m excited to further explore UX research, while my studies in econometrics and psychological well-being help me understand both the data and emotional aspects of design.

### Samantha Barnes '25

Lang Center, Chester Community Fellowship

"I am so grateful to have been a Chester Community Fellow and partner with the Ruth Bennett Community Farm this summer. [...] I am so grateful to have created a network of local farmers and food enthusiasts, young and old. Each of our supervisors were incredibly kind, funny, and curious. It was amazing to not only learn from them, but let them learn from us."

 


### Norah Wingfield ‘25

Career Services, Swarthmore Future Entrepreneurship Program

LoanStar, Sales Enablement Opportunity

"My tasks and projects ranged from market research and due diligence to assisting with implementation and creating slide decks. I particularly enjoyed conducting market research on elective medical financing opportunities nationwide, a skill that will be invaluable as I pursue a career in consulting. For the remainder of August, I will be assisting with auditing LoanStar’s CRM, another new project from which I hope to learn a great deal. The variability of my weekly schedule enabled me to acquire a wide range of technical, soft, and software skills in a short period of time. I became familiar with Google Sheets, slide decks, and CRM analysis, among other important business tools that I had not previously used at Swarthmore."



### Stathis Kalathias ‘25

"[A] key pillar of my experience was the opportunity to explore the beauty of the country outside of the office/classroom. ### Zhengfei (Alex) Li ‘25

Career Services, Swarthmore Future Entrepreneurship Program

SETVI, Business Analyst Opportunity

"One of the key skills I have developed during my internship is business communication. I frequently interacted with colleagues from various teams and time zones, learning to use different communication channels based on their availability. [...] Problem-solving has also been a significant part of my role. Initially, my approach was more exploratory, but I have since learned to address issues more professionally. [...] On the technical side, I’ve had the opportunity to work with various analytical tools to gain insights into business data and create comprehensive reports. [...] Moreover, I’ve benefited immensely from the mentorship and support of my colleagues. Their guidance has been invaluable in helping me navigate challenges and grow professionally. The collaborative environment at SETVI has allowed me to learn from diverse perspectives and enhance my problem-solving skills.

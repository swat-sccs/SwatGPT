# Visitor Information

## Hours

- Visitors are welcome in the Libraries for the purposes of using library resources, attending events, and engaging in research until 4:30 pm. See the Library Hours page for up to date, accurate information.
- Special Collections staff are available to answer questions during reading room hours. Please make an appointment to use primary sources and historical materials from the College Archives, Friends Historical Library, Peace Collection, and Rare Book Room.

## Maps and Directions

- Campus Maps, both Interactive and Printable
- Directions to Swarthmore College
- Swarthmore College encourages persons with disabilities to visit our spaces. Comprehensive, detailed access guides of our campus including McCabe Library are available from AccessAble. If you have any accessibility or mobility needs for accommodations, please let us know.

## Wireless Network

- Visitors may obtain guest accounts on the SwatGuest network.
- Accounts are good for 4 days and may be recreated when expired.

## **Use of Public Computers**

- Visitors may use our computers for access to library resources and research purposes, but preference is given to Swarthmore College students, faculty and staff members.
- Ask for login credentials at the Circulation Desk.
- Remember to log out of your guest session before you leave!

## Printing and Scanning

- Printing is available from the public computers.
- Visitors may use library scanners if they are not  being used by Swarthmore College students, faculty and staff.

## Group Study Spaces

Use of group study spaces is limited to Swarthmore College faculty, staff and students.

## Borrowing

For information about borrowing eligibility and policies, see our Visitor Borrowing page.

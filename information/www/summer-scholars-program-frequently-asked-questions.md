# Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply to be a Summer Scholar?

You must be invited to apply to become an S<sup>3</sup>P Scholar. Eligible applicants will be invited after they have been admitted to Swarthmore, and must matriculate to the college to be accepted into S<sup>3</sup>P. 

### What are the dates for the Summer Scholars Program?

The 2026 Summer Scholars Program will take place **July 5th - August 1st, 2026** and **January 13th - 18th, 2027.**

### How much does the program cost?

S<sup>3</sup>P is completely FREE. In fact, if you are selected as an S<sup>3</sup>P Scholar,  you will receive a $2,375 stipend for your full participation in the program.

### Is this program for academic credit?

While S<sup>3</sup>P provides a rigorous academic experience similar to what students will encounter during a real Swarthmore semester, Scholars will not receive course credit or official grades for their participation in S<sup>3</sup>P. For more information, please visit our Academic Program page.

### How do I apply? What is the application deadline?

Students must be invited to apply for S<sup>3</sup>P, and applications may be submitted online via your admitted student portal. Applications must be submitted by 11:59pm ET on May 14, 2026.

### Can international students apply to the Summer Scholars Program?

At this time, international students are not eligible to apply for the program.

### Can I have another job or academic commitment during S<sup>3</sup>P?

Scholars **must** commit to fully participating in this intensive program, which includes programming in the evenings and on weekends. Scholars may not make other academic or professional commitments for the duration of S<sup>3</sup>P.

### Is there a break between the end of S<sup>3</sup>P and the start of First-Year Orientation?

Yes, Scholars will return home on August 1, 2026. This year, First-Year Orientation begins on August 25, 2026.

# The Writing Center

## WHAT DOES THE WRITING CENTER OFFER?

- Assistance with all types of writing, from essays, research papers, applications, and cover letters, to lab reports, presentations, creative writing, and even help explore your personal writing!
- 30-minute, one-on-one appointments with a Writing Associate (WA) trained to assist at **ANY** stage of the writing process.
- 60-minute Speaking Associates (SPAs) appointments help you prepare to give a presentation or lead class discussions.
- Drop-ins welcome, or you can schedule an appointment at swarthmore.mywconline.com.
- Attend conferences in person (at the center located in Old Science Hall 120) or via Zoom.
- WAs who can work with writing in Spanish, French, Mandarin, and German.

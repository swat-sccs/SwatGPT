# Annual Engaged Scholarship Symposium

Since 2017, the Lang Center for Civic and Social Responsibility has sponsored an annual symposium on the topic of engaged scholarship. These symposia are designed to educate, support, and foster a community around engaged scholarship at Swarthmore College and beyond.

**9th Annual Engaged Scholarship Symposium | April 23-25, 2025**

In 2025, the Symposium took a slightly different form as we hosted the first in-person gathering since the pandemic. In collaboration with the Partners for Campus-Community Engagement (PCCE), the Lang Center convened 20+ leaders from over 15 colleges/universities and higher education networks for a two-day Symposium at Swarthmore College.

This event focused on:

- Exploring the meaning of *engaged liberal arts* and building a shared understanding of engaged scholarship in the liberal arts context.
- Addressing key strategic, structural, and operational challenges while examining trends shaping the future of engaged scholarship.
- Collaboratively designing actionable strategies to advance engaged scholarship across liberal arts colleges.

For more information and to explore ways to get involved, please contact Nimesh Ghimire, Senior Fellow at the Lang Center.

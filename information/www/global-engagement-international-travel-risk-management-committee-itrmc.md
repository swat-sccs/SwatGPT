# The International Travel Risk Management Committee (ITRMC)

### Governance of Swarthmore's International Travel Policy

It is the charge of the International Travel Risk Management Committee (ITRMC) to develop policies and procedures concerning international travel by members of the College community and to disseminate appropriate information and resources. This work is supported by additional members of the community as deemed necessary by the Provost. For safety and insurance purposes, all international Swarthmore Global Travel must adhere to the policies of the ITRMC.

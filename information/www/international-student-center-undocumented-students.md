# Undocumented Students

Swarthmore College is committed to supporting and expanding opportunities for Undocumented/Mixed-Status students. We offer supportive, sensitive and personalized services. The college offers a wide variety of resources and programs empowering students to help reach their academic and personal goals.

The International Student Center (ISC) serves as a resource center for Undocumented/Mixed-Status students. We welcome and support our students without regard to their immigration status. We are dedicated to fostering an inclusive and supportive environment that empowers undocumented students to thrive academically, personally, and professionally. We also maintain an up-to-date list of immigration lawyers and legal organizations offering pro bono legal aid.

In collaboration with the ISC, Sanctuary at Swarthmore offers additional resources for students and advocates and allies.

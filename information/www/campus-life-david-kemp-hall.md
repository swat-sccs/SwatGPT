# David Kemp Hall

One of the College's newest residence halls, David Kemp (DK) opened in Fall 2009. David Kemp is a mixed community. It houses all class years. Rooms are generally spacious with tile floors and white walls. Each floor has two wings -- one closer to the Dining Center and comprised primarily of double and triple rooms, and a second closer to Chester Road and Cunningham field, that consists of mostly singles. David Kemp shares a courtyard with its sister residence hall, Alice Paul, where residents can enjoy barbeques. Its second-floor lounge is a popular spot for social gatherings, and its close vicinity to the train station allows residents easy access to the city.

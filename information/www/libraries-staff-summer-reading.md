# Staff Summer Reading

Staff are *always* invited to enjoy library resources, but are especially invited to use the summertime for pleasure reading, and enter to win fabulous prizes by signing up for Staff Summer Reading 2026.

- *Any* kind of book counts - print books, audiobooks, ebooks!
  - Borrow books or audiobooks from the College Libraries or your local public library
  - Buy them from your favorite independent bookstore.
- Check our Community Guide for recommendations.

## Get started:

### Track your reading online with Beanstack

### Track your reading offline

- Visit McCabe Library to sign up OR
- Email summerreading@swarthmore.edu

## Visit Summer Reading Headquarters

Visit McCabe Monday-Friday, from 12:30-3:00 pm to:

- Check out books and get recommendations
- Attend events
- Report your progress and get prizes - a sticker each time you stop in, plus:
  - A reusable fan for 500 pages, or 10 hours
  - A book light for 1,500 pages, or 50 hours
  - A cooler bag for 5,000 pages, or 100 hours

## Celebrate

Come to the pizza party (tentatively scheduled for August 12) to learn who is the most voracious reader and what teams have read the most.

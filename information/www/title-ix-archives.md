# Archives

The Title IX Office is committed to maintaining a record of the College's past initiatives supporting sexual violence prevention and response.

In addition to programmatic compliance, these pages document ongoing efforts to provide clear communication to the campus community regarding state and federal guidance and regulations, policy updates, and education/training requirements. For current information on these efforts, please visit our Compliance page.

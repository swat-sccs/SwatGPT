# Swarthmore Survivors Collective

Uplifting Swarthmore’s Survivors of sexual assault, harassment, and violence.

The Swarthmore Survivors’ Collective is a peer-led support group for student survivors of sexual assault, harassment, and abuse. Spring 2026 meetings are held on Thursday evenings in the GSC from 7-8pm.

This is a confidential space led by fellow student survivors, focused on:

• Community support and understanding

• Resource sharing and campus outreach

• Planning future programming

• And of course — snacks!

All individuals are welcome regardless of gender, type of harm, or desire to actively participate in the community and conversation. For more information, follow the Survivors Collective on Instagram, or email pjennin1@swarthmore.edu

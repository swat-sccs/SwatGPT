# Annual Report

Each year, the Lang Center compiles an annual report that provides an overview of our activities for that year.

You can download the Lang Center's Annual Report for 2024-25. In our annual reports, we share information about our longstanding signature programs and initiatives as well as new or short-term projects. These programs exemplify how the Lang Center has advanced Swarthmore College’s commitment to intellectual rigor, ethical engagement, and social responsibility–locally, regionally, and globally– by connecting the curriculum, campus, and communities creatively. Our website contains complete descriptions of all of the Lang Center’s signature programs.

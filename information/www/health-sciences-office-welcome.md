# Welcome

Dear premedical students:

Welcome to Swarthmore!

Our Health Sciences/Pre-Law Office works with all students and alums interested in eventually entering the health professions.  We are available for individual advising and consultation, both through your time at Swarthmore and later, as you navigate the application process.  Be sure to consult our Guide to Premedical Studies, Pre-Dental FAQs, Pre-Vet FAQs, Podiatry FAQs, Physician Assistant and Nursing FAQs.  **Be sure we have your name and email, as we frequently send out information on programs, resources, internships and requirements.** 

Jennifer Lenway, the Health Sciences / Pre-law Administrative Assistant, and I stand ready to help and support you in any way we can. I have a sign-up sheet on my office door (Parrish 125W) for appointments. I am very available on email and happy to answer your question that way. If our doors are open, you are welcome to pop in with a question or to say hi. So please don't hesitate to be in touch. We look forward to getting to know you.


Gigi Simeone, PhD

Health Sciences Advisor

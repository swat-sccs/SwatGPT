## Vandalism on Campus

Early Friday morning, approximately six individuals in full disguise committed hundreds of acts of vandalism on campus. The incidents occurred over the span of about 30 minutes. The vandalism mainly consists of pro-Palestinian messages and symbols, as well as language targeting the College’s Board of Managers, spray-painted on buildings, trees, fences, walkways, and other areas of campus.

          

## Recent Campus Protest

On Friday, April 10, a small group of approximately 15-18 protesters held a rally on campus protesting ongoing conflicts in the Middle East. While the majority of the protesters were fully masked, we have reason to believe that at least some of the individuals involved have no affiliation with the College.

## Renaming Old Science Hall

Informed by input from members of the campus community, President Smith announced that Trotter Hall and its adjacent lawn has been renamed. Read her full message.

She expects to bring a new name to the Board of Managers later this year. In the meantime, the building will now be known as Old Science Hall.

## Revocation of Affiliation for Swarthmore Students for Justice in Palestine

On Feb. 20, 2025, the student organization Swarthmore Students for Justice in Palestine (SJP) was placed on an interim suspension due to numerous alleged violations of the Student Code of Conduct (Code). While on interim suspension and throughout the pending conduct process, SJP continued to engage in behavior on several occasions that allegedly violated the Code.

## Title IX Lawsuit

On Feb. 6, 2025, the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) adopted a new Participation Policy for Transgender Student-Athletes. The policy went into effect immediately. As a result, a transgender student-athlete at Swarthmore was unable to represent the College in certain NCAA competitions. On Aug. 14, that individual, now an alumna, filed a lawsuit alleging that the College, several employees, and the NCAA violated Title IX.

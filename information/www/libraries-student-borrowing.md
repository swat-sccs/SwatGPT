# Student Borrowing

- E-Z Borrow loans
  - Loan period: 12 weeks
  - Not renewable
  - Overdue penalty of $1 per day
- Multimedia
- ##### dvds & video
  - Loan period: 28 days (5 item limit)
  - Not renewable
 ##### cds & lps
  - Loan period: 7 days
  - 2 renewals
 ##### scores
  - Loan period: one semester
  - One renewal
- Reference Books
  - Loan period: 1 day
  - Not renewable
- Laptops and electronic equipment
- ##### Macbooks
  - Loan period: 6 hours, in library use only
  - Not renewable
 ##### chromebooks
  - Loan period: 1 week
  - Not renewable
 ##### chargers, cables, adaptors, laptop locks, headphones & remotes (for use in the viewing rooms on Mccabe level 3)
  - Loan period: 6 hours, in library use only
  - Not renewable
  - You may purchase earbuds for 75 cents!
- Journals: McCabe & Underhill (Cornell journals do not circulate)
- ##### Individual issues
  - Loan period: 7 days
  - One renewal
 ##### bound volumes
  - Loan period: until the end of the semester
  - Two renewals
- Course Reserves
- Course reserves provide fair and equitable access to course materials for everyone in your class. Borrow responsibly! ##### general reserves: Books
  - Loan period: 
		
    - Daytime: 3 hours
    - Overnight: from 3 hours before library closing until 9:30am weekdays, noon on weekends
  - Not renewable
 ##### general reserves: videos
  - Loan period: 4 hours, in library use only (although there may be some exceptions for items you may check out for 1 day.)
  - Not renewable
 ##### active honors reserves
  - Loan period: items due at 1pm the following day regardless of checkout time
  - Renewals:
		
    - McCabe & Underhill: unlimited renewals by phone or in person at the Reserves Desk, unless the item has been requested by someone else
    - Cornell: one renewal, unless the item has been requested by someone else.
 ##### inactive honors reserves
  - Loan period: until the end of the semester
		
    - All honors reserves are subject to recall and all become active on April 1 for the benefit of those studying for honors exams.
  - Renewals: 2
- Loan period: 
		
- Books, Popular Reading Room Books & Trico Books
  - Loan Period: 1 semester
  - Not renewable

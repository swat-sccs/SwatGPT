# Interfaith and the Black Cultural Center Collect Donations for MLK Day of Service

The Interfaith Center and the Black Cultural Center (BCC) are partnering to collect donations of school supplies for the campus Martin Luther King, Jr. Day morning of service on January 16. Supplies will be assembled by student, staff, and faculty volunteers and delivered to eight different after-school sites in Chester.

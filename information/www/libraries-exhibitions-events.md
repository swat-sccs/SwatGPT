# Exhibitions + Events

## McCabe Exhibition

Swarthmore's Libraries mark "America250" with a special exhibition in the McCabe Library Atrium. Drawing on art and artifacts from Special Collections, the exhibition explores how family, land, and nation have shaped the idea of “America,” and invites visitors to consider how they see this country and its history today.

**American Communities 1776 - 2026****May 1 - September 3, 2026**

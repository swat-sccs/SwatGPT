# Visitors Information

### Driving Directions from Whittier Parking Lot

To

Benjamin West Visitor Parking Lot And

The Visitor Information Center

1. From the Whittier parking lot, go back along Whittier Drive one block to the North Campus entrance.
2. Turn right on Elm Avenue, go one block.
3. Take the next right on Cedar Avenue, go one block.
4. Take the next left on College Avenue, go one block.
5. At the light, turn right on 320 / North Chester Road.
6. At the first driveway on your right, turn right into the Benjamin West Visitor Parking Lot.

**Visitor parking spaces are available to the right of the driveway.**

**The Benjamin West Visitor Information Center is a two-story house adjacent to the driveway, facing North Chester Road.**

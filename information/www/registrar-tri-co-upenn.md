# Tri-Co & UPenn

Swarthmore College is a member of the Tri-College Consortium (Tri-Co), which allows Swarthmore students to enroll in courses at Bryn Mawr College and Haverford College, and for students at Bryn Mawr and Haverford to enroll in Swarthmore courses. Swarthmore students may also take classes at the University of Pennsylvania.

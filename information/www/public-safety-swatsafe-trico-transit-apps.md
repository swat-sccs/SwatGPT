# SwatSafe & TriCo Transit Apps

## SwatSafe

SwatSafe is the official safety app of Swarthmore College. It can be downloaded on Apple and Android devices, and offers a variety of helpful features to support your safety, including:

- **Emergency Contacts:** Contact the correct services for the Swarthmore College area in case of an emergency or a non-emergency concern.
- **Panic Button/Mobile Bluelight:** Send your location to Swarthmore College security in real-time in case of a crisis.
- **Friend Walk:** Send your location to a friend through your device. Once the friend accepts the Friend Walk request, the user picks their destination and their friend tracks their location in real time.
- **Virtual WalkHome:** Allows Public Safety to monitor a user’s walk. If a user feels unsafe when walking on campus, they can request a Virtual WalkHome and a dispatcher on the other end will monitor their journey until they reach their destination.
- **Tip Reporting:** Multiple ways to report a safety/security concern directly to Public Safety.
- **Emergency Plans:** Campus emergency documentation that can prepare you for disasters or emergencies. This can be accessed even when users aren’t connected to Wi-Fi or cellular data.
- **Safety Notifications:** Receive instant notifications and instructions from Swarthmore College safety when on-campus emergencies occur.

## Tri-Co Transit App

The Tri-Co Transit app is your personal guide to real-time bus schedules, routes, and updates! Designed for the Haverford, Swarthmore, and Bryn Mawr communities, the app helps you stay on the move. You can download the TriCo Transit App on either iOS or Android.

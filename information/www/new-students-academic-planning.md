# Academic Planning

In mid-July, we will email you with information about how to access academic placement exams in the New Student Orientation Moodle course. In that course, we will provide more information about placement tests, degree requirements, advising and academic support. Academic advising and registration occur in late August during New Student Orientation on-campus.

We look forward to meeting you during Orientation. The Office of Academic Success is here to help you thrive at Swarthmore and beyond.

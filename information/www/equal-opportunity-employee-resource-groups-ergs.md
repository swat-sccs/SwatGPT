# Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)

## All ERGs are open to any faculty and staff interested in participating.

### **Goals of Employee Resource Groups**

At Swarthmore, we value the diverse perspectives that make up our College community. Employee Resource Groups (ERG) are voluntary groups of individuals formed around shared experiences, backgrounds, or identities. ERGs provide opportunities for Swarthmore staff and faculty members on campus to gather and build community with other around their shared interests and experiences. They offer interested faculty and staff members the chance to strengthen our College community through dialogue, education, professional development, and fun. They also help Swarthmore’s leadership learn about and respond to the needs and interests of our community members.

Every ERG is driven by the core values of ***commitment, respect, diversity, and honest communication***. ERGs are designed to:

- Foster a more diverse, equitable, and inclusive work environment consistent with the mission of Swarthmore College.
- Enhance the engagement, productivity, job satisfaction, and sense of belonging of our community members.
- Nurture informal mentorship relationships among members.
- Provide avenues through which Swarthmore faculty and staff can voice shared recommendations to College leadership.
- Provide a social space for Swarthmore faculty and staff to connect and network.

To learn more about the current groups, explore this page or contact the Equal Opportunity Officer at eoo@swarthmore.edu.

### **How ERGs work**

ERGs can be built around a range of professional and personal interests and affiliations. Although every ERG is guided by its membership in terms of activities, all ERGS:

- Are open to any faculty or staff member who wishes to participate
- Are guided by The program’s core values of commitment, respect, diversity, and honest communication
- Meet at least twice per semester
- Have at least one identified Coordinator who serves as both the liaison to the Equal Opportunity Officer and budget manager for the group

ERGs will receive support and funding from the Office of Equal Opportunity to help them meet their goals.

### **How can I start an ERG that doesn't already exist?**

ERG's are determined by employee interest at Swarthmore. If you are interested in starting a new ERG, reach out to the Equal Opportunity Officer within the Office of Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion to discuss your vision for the new group at eoo@swarthmore.edu.

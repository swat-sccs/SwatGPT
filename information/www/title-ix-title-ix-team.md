# Title IX Team

This staff committee, formed in fall of 2014, was created to review the Sexual Misconduct Task Force Report, paying particular attention to recommended changes and suggestions. In addition, this team reviews policy and resources, and works collaboratively to create seamless systems that support students through the sexual misconduct reporting and adjudication process.

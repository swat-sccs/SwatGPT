# Summer Resources 2023

**Student Affairs Summer Resources Guide**

On behalf of the Division of Student Affairs, the below document includes some of the resources available to students over the summer. All offices will operate on a flexible summer schedule with extended hours Monday-Thursday, and half days on Fridays, with the exception of the Student Health and Wellness Center and CAPS (Counseling and Psychological Services). The availability of individual staff will depend on vacation schedules.

# Programming

- Title IX Talks
- Title IX Talks is a series of roundtable discussions covering important topics within the field of prevention, remedy, and response work to sexual violence and gender discrimination. These 75-minute discussions are meant to plant seeds that can grow into more fruitful community dialogue around harm reduction. The focus is on education, providing a container to ask questions and resources for further learning. Do you have any suggestions for topics to cover in future Title IX Talks?
- How to Get Involved
- If you are a member of the Swarthmore community and interested in collaborating with us, please email Mwalsh2@swarthmore.edu. We look forward to hearing from you! Our office also partners with organizations in the community to bring panel discussions, special programs, and guest speaker events to campus.

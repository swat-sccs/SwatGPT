# Frequently Asked Questions

- **Where can I find accessible parking on campus?**
The online campus map includes the locations of all accessible parking on campus.
- **I've encountered a physical barrier to accessibility on campus. Who should I report it to?**Physical barriers to accessibility should be reported to ADA Compliance Program Manager Susan Smythe, who can be reached at ssmythe1@swarthmore.edu
- **What are the best practices for creating accessible course materials?**Please consult these course material guidelines for more information.
- **Where can I find information on organizing events that are accessible to the community?**The Events Management site contains resources for event organizers and speakers looking to make events accessible.
- **I am a disabled person. How can I make sure I am as safe as possible if there is an emergency on campus?**Please contact Public Safety to alert them of your concerns. Public Safety also provides information on how to best assist people with disabilities.
- **I have an injury and need help getting around campus and/or to an off-campus medical appointment. Who can help me?**Public Safety manages the Garnet Shuttle and can help arrange for transportation on- and off-campus.
- **Where are the accessible entrances and exits in my building?**Detailed information, including accessible entrances and exits, is available for every building on campus.
- **Where can I engage with the disabled community on campus?**
Student interest groups include the Swarthmore Disability Association and the ASL Club. Faculty and staff of the College are welcome to join the Disability Employee Resource Group.
- **How do I request dietary accommodations in dining?**Students who may need disability accommodations for dining can request accommodations through Student Disability Services by completing the Voluntary Disability Disclosure in mySwarthmore.
- **What kind of accessibility features do your athletic venues have?**Detailed accessibility information is available for Swarthmore's recreation centers and sports fields.

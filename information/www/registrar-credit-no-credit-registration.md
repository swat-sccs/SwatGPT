# Credit No Credit Registration

Use the electronic e-forms in mySwarthmore under Registration/Swarthmore e-Forms to:

- **register for CR/NC** for courses, or
- **uncover your shadow letter grade** .

**DEADLINE:**

By policy, the deadline is Friday at 11:59 PM of the ninth week of classes to complete the e-form to apply for Credit No Credit, or Friday Midnight of the fifth week if the course only meets for half the semester. For other semesters, see the Academic Calendar.

Unfortunately, there are no extensions to the deadline.

More about the policy and the deadline.

**SAVE YOUR RECEIPT:**

After completing the application e-form, you will receive an email receipt -- save your receipt to prove you submitted the form.

**ACCESSING THE E-FORM:**

Off Campus? Use VPN: To access the e-form, you need to be either on campus OR on the VPN into the network. Click here for VPN instructions.

For wifi on campus, use the eduroam wifi for direct access to the network to access the e-form.

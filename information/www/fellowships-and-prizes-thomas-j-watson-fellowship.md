# Thomas J. Watson Fellowship

### Description

The Watson Fellowship provides a one-year grant for purposeful, independent exploration outside the United States, allowing Fellows to engage their deepest interests on a global scale. Fellows plan out a year long program visiting any number of countries .

### Selection Criteria

- U.S. citizenship is NOT required.
- Open to graduating seniors ONLY.

### Internal Deadline

October 2, 2026

### How to Apply

Please contact Melissa Mandos at mmandos1@swarthmore.edu to access an online application. The application elements include:

- 1200 word personal statement
- 1200 word project proposal
- Two recommendations
- Transcript
- Supplementary material (optional)

### Link

### Selection Process

- The Fellowships and Prizes Committee reviews all applications.
- Based on application materials, interviews, and recommendations, the Committee selects four nominees. Nominees have an opportunity to revise their essays prior to Watson's national deadline, which is early November.
- All nominees receive an interview with a member of Watson's national selection committee.
- Final selections are announced on March 15, 2027

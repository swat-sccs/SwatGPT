# Swarthmore Dining

Dining in the community is at the heart of the college experience. Gathering to share a meal brings a chance to rest, share stories, and build lasting relationships with friends and colleagues.

Swarthmore Dining is proud to serve our community and provide nutritious and satisfying food in a welcoming setting to our students, faculty, and staff, every day. Our mission is to offer a diverse menu using the highest-quality ingredients, highlighting locally grown and sustainably produced products whenever possible. Our menu always includes vegetarian and vegan options, as well as accommodations for our community members who have dietary restrictions.

We are always open to feedback, suggestions, and recipe ideas! Reach out to us at dining@swarthmore.edu anytime.

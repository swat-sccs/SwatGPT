# Off-Campus Counseling Referral Process

CAPS offers care management, or services that make finding affordable, off-campus therapy, psychiatry, and other health services (like gender-affirming care) easier. The care manager will help you identify what you are looking for in off-campus care, understand your health insurance coverage, and connect you with a good fit.

If you would like to connect with the care manager, we ask that you first complete one access appointment for this academic year. To schedule an access appointment, click here. (Please note, you must be on campus internet or using VPN in order to access the scheduling page.)

If you have already completed an access appointment, you can email the care manager directly.

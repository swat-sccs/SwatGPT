# Other Health Professions

There are many other health related fields including public health, nursing, dental medicine, physician assistants, genetic counseling and pharmacy. Links to programs and other information about these fields, and others, are available on our Useful Websites page.

We also have resources in our library.

Gigi Simeone is available to meet with students to discuss their interest in these fields.

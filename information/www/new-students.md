# New Students

Congratulations on your admission to Swarthmore! We are so excited you will be joining us this upcoming academic year.

The next four years of your life will be a time of incredible intellectual and personal growth. The Division of Student Affairs staff facilitate your transition to the Swarthmore College community and your successful journey to graduation. We want to help you take full advantage of the opportunities that you will find here. The Division Directory will tell you a bit about who we are. Make particular note of Karen Henry ’87, dean of the first-year class, and Jennifer Marks-Gold, assistant dean and director of the international student programs.

**More information on Orientation and moving on to campus will come in May.** 

We look forward to talking with you all soon!

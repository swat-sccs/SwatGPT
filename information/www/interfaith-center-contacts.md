# Contacts

### Our Chaplain is Rev. Edward A. Windhaus

Office: Bond Hall - 20

Phone: 610.328.8578 

Email: ewindha1@swarthmore.edu

Father Ed Windhaus has a B.A. in Philosophy and an M.Div. from St. Charles Borromeo Seminary, Archdiocese of Philadelphia.; he holds an M.A. in Theology, Liturgical Studies from Catholic University of America.

He has been involved in Newman at University of Pennsylvania, Bucks County and Montgomery County Community Colleges, Pennsylvania.

He was a member/vice chair of the Philadelphia Archdiocesan Liturgical Commission and has given numerous liturgical presentations and workshops on liturgy. He authored, for the Liturgical Commission, the Archdiocesan Guidelines for Weddings, To Seal and to Strengthen; and for Funerals, Marked With the Sign of Faith.

He has served as Parochial Vicar or Pastor in five parishes of the Archdiocese of Philadelphia.

He was appointed to the TriCo Newman colleges in 2005.

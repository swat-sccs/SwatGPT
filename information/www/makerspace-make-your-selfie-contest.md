# Make Your Selfie Contest

**The Makerspace at Swarthmore College is hosting a Make Your Selfie Photo Contest**

Submissions must be made by students of Swarthmore College. **Submission deadline is midnight on October 31, 2020.**

**Submissions can be made here.**


Submit a selfie using found materials to transform yourself into someone (or something) new. 

**DIY only!** We are looking for ambition and creativity.

Submissions will be judged by Guest Judge, Visiting Sculpture Professor Erin Wheary.


First Place Prize: $100 Amazon Gift Card

Second Place Prize: $50 Amazon Gift Card

Third Place Prize: $25 Amazon Gift Card

Honorable Mention


Submissions containing more than one participant must follow COVID-19 social distancing guidelines as outlined by the Garnet Pledge. 

Submissions containing hate speech, illegal activity, or offensive material will not be accepted.

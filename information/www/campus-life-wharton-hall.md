# Wharton Hall

Wharton, centrally located and with beautiful views of the Crum and the amphitheater, is divided into three sections (AB, CD, and EF), CD houses only seniors. AB and EF house first-year student. Many of the remaining rooms in AB and EF are singles for upper-class students. Incoming students live in doubles, which can be large, or quads consisting of two or three-rooms. There are many small lounges scattered around the building, and a main lounge in the central section used for large group meetings, cooking, or registered events. Wharton is also located near the tennis courts and a quick walk from the dining hall.

# Research Help

## Help from humans

Librarians can help you get started with a research project, clarify your topic, help find and evaluate resources, cite sources, and provide guidance for your thesis.

Real people with research expertise monitor the librarian@swarthmore.edu account and library chat, and they will be delighted to respond to your questions.

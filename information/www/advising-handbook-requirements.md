# Requirements

The nine degree requirements are described in the College Catalog, Section 9: Degree Requirements. Details about degree requirements and the overall educational program are described in the College Catalog, Section 7: Educational Program.

As an advisor, you can review your advisees' degree requirements progress through the Degree Audit tool in Faculty Services.

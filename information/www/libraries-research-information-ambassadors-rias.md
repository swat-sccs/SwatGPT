# Research & Information Ambassadors (RIAs)

## **What's a RIA?**

Research & Information Ambassadors (RIAs) are leaders in student library engagement. They develop detailed knowledge of library resources and services, and share this information with fellow students by participating in New Student Orientation, leading library tours, writing for social media and the library newsletter, The Latest from the Libraries, and hosting events throughout the semester.

### Program Coordinators

The RIAs program is coordinated by Abigail Weil, Research & Instruction Outreach Librarian, and Simon Elichko, Social Sciences & Data Librarian.

# TLC Programs & Services

TLC programs create opportunities for Swarthmore community members to advance their teaching and learning goals. Ranging from casual drop-in activities to semester-long learning communities, our programming is designed in response to community needs and interests. Read more about our approach in our mission and values. Program registration is shared in The Commons, our monthly newsletter. Please reach out to us at tlc@swarthmore.edu if you would like to get involved.

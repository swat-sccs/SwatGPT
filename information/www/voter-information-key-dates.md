# Key Dates

| **October 19, 2026** | Last day for your registration to be RECEIVED before the Pennsylvania primary election | 
| **October 27, 2026** | Last day for applications for mail-in or civilian absentee ballots to be RECEIVED | 
| **November 3, 2026** | Last day for the County Board of Elections to receive mail-in/absentee ballots. Must be received by 8:00pm | 
| **November 3, 2026** | ELECTION DAY! |

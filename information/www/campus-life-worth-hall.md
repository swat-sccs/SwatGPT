# Worth Hall

“Living in Worth is like living in Hogwarts with your best friends.”

Worth houses juniors and seniors (and sometimes sophomores) in singles, two-room doubles, and the occasional two-room triple. The building is split into six separate sections (Towers) with interconnected indoor passageways. All the bathrooms are semi-private and gender-inclusive. Residents enjoy views of a scenic courtyard that in warmer weather is often used for evening social gatherings and all campus events. Worth is always lively and has an amazing community.

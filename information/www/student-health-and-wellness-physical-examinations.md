# Physical Examinations

Physical Health Exams are available through the Student Health and Wellness Service for off-campus study programs, volunteer applications, Peace Corps applications, fellowship grants, etc.

It is wise to make an appointment for your physical exam several weeks before your application deadline. Some programs will require laboratory testing which may take additional time receive results. Laboratory testing will be billed through the students health insurance plan. Students may schedule for an appointment with one of our health care providers by calling 610-328-8058.

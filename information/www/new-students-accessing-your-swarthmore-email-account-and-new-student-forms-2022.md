# Accessing Your Swarthmore Email Account and New Student Forms 2022

*This message was sent by Director Rebecca Weintraub-Barth on May 18, 2022:*

Greetings,


Welcome to Swarthmore! In partnership with my colleagues across campus, I coordinate new student orientation. We look forward to meeting you in August. I write to share instructions on how to access your new Swarthmore email account and complete the new student forms in mySwarthmore.


**Please follow the instructions under the “TO DO” sections to set up your Swarthmore email account and complete the new student forms by June 1**. Your Swarthmore username is listed at the bottom of this message. Your Student ID number will be provided to you in a separate email today. 


Email is our main way of communicating with you. Please regularly check your new Swarthmore email account. If you have any questions about setting up your Swarthmore network or email account, email the College’s Helpdesk at support@swarthmore.edu. If you have general questions and are not sure who to ask, please email new_student_question@


In July, we will share information about academic placement exams. In August, we will share information about housing assignments. We look forward to welcoming you to campus for new student orientation, which begins on August 23.


Please explore the website for new students. 


We look forward to getting to know you. 


Sincerely,

Rebecca Weintraub-Barth, Director of Student Activities and Leadership

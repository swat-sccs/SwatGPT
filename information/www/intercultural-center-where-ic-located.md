# Where is the IC located?

The **Intercultural Center (IC)** is located in the Hormel-Nguyen Intercultural Center at Sproul Hall. You can identify it on campus by its green dome.

The **IC Big Room** is located in **Clothier Hall** with the entrance in the Theresa Lang Fragrance Garden alongside the Cloisters.

For more information about campus locations, please utilize the campus map.

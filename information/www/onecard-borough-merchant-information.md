# Borough Merchant Information

You can use Swat Points at a range of locations across Swarthmore’s downtown, affectionately known as the ville. Stock up on fresh food from the Co-op, grab a slice from Renato, and more. See where your Swat Points are accepted, below. Please note, participating merchants may change.

- **Aria**
Mediterranean Food. Closed Sundays and Mondays. Opening evenings until 8 p.m.
- **Bamboo Bistro**  
Chinese and Japanese cuisine.
- **Chef Reeky's** **(located in nearby Woodlyn, PA)**
Café and juice bar with vegan options.
- **Dunkin’ Donuts/Baskin Robbins**
Coffee, donuts, and ice cream
- **Everybody Eats Café****(located in nearby Chester, PA)**
Elevated soul food prepared with love
- **Hobbs Coffee**
Gourmet coffee and food
- **Luna's Mexican Grill**
Authentic Mexican breakfast, lunch, and dinner
- **Name a Better Duo Tacos and Bowls** **(located in nearby Chester, PA)**
Afro-Mexican cuisine
- **Pastry Pants**
Neighborhood bakery
- **Renato Pizza** 
Pizza, stromboli, and sandwiches
- **Vicky’s Place**
Diner serving breakfast and lunch.

## **Groceries**

- **Swarthmore Co-op**
Grocery, deli, and fresh prepared food
- **Swarthmore Farmers Market** 
Fresh, local selections on Saturdays from May - November (beginning 5/9/26)

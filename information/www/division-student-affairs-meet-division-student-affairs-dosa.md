# Meet the Division of Student Affairs (DoSA)

# 
            Meet the Division of Student Affairs (DoSA)
      

            
                                      
  The Division of Student Affairs supports the holistic development of students through providing experiences and services that contribute to their overall learning in and out of the classroom, enhances the student experience at the College, and prepares students for life after graduation.  

The Vice President for Student Affairs also oversees and provides leadership for:

Academic Success | Black Cultural Center | Career Services | Center for Innovation and Leadership | Counseling and Psychological Services | First Generation and Low-Income Student Initiatives | Gender & Sexuality Center | Inclusive Excellence & Community Development | Intercultural Center | Interfaith Center | International Student Center | Office of Student Engagement | Registrar | Student Conduct | Student Deans | Worth Student Health & Wellness

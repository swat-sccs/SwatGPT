# Cooperative Agreements

## Domestic Study Away Through GEO

The Global Engagement Office (GEO) facilitates Swarthmore-approved opportunities to spend a semester "studying abroad" within the United States. Learn more.

## Military & Veterans

Jenna Hunt, Assistant Registrar, is Swarthmore College's point of contact for support services for veterans, military service members, and their families. Please contact her for questions about veterans benefits and the College's Yellow Ribbon Program.

## ROTC

Swarthmore College has a cooperative agreement with the Reserve Officers' Training Corps (ROTC) program at St. Joseph's University in Philadelphia. Learn more.

## Strath Haven High School Students

Eligible juniors and seniors at Strath Haven High School (SHHS) are permitted to enroll in select courses at Swarthmore College with the permission of the instructor and the appropriate SHHS officials. Learn more.

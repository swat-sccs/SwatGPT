# How To Tell If You've Responded To an Email

In Gmail, there are a few ways to know whether or not you have responded to an email. Your options will depend on whether or not you are using Conversation View – You can learn more about this feature on [Google’s support site](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/5900?hl=en). 

Should you use Conversation View? It’s up to you! Feel free to test the feature out, and decide whether it’s a good fit for your workflow. 

## With Conversation View On

Navigate to [mail.gmail.com](http://mail.gmail.com) and take a look at your inbox. If you have already replied to a message, a **number** should appear in the **sender** field to the right of the original sender’s name. 

In the example below, John sent me an email, which I have replied to. The number to the right of John’s name corresponds to the total number of emails in the thread.

The user's messages to another user named John are indicated with a number '3' -- This is the number of emails in the thread.](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/1/1c/Screenshot_2024-08-15_at_3.25.40_PM.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:Screenshot_2024-08-15_at_3.25.40_PM.png)

## With Conversation View Off

From the inbox, hold your mouse over a sender's name (but don't click). 

In the address card that appears, select  **Open Detailed View.**

[![Contact view for the user 'John'. Below his name, a button reads 'Open Detailed View'.](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/a/ab/Screenshot_2024-08-15_at_3.30.47_PM.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:Screenshot_2024-08-15_at_3.30.47_PM.png)

The resulting screen will show your open threads with that user, as well as recent calendar events and shared documents. This should include your replies to the user, as well. Click on a given message to view the full thread. 

**Alternatively** , you can run a search of your mailbox using the following structure: "from:me to:INSERTEMAILHERE".

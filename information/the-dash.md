## Description

[The Dash](https://dash.swarthmore.edu) is produced by the Swarthmore College Communications Office and Information Technology Services (ITS). It is a central location for students and employees to gain access to a broad range of information and services. 

## Feedback

If you have a comment, a request, or a suggestion for improving the Dash, please send your request to [dash@swarthmore.edu](mailto:dash@swarthmore.edu).

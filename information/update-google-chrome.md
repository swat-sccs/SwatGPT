# Update Google Chrome

## Update the Google Chrome Browser

  1. Open Google Chrome on your Windows or Mac computer.
  2. In the upper right corner, click on the three vertical dots icon.



[Chrome three vertical dots button image](https://support.ti.davidson.edu/hc/article_attachments/360000845087/Chrome_Settings.JPG)

  1. In the resulting drop-down menu, select the **Help** > **About Google Chrome** menu options. Simply opening this page will initiate the update process.
  2. Once the browser updates, you might need to click on the **Relaunch** button to reopen Chrome.



### If the Update Fails

  1. If the update fails, it is recommended that you download and install the latest version of Chrome. Start by opening this site <https://www.google.com/chrome/browser>
  2. Click on the **DOWNLOAD CHROME** button.
  3. Open the installer and follow the instructions to complete the installation of Chrome.

## General Remote Teaching Resources

[Stanford has an informative site for faculty teaching remotely](https://teachanywhere.stanford.edu/best-practices), including a set of best practices for lectures, lab activities, fostering collaboration among students, and assessing student work. 

[Teaching Effectively During Times of Disruption](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ccsudB2vwZ_GJYoKlFzGbtnmftGcXwCIwxzf-jkkoCU/preview#heading=h.ujnzpvd0k21x) \- from Stanford (Jenae Cohn and Beth Seltzer) 

[Going Online in a Hurry](https://www.vanderbilt.edu/brightspace/2020/03/06/putting-some-of-your-course-content-online-in-a-hurry-we-have-resources-for-you/) \- Vanderbilt 

[Going Online in a Hurry: What to Do and Where to Start](https://www.chronicle.com/article/Going-Online-in-a-Hurry-What/248207?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=facebook_group_post&utm_campaign=group_1526889350715555) \- The Chronicle of Higher Education 

## Hybrid Course Resources

[HyFlex Course Design Examples](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gokdIjWbfERADiswlUBL4hE81hEnNiEYmBzRUeDfUdk/preview) \- example 50 and 75 minute sessions with details for teaching the same course to Synchronous in person, Blended online, and Asynchronous online students. From Kevin Kelly's article, "COVID-19 Planning for Fall 2020: A Closer Look at Hybrid-Flexible Course Design". 

## Exams

[Strategies for Exams During Remote Learning](https://drive.google.com/file/d/186nEXuIv05er1mxnF-WMSY0esT0SwJc7/view) \- from Amherst's Center for Teaching and Learning 

[Unproctored Online Assessments](https://ce.uwex.edu/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/UnproctoredAssessments.pdf) \- University of Wisconsin Extended Campus Resource 

[Best Practices for Online Tests](https://community.pepperdine.edu/seaver/center-teaching-excellence/keep-on-teaching/online-tests.htm) \- Pepperdine 

[Thwarting online exam cheating without proctor supervision](https://www.aabri.com/manuscripts/11775.pdf) \- Jr, Cluskey, & Ehlen, Craig & Raiborn, Mitchell. (2011). Journal of Academic and Business Ethics.

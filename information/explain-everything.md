## Description

Explain Everything Whiteboard is a subscription-based digital whiteboard app that can be used to record a presentation or collaborate on a shared project. Explain Everything Whiteboard has collaboration features as well as a cloud storage feature called Explain Drive. You can access your Explain Everything Drive account via [drive.explaineverything.com](https://drive.explaineverything.com/). Log in via Google using your Swarthmore email address. 

## EDU Paid Subscription Access

Licenses for the paid version of Explain Everything are provided by ITS under our subscription and can be installed on College-owned devices. To request access, please contact ITS at [support@swarthmore.edu](mailto:support@swarthmore.edu).

## Installation Instructions

### iPad

You can find Explain Everything Whiteboard app listed in the Jamf Self Service app on your College-owned iPad. Instructions for installing iPad apps from Jamf Self-Service can be found using the link below. 

[iPad Jamf Self Service](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/iPad_Jamf_Self_Service "iPad Jamf Self Service")

Once you've installed Explain Everything Whiteboard onto your iPad set up your account using your **Swarthmore email address**. You will be **automatically enrolled** under our subscription license. You can also access your Explain Everything Drive account via [drive.explaineverything.com](https://drive.explaineverything.com/). Log in via Google using your Swarthmore email address. 

### Android and Chromebook

Instructions to [Download Explain Everything for Android and Chromebooks from Google Play](https://explaineverything.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360015308253-Downloading-Explain-Everything-for-Android).

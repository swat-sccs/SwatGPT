## Accessing Swarthmore Remotely

### For all Employees

  * [Check your Duo devices](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Duo_Two-Factor_Authentication "Duo Two-Factor Authentication")
  * Have at least one device registered with Duo that you can access from off-campus 
    * We strongly recommend adding your smartphone, or an app-enabled mobile device, to the Duo app
    * Check any hardware tokens you may have for Duo authentication and ensure they are working properly
  * Install the [Swarthmore Virtual Private Network (VPN)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/VPN_and_Off-Campus_Access "VPN and Off-Campus Access") on any devices you wish to use off-campus 
    * Verify that you can log in to the Swarthmore VPN and that the VPN application is up-to-date
  * Upload files to Google Drive and review [Google Drive File Sharing](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Sharing_a_File_from_Google_Drive "Sharing a File from Google Drive") for collaborating on documents and projects
  * Make sure you can connect to the Internet. Check out our [tips for better WiFi](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Tips_for_Better_WiFi "Tips for Better WiFi") for more information.

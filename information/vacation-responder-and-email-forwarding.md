# Vacation Responder and Email Forwarding

## Setting up a Vacation Responder

  1. Click on the gear icon in the top-right.
  2. Choose **See all settings**.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the General settings page.
  4. Choose **Vacation responder on**.
  5. Now you can set start and end dates, as well as choose a subject and type out your vacation message.
  6. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page.



You will have two additional options under the message field to choose from: Only send a response to people in my Contacts and Only send a response to people in Swarthmore.edu. You can check either or both of these as needed. To ensure everyone receives your vacation responder, leave both of these options unchecked. 

## Setting up Email Forwarding

  1. Click on the gear icon in the top-right.
  2. Choose **See all settings**.
  3. Along the top of the Settings window, choose **Forwarding and POP/IMAP**.
  4. In the top Forwarding section choose **Forward a copy of incoming mail to**.
  5. Enter the email address you wish to forward mail to.
  6. **Recommended:** Keep the default setting of **keep Swarthmore.edu Mail's copy in the Inbox**.
  7. A confirmation email will be sent to the email you wish to forward to. You must accept this confirmation before message forwarding will begin.



You can turn forwarding off by choosing **Disable forwarding** in the **Forwarding and POP/IMAP** settings at any time.

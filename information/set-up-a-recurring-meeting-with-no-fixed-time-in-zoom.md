# Set Up a Recurring Meeting with No Fixed Time in Zoom

## Set up a recurring meeting with No Fixed Time

  1. Sign into swarthmore.zoom.us using your Swarthmore credentials.
  2. Click on the **Meetings** tab from the left-hand menu.
  3. Click on **Schedule a meeting** from the top right.
  4. Enter the information for the meeting. 
     1. The **Topic** will be the name of the meeting. If it is for a class, be sure to title it with the name of the course and the semester the meeting is for.
  5. Select the **Recurring meeting** check box.
  6. Select **No Fixed Time** from the **Recurrence** dropdown menu.
  7. Set meeting options that are applicable.
  8. Click **Save** when finished.
  9. Share the zoom meeting invitation through your Moodle course, calendar invite, or email.

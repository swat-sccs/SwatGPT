# Signing Out of iCloud on iPad

If you have a College-owned iPad and you need to return it to ITS, please be sure you are signed out of iCloud so we can easily reset the device. To sign out of iCloud follow the instructions below. 

**How to sign out of iCloud on your iPad**

  1. Click on the Settings app on your iPad
  2. Tap the Apple ID banner at the top of the screen
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Sign Out
  4. Tap Sign out of iCloud
  5. Enter your password for your iCloud Apple ID

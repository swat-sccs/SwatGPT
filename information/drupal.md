## Description

Drupal is an open-source content management system (CMS), to build and update websites hosted at [www.swarthmore.edu](http://www.swarthmore.edu/). 

Authentication is required in order to edit Drupal pages. 

Find out more about Drupal in our [Drupal Knowledge Base Pages](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Drupal_Home "Drupal Home")

## Troubleshooting

Please contact our web team through [support@swarthmore.edu](mailto:support@swarthmore.edu) for help with Drupal questions.

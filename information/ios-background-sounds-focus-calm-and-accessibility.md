# iOS Background Sounds Focus, Calm, and Accessibility

Apple’s Background Sounds is a feature available on iPhones, iPads, and Apple desktops running iOS 15 or later. We talk more about how it might be useful in [Apple’s “Background Sounds”: Focus, Calm, and Accessibility in One Feature](https://blogs.swarthmore.edu/its/2025/10/21/apples-background-sounds-focus-calm-and-accessibility-in-one-feature/). 

## Set Up Background Sounds

### iOS

[![iOS Settings menu highlighting Accessibility, then Accessibility menu highlighting Audio & Visual option](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/7/78/ALLY_iOS_Background_Sounds_Focus%2C_Calm%2C_and_Accessibility-MobileSounds1.jpg)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:ALLY_iOS_Background_Sounds_Focus,_Calm,_and_Accessibility-MobileSounds1.jpg) [![iOS Accessibility settings showing Audio & Visual options with Background Sounds off, and the Background Sounds menu with the feature toggled on and sound set to Balanced Noise.](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/f/f5/ALLY_iOS_Background_Sounds_Focus%2C_Calm%2C_and_Accessibility-MobileSounds2.jpg)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:ALLY_iOS_Background_Sounds_Focus,_Calm,_and_Accessibility-MobileSounds2.jpg)

  1. Open **Settings** then **Accessibility**.
  2. Go to **Audio & Visual (under Hearing)**.
  3. Select **Background Sounds**.
  4. Turn **Background Sounds** on.
  5. Choose your preferred sound and decide if it should continue when the screen locks.



### macOS

[![Accessibility settings on macOS showing the Audio section with Background Sounds enabled to mask environmental noise and aid focus, calm, or rest.](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/4/4d/ALLY_iOS_Background_Sounds_Focus%2C_Calm%2C_and_Accessibility-DesktopSounds.jpg)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:ALLY_iOS_Background_Sounds_Focus,_Calm,_and_Accessibility-DesktopSounds.jpg)

  1. Open **System Settings** , then go to **Accessibility**.
  2. Go to **Audio**.
  3. Turn **Background Sounds** on and choose your preferences.

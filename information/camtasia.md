## About

Camtasia is a screen-recording and video editing software program. It is useful for recording and editing screencasts. 

Swarthmore has a limited number of licenses for Camtasia available upon request. 

## Download

Download and install a [free trial of Camtasia from the TechSmith website](https://www.techsmith.com/download/camtasia/). 

To obtain a permanent license, email support@swarthmore.edu to request a license code then follow the instructions from the TechSmith website – [Camtasia: Entering Your Software Key](https://support.techsmith.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021040091).

# Screen Capture, Recording, and Editing

The College has acquired licenses for two tools that are designed for screen capture and recording ([Snagit](https://www.techsmith.com/screen-capture.html)), one of which ([Camtasia](https://www.techsmith.com/video-editor.html)) is also an easy tool for editing captured content and making it into presentations for your students. While faculty will need to [contact ITS](http://support.swarthmore.edu) for an installation of one of these tools, they are both fairly easy to use, and quick to learn, but still quite powerful. 

## For simple Screen Capture & Recording: Snagit

Tutorials for Snagit can be found here: <https://www.techsmith.com/tutorial-snagit.html>

## For more advanced Screen Capture, Recording and Editing of a video (including captioning): Camtasia

Tutorials for Camtasia can be found at this site: <https://www.techsmith.com/tutorial-camtasia.html>

# Export Student Grades into a Spreadsheet

In order to export all of the student grades from the grade book into an excel spreadsheet follow the steps below: 

  1. Click on **Grades** from the top of the page to go to the **Grader Report**
  2. Click on **Export** from the pulldown menu
  3. Then select** Excel Spreadsheet **from the pulldown
  4. Select Grade Items to be included
  5. Click** Download**

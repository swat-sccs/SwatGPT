## Installation Information

Visit the [R Project website](https://www.r-project.org/) for downloads and installation instructions. 

## Learning R

There are several ways to learn **R** at Swarthmore. If you're in a class that requires **R** , please talk with your instructor first. There are also resources available through the open source R Project and various blogs and sites. Swarthmore has a limited supply of licenses for online training through [DataCamp](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/DataCamp "DataCamp") as well, targeted for Faculty and Staff users who are interested in learning **R** for use in their research and/or classes. 

## Accessibility Features

R has a number of [options and features that can improve accessibility](https://support.rstudio.com/hc/en-us/articles/360044226673-RStudio-Accessibility-Features)

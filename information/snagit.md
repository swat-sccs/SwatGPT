# Snagit

Quick computer screen video capture software 

Faculty, Staff 

macOS, Windows 

  


No 

Property | Value   
---|---  
Description | Quick computer screen video capture software   
Access | Faculty, Staff   
Platform | macOS Windows  
  
When you need to quickly capture your screen, including capturing all of a long or wide web page, or create a short video, or even convert a short video into an animated GIF without a lot of fuss, then [Snagit](https://www.techsmith.com/screen-capture.html) is the tool for you. 

## Download

Contact [support@swarthmore.edu](mailto:support@swarthmore.edu) for a link to the software download and a license code. 

ITS tracks installations of this software due to licensing restrictions.   
---  
  
## Installation Instructions

**Windows:** Download the package to your desktop and double click to install. 

**Mac:** Download the appropriate package to your desktop and unzip. 

After either installation, you will need to provide a license code and follow the instructions provided by TechSmith: 

  * [How to enter your Snagit License Key](https://support.techsmith.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021040791-Snagit-Entering-Your-Software-Key)

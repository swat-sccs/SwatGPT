## Student view

Students click on the H5P icon in the course to access it. 

Depending on the H5P activity and teacher's settings, the activity may be gradable. Students can see their attempts from the View my attempts link top right. 

[![](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/8/85/StudentH5Pview.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:StudentH5Pview.png)

  
In the attempts report, the success column reports when the student has obtained 100% in the activity. The completion column reports whether the student went through the H5P content to the end. (The attempts report only displays data from the H5P content - there is no connection with the 'Grade to pass' activity setting nor with activity completion settings.) 

[![](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/5/59/StudentH5PScore.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:StudentH5PScore.png)

## Teacher view

Teachers can see all attempts by all users from the link above the activity. Note that the figure refers to the number of attempts, not to the number of students who have attempted the activity. 

[![](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/1/19/h5pteachersubmitted.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:h5pteachersubmitted.png)

## Capabilities

There are 4 capabilities relating to the H5P activity: 

  * [Add a new H5P](https://docs.moodle.org/402/en/Capabilities/mod/h5pactivity:addinstance) activity to the course
  * [Review H5P attempts](https://docs.moodle.org/402/en/Capabilities/mod/h5pactivity:reviewattempts)
  * [Submit H5P attempts](https://docs.moodle.org/402/en/Capabilities/mod/h5pactivity:submit)
  * [View H5P](https://docs.moodle.org/402/en/Capabilities/mod/h5pactivity:view)



## Accessibility

H5P has many activity types, and they all have varying levels of accessibility. Please reach out to accessibility@swarthmore.edu to help ensure your H5P activities will be accessible to all users.

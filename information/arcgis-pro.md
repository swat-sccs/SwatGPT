## Description

ArcGIS Pro is the industry standard Geographic Information System (GIS) software. It can be installed on computers owned and used by Swarthmore students, faculty and staff, while they are affiliated with the College. 

## Student access

ArcGIS (Arc Map, Arc Catalog, etc.) is installed on all public Windows machines to be available for student use. We recommend using the Singer 222 Windows computer classroom, which is open for general use when there is not a class in session. 

Students with their own Windows computers can download and install ArcGIS Pro on those computers for use while enrolled at Swarthmore. See below for details of how to get the installer. 

## Training

Many online training courses from ESRI are available for free through our site license. Access to these training resources is through our ArcGIS Online instance. Log into [swarthmore.maps.arcgis.com](https://swarthmore.maps.arcgis.com/), click the blue Swarthmore College button to sign-in with your campus network credentials, then select your username in the upper right corner of the screen, and select Training on the pull-down menu. Any course that is listed as "free" or "Requires Maintenance" should be available to you. 

## Installation Instructions

To install ArcGIS Pro or other ESRI Tools, you must have administrative privileges. After you download the installer, don't just click it; right-click and **Run as Administrator**. 

### Installation Steps

  1. Go to the Swarthmore College ArcGIS Online instance at [swarthmore.maps.arcgis.com](https://swarthmore.maps.arcgis.com)
  2. Click the blue Swarthmore College button to sign-in with your Swarthmore credentials
  3. Click on your **username** in the upper right corner of the screen.
  4. Choose **My settings**
  5. Click on **Licenses** in the left side panel
  6. You should see a link to **Download** to the right of the line for ArcGIS Pro, click that link to download the installer
  7. Follow these instructions to install ArcGIS Pro: [pro.arcgis.com/en/pro-app/latest/get-started/install-and-sign-in-to-arcgis-pro.htm](https://pro.arcgis.com/en/pro-app/latest/get-started/install-and-sign-in-to-arcgis-pro.htm)

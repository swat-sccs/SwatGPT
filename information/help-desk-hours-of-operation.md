# Help Desk Support Offerings

The ITS Help Desk is located in Clothier Hall through July 2026 [[campus map](https://www.swarthmore.edu/visit-swarthmore/campus-map)]. It is the first point of contact for all requests, incidents, and problems related to technology services provided by Swarthmore College. Support is provided via the [ITS Service Portal](https://support.swarthmore.edu), phone, zoom, and in-person appointments. During the school year, after hours and weekend support is provided by the student IT Consultant in McCabe Library. The Help Desk offers extended support options to meet the varying needs of the college community. 

**Important Note:** Hours, operations, and coverage are modified during Semester Breaks and College observed Holidays. Hours and operations may also vary prior to the start of the Fall/Spring semesters to allow our staff to prepare for the return of students. Visit [The Dash](https://dash.swarthmore.edu/) for current hours. 

**The hours listed below are during the semester when classes are in session**. 

## ITS Service Portal

Visit the ITS Service Portal ([support.swarthmore.edu](http://support.swarthmore.edu)) to quickly find answers to questions and solutions to common issues. Enter a ticket for support or to request a service. See information about upcoming maintenance, service outages, and important announcements. The portal is interactive and available 24/7/365 days a year. It offers how-to guides and FAQs. 

## Phone Support

Phone support is available at x8513 or 610-328-8513. If no one answers, please leave a message. A voicemail message will create a support ticket for follow-up. When leaving a voicemail message, please provide your name, Swarthmore username, a return phone number, and an alternate email address if you are unable to access your Swarthmore account or email. 

## In-Person & Remote Support

### Clothier Hall Help Desk Appointments and Drop-ins

The ITS Help Desk provides limited drop-in support and recommends making an appointment to ensure that someone is available to help you. To schedule in-person support or computer drop-off, enter a [ticket](https://support.swarthmore.edu/support/tickets/new) and then [select a time slot](https://calendar.app.google/fKpCdqzm53k7iPm58):

Visit [The Dash](https://dash.swarthmore.edu/) for current hours. 

  * Monday-Thursday 9:30 am-3:30 pm
  * Friday 9:30 am-12:30 pm



### Remote Support Hours (phone, portal, zoom)

**What if I can’t visit the Help Desk or need help after hours?**

The ITS Help Desk offers remote support via phone, Zoom, and the ITS Service Portal. Calls made via Zoom Phone can be elevated to a Zoom Meeting. Virtual Zoom support can also be arranged by entering a ticket through the ITS Service Portal ([support.swarthmore.edu](http://support.swarthmore.edu)). 

#### Extended Hours for Remote Support:

Visit [The Dash](https://dash.swarthmore.edu/) for current hours.

  * Friday 8 am-8 pm (when classes are in session)
  * Saturday Closed
  * Sunday 4 pm-10 pm (when classes are in session)

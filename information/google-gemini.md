## Description

Gemini is Google’s Artificial Intelligence (AI) tool. The service is available as a web chatbot or mobile app. 

## Privacy

When logged in with your Swarthmore Google account, Google will not use your information to train their models. If you use Gemini with a personal account, Google may use your data for training purposes. 

## Access

The service is available as a chatbot at [gemini.google.com](http://gemini.google.com). 

There are Gemini apps for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-gemini/id6477489228) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.bard&hl=en_US). 

## Data Retention

Gemini Chats are saved by Google for 3 months and then deleted. There is an option to create a temporary chat that is not retained in the chat history. 

To preserve a chat longer than 3 months, copy and paste the content into a Google Doc or print the page to a PDF file. 

Individual chat responses can also be exported to Gmail or a Google Doc. This will only include a single chat response, not the entire conversation. Look for the “share” icon at the bottom of a response.

## Display Adapters

Display Adapters are necessary if you intend on connecting your laptop to a projector or additional display. We have various HDMI adaptors available for your PC or Mac device including: USB-C, Full and Mini Display Port, and Lightning to HDMI for your iPad or iPhone.

### iClickers*

  * Each student uses a "clicker", a portable, handheld device that allows students to vote by "clicking" on the appropriate button for their choice.
  * Each instructor uses a receiver that collects votes sent by students' clickers. Light and portable, the receiver is powered through any computer's USB port.
  * Instructors present a question and enable polling.
  * Each student responds by "clicking" the appropriate button for their choice.
  * A wireless signal is sent immediately from each responding clicker to the receiver.
  * The receiver, through iClicker's easy-to-use software, logs and stores the data of each individual student.
  * The instructor can then display voting results in a graph, to the audience.
  * The results are also available for later analysis, grading, and exporting to any grade book software or course management system.

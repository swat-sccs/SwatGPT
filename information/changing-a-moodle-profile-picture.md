# Changing a Moodle profile picture

To change your Moodle profile picture: 

  * Log into Moodle
  * Click your initials or thumbnail picture in the top right of the screen
  * Click **Profile → Edit Profile** (in the **User Details** section)
  * Scroll down to the **User Picture** area. 
  * Drag your desired image file into the **New Picture **box.  The file should be a .jpg or .png file format and will be cropped and resized to a 100x100 pixel square automatically.

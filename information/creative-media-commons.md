# Creative Media Commons

[The Creative Media Commons](https://www.swarthmore.edu/language-center-and-creative-media-commons/creative-media-commons) in Martin Hall is an open technology facility for media production: video editing, photo editing, audio mixing, graphic design, and animation. Scanning and printing are also available.

# Grade Essay Questions Within a Moodle Quiz

1. Click the on the name of the quiz on the main course page
  2. Click the **Results** tab at the top
  3. Select **Manual grading **(from the dropdown menu)
  4. On the next page, click the **Grade all** link in the row of the question that you would like to grade
  5. Under the **Options** section, next to **Order attempts by** select **By student last name** or **By student first name**
  6. Click the **Change Options** button
  7. Read responses and enter a grade (mark) for each one: 
     1. Student responses will appear in the top area in the essay answer section
     2. You can leave optional feedback for your students in the comments section. You will need to have set up your [Quiz Settings](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Creating_a_timed_quiz_or_exam_in_Moodle "Creating a timed quiz or exam in Moodle") to allow your students to view this feedback after the Quiz is closed.
     3. Enter your grades in the points box. Note that Moodle will provide the total number of possible points as a reference for you.
  8. When you have entered all the grades and optional feedback for the selected question on the page, scroll down and click the **Save and go to next page** button. This will take you to the next student.
  9. When you are done grading this question, scroll to the top of the page and click the **Back to the list of questions** link.

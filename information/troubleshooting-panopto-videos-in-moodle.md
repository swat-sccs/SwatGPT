# Troubleshooting Panopto Videos in Moodle

If you are having trouble viewing Panopto videos in Moodle, you may need to adjust your browser settings. Common symptoms are a black video window or a login window that pops up and immediately disappears. 

Visit the Panopto help link below to learn more: 

[How Do I Enable Third Party Cookies in Supported Browsers?](https://support.panopto.com/s/article/How-to-Enable-Third-Party-Cookies-in-Supported-Browsers)

# Searching for Courses

Some people are enrolled in many courses and it can be hard to find a specific Moodle course in the Course Overview listing. In this case, it may be faster to search for a course. 

On the Moodle home page, look for the "Browse all courses" link. 

[!["Browse all courses" link under the heading "All Courses" in a learning management system interface](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/5/5d/browse_courses.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:browse_courses.png)

Click the link and enter text to search for. A best bet is to search for the department, course number, and semester code. For example "BIOL001 F26". The search will also work with course names but they are sometimes abbreviated.

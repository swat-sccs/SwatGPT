## About

BeeLine Reader’s is a browser-based software suite that helps you read more quickly and easily by applying a line-wrapping color gradient to text. This technique improves visual tracking and focus to enable faster and easier reading. To learn more about BeeLine Reader, visit their [BeeLine Reader website](https://www.beelinereader.com/) or see features in [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2016/05/a-better-way-to-read/482127/) and on [NPR](https://www.wnyc.org/story/eureka-moment-makes-reading-easier/). 

BeeLine will work with macOS, Windows, and Linux web browsers. 

Swarthmore has a license agreement that gives all students, faculty, and staff access.

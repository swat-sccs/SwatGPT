# View Panopto Quiz Results

It is possible to insert quizzes within a Panopto video. This page explains how to see the results of the quiz. 

  1. Login to Panopto at [panopto.swarthmore.edu](http://swarthmore.hosted.panopto.com).
  2. **Hover** over the video with the embedded quiz (should display menu options).
  3. Click on **Settings.**[![Panopto video titled 'Taking care of new pets' with the Settings button highlighted](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/d/d0/View_Panopto_Quiz_Results_1778185904090.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:View_Panopto_Quiz_Results_1778185904090.png)
  4. Click on the **Quiz Results** tab.[![Quiz Results Summary for sections Taking Care of a Puppy and Taking Care of a Kitten with detailed results by question and user performance](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/9/92/View_Panopto_Quiz_Results_1778185948544.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:View_Panopto_Quiz_Results_1778185948544.png)
  5. **Review** results summary or **download** a copy. The downloaded file is in CSV format.[![Panopto Quiz Results Summary showing a quiz named Google Sheets Quiz with a Download link highlighted in blue](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/1/19/View_Panopto_Quiz_Results_1778185977344.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:View_Panopto_Quiz_Results_1778185977344.png)



Read more about [Panopto Quiz Results](https://support.panopto.com/s/article/How-to-Add-a-Quiz-to-a-Video#hTargetInstance2) from Panopto's support documentation.

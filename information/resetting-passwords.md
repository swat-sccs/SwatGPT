### Faculty & Staff

To set or change your Swarthmore password, please visit [password.swarthmore.edu](https://password.swarthmore.edu). For step-by-step help visit our [password change instructions](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Resetting_Email_and_Network_Passwords "Resetting Email and Network Passwords"). For help choosing a password visit our [password tips page](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Password_Tips "Password Tips"). 

All faculty and staff at Swarthmore College are assigned a user name and employee ID number. With these two pieces of information, you will be able to set a password at [password.swarthmore.edu](http://password.swarthmore.edu) using our [password reset instructions](https://kb.swarthmore.edu:8443/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=34242894). Your password will expire every 180 days and will need to be reset. 

The department for which you work will request account creation through either the Provost Office (for faculty) or the HR Office (for staff). Once the account is created, your hiring manager will contact you with instructions. New faculty and staff can request an account through their new supervisor prior to arrival. 

[Resetting Passwords](https://kb.swarthmore.edu:8443/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=34242894)

Your username and password can be used to access various campus resources, such as: 

  * The campus wireless network **eduroam** (on your personal computer, laptop or mobile device)
  * Email
  * [mySwarthmore](https://myswat.swarthmore.edu/)
  * Log onto campus computers



### Change your Banner Password

_**Note:** only applicable to authorized users in the administrative functional areas_

  1. Go to [Administrative Tools](https://apex.swarthmore.edu/prod/apex/f?p=181)
     * Enter your Email Username.
     * Enter your Email Password.
  2. Enter the new Banner password you would like.
  3. Click the Submit button to change the password.



### Students

To set or change your Swarthmore password, please visit [password.swarthmore.edu](https://password.swarthmore.edu). For step-by-step help visit our [password change instructions](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Resetting_Email_and_Network_Passwords "Resetting Email and Network Passwords"). For help choosing a password visit our [password tips page](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Password_Tips "Password Tips"). 

All students attending class at Swarthmore College are assigned a username and a student ID number. With these two pieces of information, you will set your password at [password.swarthmore.edu](https://password.swarthmore.edu) using our [password reset instructions](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Resetting_Email_and_Network_Passwords "Resetting Email and Network Passwords"). Your password will expire every 180 days and will need to be reset. Accounts are created for new students when they enroll at Swarthmore. Accounts are created for visiting students when they enroll in a class. 

If you have not received a username and ID number, please contact the **Registrar's Office**(registrar@swarthmore.edu). 

Your username and password can be used to access various campus resources, such as: 

  * The campus wireless network **eduroam** (on your personal computer, laptop, or mobile device)
  * Email
  * [mySwarthmore](https://myswat.swarthmore.edu/)
  * Log onto campus computers
  * Moodle (access may take up to 24 hours and may also require class registration)



### Guest Accounts (wireless access only)

College visitors who do not have College IDs may use the wireless network called **SwatGuest**. Guest accounts may be attained by following the [self-service instructions](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Self-Service_Guest_Wireless "Self-Service Guest Wireless").

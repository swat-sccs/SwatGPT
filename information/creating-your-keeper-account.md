# Creating Your Keeper Account

If you would like to take advantage of a Keeper password manager account to keep all of your work passwords secure and help create unique, strong passwords for all of your accounts - follow the simple process below! 

On a laptop or desktop computer that is assigned to you/you own - not another person’s computer or a public computer (a computer lab or library computer): 

  1. Open your preferred web browser and go to [_keepersecurity.com/vault_](https://keepersecurity.com/vault)
  2. Choose “**Create an account** ”
  3. Enter your full Swarthmore email address, i.e. test1@swarthmore.edu
  4. Choose “**Next** ”
  5. You will be redirected to the usual Swarthmore login page
  6. Enter your username and password and choose “**Login** ”
  7. Authenticate with DUO
  8. You will be redirected to your new Keeper vault



A pop-up will help walk you through setting up your account, including importing passwords. You can also check out our page on [importing passwords into Keeper with a CSV file](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Importing_Your_Passwords_Into_Keeper "Importing Your Passwords Into Keeper").

# Adding Zoom Meetings to Google Calendar

You can add meetings you create in the Zoom web interface to your Google Calendar and invite guests. 

  1. Go to [swarthmore.zoom.us](http://swarthmore.zoom.us)
  2. Log in with your Swarthmore credentials
  3. Click **Schedule a Meeting** (on the top right)
  4. Configure the meeting settings
  5. Click **Save**
  6. Add to **Google Calendar**  
[![Zoom meeting details page showing meeting topic, time, and options to add the meeting to Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, or Yahoo Calendar](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/7/79/Adding_Zoom_Meetings_to_Google_Calendar_1779389085327.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:Adding_Zoom_Meetings_to_Google_Calendar_1779389085327.png)
  7. Choose your Swarthmore account to sign in  
[![Google sign-in prompt asking to choose an account to continue to Zoom](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/5/5e/ZoomCooseAccount.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:ZoomCooseAccount.png)  

  8. Allow Zoom to access your Swarthmore Google account  
[![Permissions window asking to allow Zoom to access the Google Account with options to Cancel or Allow](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/6/68/ZoomAllowGoogle.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:ZoomAllowGoogle.png)
  9. Add Guests to the calendar invite (optional)
  10. Click **Save**



This will send a calendar invite with the Zoom meeting details included.

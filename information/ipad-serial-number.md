# iPad Serial Number

Your iPad Serial number can be found in the Settings App. 

  1. Tap on the **Settings app** [![Settings app icon with gears on a gray background](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/images/7/7b/iOS_Settings_Icon.png)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/File:iOS_Settings_Icon.png)
  2. Tap **General**
  3. Choose **About**
  4. Look under **Serial Number**

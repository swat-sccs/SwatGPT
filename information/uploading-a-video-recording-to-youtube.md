# Uploading a Video Recording to YouTube

Record your video. It is easiest to use the YouTube app on a phone. If you don't have a phone, [the Creative Media Commons](https://www.swarthmore.edu/language-center-and-creative-media-commons/creative-media-commons) has cameras available for checkout.  
  
Upload the video to YouTube ([instructions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407)). Everyone at Swarthmore has a Google account that you can use to log into YouTube. For Privacy Settings, we recommend using "Unlisted", which means the video won't show up on your video page or appear in searches, but anyone with the URL of the video will be able to watch it. 

Publish the video. 

Copy the URL of the video and share as needed.

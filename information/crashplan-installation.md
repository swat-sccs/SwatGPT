## Swarthmore College's Backup Solution - CrashPlan

## Step 1: CrashPlan Installation

You may already have CrashPlan installed. If so, you can skip to **Step 2**. 

Download the appropriate version of the CrashPlan software below. The installation process may take up to 10 minutes. 

  * [CrashPlan for Windows](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xU_KxZUdH-TDlAX2BENnoSHfv3YfeUfQ/view?usp=drive_link)
  * [CrashPlan for MacOS](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Xw0RYkwixI_-MvHdu_SBNdAb4Qxja4xa/view?usp=drive_link)
  * [CrashPlan for Linux](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1NAm4lbxW4IxGAWc6uabPbjLTnmD7uAuq/view?usp=drive_link) ([Linux installation Instructions](https://support.crashplan.com/hc/en-us/articles/9054858869773-Install-the-CrashPlan-app))



To sign in to CrashPlan: the computer's primary user will need to log in with their Swarthmore network credentials (**with **the @[swarthmore.edu](http://swarthmore.edu) at the end). 

The CrashPlan server address is: [**clients.us2.crashplan.com**](http://console.us2.crashplan.com/)

After installation, CrashPlan may require an update for you to sign in again. 

## Step 2: Launch and Sign into CrashPlan

Once CrashPlan is installed, open the CrashPlan application. 

How to locate: 

**Windows** : Click the start button and type “Crash” in the search. Then click on the CrashPlan icon. 

**macOS** : Click on Applications and locate CrashPlan. Or search for "CrashPlan" in Spotlight (magnifying glass icon) in the upper right corner. 

To sign in to CrashPlan**: ** the computer's primary user will need to log in with their Swarthmore network credentials (**with** the @swarthmore.edu at the end). 

The CrashPlan server address is: [**clients.us2.crashplan.com**](http://console.us2.crashplan.com/)

After the initial sign-in, the setup will prompt your web browser to launch and require your Swarthmore sign in, (**without** the @[swarthmore.edu](http://swarthmore.edu) at the end) 

Once logged in, click on **Start Backup.**

## Step 3: Confirmation

Please get in touch with Support Services after installation so we can verify your backup (information is located at the bottom). Please save any critical files onto your Swarthmore Google Drive and/or an external drive until we confirm that your CrashPlan backup is running smoothly.

# Recommended Computers

ITS recommends having an Apple or Dell computer that meets the minimum specifications listed below.  Follow the "Visit" links below each heading for education discounts.  For departmental purchases with Swarthmore funds, order through ITS. 

  


## Minimum Hardware Requirements

### Apple

  * Recommended operating system: Mac OS 14 or Higher
  * For education, discounts visit [Apple Computer](https://store.apple.com/us-hed)

=== **iMac Desktop** ===  | === **MacBook Laptop** ===   
---|---  
Version of macOS no older than two years  | Version of macOS no older than two years   
M Series Processor  | M Series Processor   
16GB RAM  | 16GB RAM   
256GB SSD  | 256GB SSD   
24" Widescreen display  | 13" Widescreen display   
  
### Dell

  * Recommended operating system: Windows 11
  * For education, discounts visit [Dell Computer](http://dell.com/Swarthmore)

=== **Desktop** ===  | === **Laptop** ===   
---|---  
Windows 11  | Windows 11   
Intel i7 Processor or higher  | Intel i7 Processor or higher   
16GB RAM  | 16GB RAM   
256 GB SSD  | 256 GB SSD   
24" Widescreen display  | 13" Widescreen display

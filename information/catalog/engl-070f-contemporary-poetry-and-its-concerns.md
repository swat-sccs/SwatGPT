# ENGL 070F. Contemporary Poetry and Its Concerns

This workshop examines how contemporary poets explore present-day topics while navigating their own subjectivity, how their works are in conversation with cultural trends and currents, and how these socio-political themes intersect and overlap. Students will learn to honor their own contemporary obsessions and perspectives to create authentic and meaningful poems.  _Though this workshop is open to all levels, it is recommended for students who have some poetry-writing experience_.

# MATH 035. Several-Variable Calculus with Theory

This course includes the topics found in [MATH 034](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110608) but covered in a more theoretical, abstract, and rigorous manner. It is intended for students with exceptionally strong mathematical skills. Students may take only one of [MATH 033](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110607), [MATH 034](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110608), or MATH 035 for credit.  
Prerequisite: Both (1) A grade of C- or better in, or placement out of, [MATH 025](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110601) and (2) one of the following: a grade of C- or better in [MATH 028](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110604), a grade of B or better in [MATH 027](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110603) or placement out of [MATH 027](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110603) together with successful completion of Part IV of the [Calculus Placement Exam](https://www.swarthmore.edu/mathematics-statistics/placement-information).  
Natural sciences and engineering.

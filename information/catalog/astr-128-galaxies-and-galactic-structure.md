# ASTR 128. Galaxies and Galactic Structure

Study of our own galaxy and other galaxies, including galaxy morphology; observational properties of galaxies; kinematics: stellar motions, galaxy rotation, spiral density waves, and instabilities; galaxy and star formation; starburst galaxies; quasars and active galaxies; galaxy clusters  
and interactions; and large-scale structure of the universe.  
Prerequisite: [ASTR 016](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109882)   
Natural sciences and engineering.  
1 credit.  
Spring 2028 Zanger.

# ENVS 037A. Attachment: Policies and Issues of Fresh Water Resources in China/Taiwan

(Cross-listed as [CHIN 087A](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=111269) and POLS 087A)  
This is an attachment to [ENVS 037](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112133) (. Students who complete the course have the option of adding a 0.5 credit field work component. Field work will be conducted in China under the supervision of Professors Nackenoff and Kong, and will include specific Chinese language training in the vocabulary used in the field of environmental studies.

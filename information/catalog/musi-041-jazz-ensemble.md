# MUSI 041. Jazz Ensemble

The Jazz Ensemble rehearses Tuesday evenings in Lang Concert Hall. Additional sectional and jazz combo rehearsals will be scheduled as necessary throughout the semester. There are no auditions to register. Background in jazz is helpful, not not mandatory.  
Repeatable course.  
0.0 or 0.5 credit.

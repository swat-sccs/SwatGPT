# CHIN 087A. Attachment: Policies and Issues of Fresh Water Resources in China/Taiwan

(Cross-listed as [ENVS 037A](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112134))  
This is an attachment to [CHIN 087](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=111268). Students who complete the course have the option of adding a 0.5 credit field work component. Field work will be conducted in China under the supervision of Professor Nackenoff and another faculty member, and will include specific Chinese language training in vocabulary used in the field of environmental studies.

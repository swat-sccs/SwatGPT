# PHYS 111. Analytical Dynamics

Intermediate classical mechanics. Motion of a particle in one, two, and three dimensions; Kepler's laws and planetary motion; phase space; oscillatory motion; Lagrange equations and variational principles; systems of particles; collisions and cross sections; motion of a rigid body; Euler's equations; rotating frames of reference; small oscillations; normal modes; and wave phenomena. Offered every Spring.  
Prerequisite: [PHYS 008](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110763), and one of [PHYS 007](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110762), [ENGR 006](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110227), or strong performance in [PHYS 003](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110758).  
Natural sciences and engineering.

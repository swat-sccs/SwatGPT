# STAT 051. Probability

Introduction to the mathematical theory of probability. Topics include sample spaces and events, conditional probability and Bayes' theorem, univariate probability and density functions, expectation and variance, moment generating functions, Binomial, Negative Binomial, Poisson, Normal, t, Gamma and Beta distributions, joint, marginal and conditional distributions, independence, transformations, the multivariate Normal distribution, the law of large numbers and the central limit theorem.  
Prerequisite: A grade of C- or better in one of [[MATH 033](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110607)](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course.php?catoid=29&coid=86271#tt2856), [[MATH 034](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110608)](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course.php?catoid=29&coid=86271#tt9255), or [[MATH 035](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110609)](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course.php?catoid=29&coid=86271#tt72); or concurrent enrollment in [[MATH 034](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110608) ](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course.php?catoid=29&coid=86271#tt9980)or [[MATH 035](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110609) ](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course.php?catoid=29&coid=86271#tt2346)and a grade of C- or better in [[MATH 027](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110603)](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course.php?catoid=29&coid=86271#tt1947) or [MATH 028](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110604).

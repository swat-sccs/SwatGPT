# CHEM 199. Senior Thesis Workshop

CHEM 199 is an interactive course where students completing a senior research thesis discuss their work. Strategies for effective writing and oral presentations will be emphasized. Course is required of all students enrolled in sections of [CHEM 096](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109960) or [CHEM 180](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109961) . On Colloquium weeks, this course will also include Colloquium Journal Club attended by all Majors and Minors within the Department.

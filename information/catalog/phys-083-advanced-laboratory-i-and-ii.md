# PHYS 083. Advanced Laboratory I and II

[PHYS 081](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110784)   
This course is designed to fulfill the physics major advanced laboratory requirement for students who have already had sufficient experience with electronics ([ENGR 072](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110255) or [ENGR 021](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112597), [ENGR 021](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112597) and [ENGR 029](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=111665)). Students will perform experiments chosen from a wide array of physics topics. Students must obtain permission from the instructor to take this course rather than [PHYS 081](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110784) and [PHYS 082](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110785).

# ENGL 010. Monsters, Marvels, and Mysteries: Beowulf to Paradise Lost

The first thousand years of English Literature with an emphasis on monsters like Grendel and Satan, marvels like a talking tree and a boy actor playing a woman pretending to be a man, and mysteries like the moth that devours words and a green knight who offers a hero the chance to chop off his head. Some modern retellings such as Gardner's  _Grendel_ and Stoppard's  _Rosencrantz and Guildenstern Are Dead_ will be included. Major authors include Chaucer, Shakespeare, and Milton.

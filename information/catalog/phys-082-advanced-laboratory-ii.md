# PHYS 082. Advanced Laboratory II

This is the second of a two-semester sequence designed to fulfill the physics major advanced laboratory requirement. Students will perform projects in digital electronics. They will also perform experiments chosen from among the areas of thermal and statistical physics, solid state, atomic, plasma, nuclear, biophysics, condensed matter physics, and advanced optics. When both [PHYS 081](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110784) and PHYS 082 are taken, students will receive credit for having completed a writing (W) course.

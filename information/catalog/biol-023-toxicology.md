# BIOL 023. Toxicology

This course offers an introduction to the fundamental principles of toxicology, with special emphasis on neurotoxicology. Students will learn about the molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying toxic responses and gain insights into modern approaches for chemical safety assessment in toxicology. In the laboratory, students will learn safe working practices with hazardous materials and study the effects of various agents on the nervous system.

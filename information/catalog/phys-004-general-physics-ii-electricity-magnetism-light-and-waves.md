# PHYS 004. General Physics II: Electricity, Magnetism, Light, and Waves

Topics include wave phenomena, geometrical and physical optics, electricity and magnetism, and direct and alternating current circuits. Possible additional topics may be added.  
Prerequisite: [PHYS 003](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110758) or the permission of the instructor.  
Corequisite: [MATH 025](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110601) or equivalent.  
Natural sciences and engineering practicum.  
Lab required.

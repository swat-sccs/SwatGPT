# HIST 002A. War, Religion, Revolution: Europe, 1096-1789

Centered on the history of religious war and toleration in Early Modern Europe (15th to 18th centuries), the course begins with the Crusades, and ends with the French and American Revolutions.  
MDST and GLBL-paired. Concentrations: Culture and Identity; Domination and Resistance; Empires and Nations.  
Social sciences.  
1 credit.  
Eligible for MDST, GLBL-paired. Concentrations: Culture and Identity; Domination and Resistance; Empires and Nations

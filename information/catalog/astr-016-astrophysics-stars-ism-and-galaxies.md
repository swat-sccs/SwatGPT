# ASTR 016. Astrophysics: Stars, ISM, and Galaxies

This is a one-semester calculus- and physics-based introduction to astrophysics as applied to stars, the interstellar medium, and galaxies.   
Prerequisite: Prerequisites: [PHYS 003](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110758) and [PHYS 004](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110759); [MATH 025](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110601) or equivalent by placement  
Natural sciences and engineering.  
Evening labs and observing sessions required.  
1 credit.

# DANC 023. Contemporary Performance

This course interrogates issues surrounding twenty-first-century movement-based performance including cultural hybridity and the relationship between movement and text. Using aesthetic theory and methodologies developed by performance studies and dance studies, we will ask what gets performed, where, and why.  
Humanities.  
1 credit.

# ARTT 035A. Painting III: Self-Direction Practice in Painting

Based on the discoveries made in the assignment-based painting I and II courses, students will continue to deﬁne and express their personal intentions in their paintings and begin their fully independent studio practice. Particular importance is placed on the ability to develop and maintain an independent studio practice with the goal of producing a body of self-directed work. Students are required to express their intent visually in their work and orally in critiques.  
Prerequisite: Painting I and II or instructor approval.

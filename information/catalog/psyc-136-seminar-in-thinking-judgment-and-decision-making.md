# PSYC 136. Seminar in Thinking, Judgment, and Decision Making

The seminar considers in depth several of the topics introduced in [PSYC 036](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110872).  
This course may not be taken as pass/fail.   
Prerequisite: [PSYC 001](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110859) and [ PSYC 036. Clinical Neuroscience](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110872) or permission of the instructor.

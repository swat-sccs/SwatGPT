# PEAC 034. Organizing for Social Change

Organizing for Social Change focuses on the strategic work of developing effective nonviolent social change campaigns while keeping our eye on potentials for constructive conflict. Students will learn from activists, organizers, and scholars as they design and carry out campaigns, addressing social needs and effecting policy changes.  
  
1 credit.  
Eligible for PEAC, ESCH  
Spring 2028 Smithey.

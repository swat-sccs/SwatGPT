# ENGR 025. Principles of Computer Architecture

(Cross-listed as [CPSC 052](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110025))  
This course covers the physical and logical design of a computer. Students will learn about and apply principles of computer architecture concepts through a combination of interactive textbook problems and lectures. Labs will explore the basics of assembly and Verilog programming, culminating in prototyping a single cycle processor from scratch. Students will complete a final project in assembly and/or Verilog.

  
Prerequisite: [ENGR 021](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112597) or [CPSC 031](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110017).   
Natural sciences and engineering practicum.

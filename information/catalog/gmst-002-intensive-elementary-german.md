# GMST 002. Intensive Elementary German

This class is the continuation of [GMST 001](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110317) and also for students who placed into the second semester.

Second semester German continues to develop core language skills (listening, speaking, reading, and writing). Regular engagement with authentic texts, videos, and music from Germany, Switzerland, and Austria further enhances cultural competency.

This 1 credit class meets on Tuesdays and Thursdays with a one-hour tutorial on an additional day.

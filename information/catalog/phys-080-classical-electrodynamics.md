# PHYS 080. Classical Electrodynamics

Junior-level treatment of classical electrodynamics: electric and magnetic forces, fields, and potentials in vacuum and materials, relativistic origin of magnetic fields, electromagnetic induction, conservation laws, Maxwell's Equations, and electromagnetic waves. Uses multivariable calculus and differential equations (differential equations taught as part of course) to describe these physical phenomena.

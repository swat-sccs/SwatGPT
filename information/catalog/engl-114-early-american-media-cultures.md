# ENGL 114. Early American Media Cultures

This course borrows some of the methods of new media studies to look anew at the multimedia culture of the 18th- and 19th-century United States. We will study newspapers, maps, wampum, photographs, songbooks, advertisements, and counterfeit money, alongside literary texts that thematize this rich media culture.  
18th/19th c.  
Humanities.  
2 credits.

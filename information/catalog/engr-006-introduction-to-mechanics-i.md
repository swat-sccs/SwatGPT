# ENGR 006. Introduction to Mechanics I

This is the first of two courses that cover fundamental areas of engineering statics including force equilibrium, deformable bodies, stress-strain relations, flexure, and torsion. Laboratory work includes a MATLAB workshop, experiments on deformable bodies, and a truss-bridge team design competition.  
Prerequisite: [MATH 015](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110598) is required. [PHYS 003](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110758) is strongly recommended.  
Natural sciences and engineering practicum.  
Lab required.  
1 credit.

# BLST 067B. James Baldwin’s B-sides

Cross-listed with [ENGL 067B](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=31&coid=105609).  
"James Baldwin's B-sides" Whereas ENGL 67 ("James Baldwin's Civil Rights") focuses closely on the legendary Black author's major work of the 1960s, this complementary course explores Baldwin's other work-fiction, drama, essays (both collected and uncollected), interviews, conversations, and films.This reading-intensive course will continue to examine some key themes in Baldwin's work: Blackness, race, gender, sexuality, and history. Appropriate for students who have not yet taken ENGL 67, as well as those who have.

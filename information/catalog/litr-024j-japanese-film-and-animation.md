# LITR 024J. Japanese Film and Animation

(Cross-listed as [JPNS 024](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110474), [FMST 057](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=111525))  
This course offers a historical and thematic introduction to Japanese cinema, one of the world's great film traditions. Our discussions will center on the historical context of Japanese film, including how films address issues of modernity, gender, class conflict, and national identity. Through our readings, discussion, and writing, we will explore various approaches to film analysis, with the goal of developing a deeper understanding of formal and thematic issues. A separate unit will consider the development of Japanese animation (anime) and its characteristics. Screenings will include films by Ozu, Kurosawa, Koreeda, Tanaka, Miyazaki, and Shinkai.

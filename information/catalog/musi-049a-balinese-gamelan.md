# MUSI 049A. Balinese Gamelan

Performance of traditional and modern compositions for Balinese Gamelan (Indonesian percussion orchestra). Students will learn to play without musical notation. No prior experience in Western or non-Western music is required. The course is open to all students. Rehearsals are Monday afternoons in the Presser Room of Lang Music Building, with additional individual instruction available Monday and Wednesday afternoons.  
Repeatable course.  
0.5 or 0.0 credit.

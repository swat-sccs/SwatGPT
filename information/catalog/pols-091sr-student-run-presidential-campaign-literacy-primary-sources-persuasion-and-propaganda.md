# POLS 091SR. Student Run: Presidential Campaign Literacy: Primary sources, Persuasion, and Propaganda

Presidential Campaign Literacy: Primary sources, Persuasion, and Propaganda is a course intended to develop media literacy skills through examination of presidential election communications, publications, and current events. The course is not only student-led in the sense that it is organized by a Swarthmore student, but also in the sense that the students in the class will often be in control of their own learning, researching and engaging with media examples from current and recent election cycles that they find themselves. Students will read assigned literature on topics spanning fake news, media literacy skills, propaganda development and identification, and specific forms of campaign (and occasionally outside) communications that occur during presidential election cycles. The course meeting times will be full of reflections on the literature, current event discussions, practice and application of old and new literacy skills.  
Social Sciences.

# FREN 045D. Le monde francophone: Cinémas africains

This course is an introduction to the filmmakers and history of Francophone West African cinemas, including film, video, and new media. Students will study the history and culture of this region, be introduced to key film concepts, and develop their ability to do in-depth film analysis. Students must attend weekly screenings.   
Prerequisite: [FREN 015](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110303) or instructor permission.  
Humanities.  
1 credit.

# HIST 033. Communism and the Environment

This course focuses on the impact of ideology and politics on the environment in twentieth-century Russia. Readings include short stories, novels, monographs, articles, and documents.  
Social sciences.  
1 credit.  
Eligible for ENVS. Concentrations: Science, Medicine, and Environment

# CHIN 089A. Attachment: Tea in China: Cultural and Environmental Perspectives

This is an attachment to [CHIN 089](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=111272). Students who complete the course have the option of adding a 0.5 credit field work component. Field work will be conducted in China under the supervision of Professors Kong and Berkowitz, and will include specific Chinese language training in vocabulary used in the field of environmental studies.

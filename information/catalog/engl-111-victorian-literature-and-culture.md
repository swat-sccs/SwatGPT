# ENGL 111. Victorian Literature and Culture

This research-intensive seminar pays close and deep attention to a handful of major Victorian novels by authors like Anthony Trollope, George Eliot, Charles Dickens, Margaret Oliphant, and William Thackeray.  
18th/19th c.  
Humanities.  
2 credits.  
Eligible for INTP, GSST

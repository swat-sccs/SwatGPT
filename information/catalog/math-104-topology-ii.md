# MATH 104. Topology II

A continuation of the topics from Math 64, including an in-depth exploration of the fundamental group and covering spaces. Additional topics will be drawn from algebraic, differential, and/or geometric topology. Previous to Spring 2026, this was a 2-credit seminar.  
Alternate years.  
Prerequisite: A grade of B or better in [MATH 063](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110620), [MATH 064](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112678), and [MATH 067](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110621); or a grade of B or better in [MATH 063](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110620) and [MATH 064](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112678) and concurrent enrollment in [MATH 067](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110621).

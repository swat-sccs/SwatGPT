# PHIL 101. Moral Philosophy

This seminar focuses on one of the age-old questions in philosophy: what is the right thing to do? We start with an in-depth look at some of the major historical figures in moral philosophy from both western and eastern traditions. We then move into contemporary discussions of responsibility, practical reason, moral emotions, and moral skepticism.  
This seminar is intended for Seniors and Juniors, with priority given to honors students taking it as an honors preparation. Others should contact the instructor for permission to enroll.

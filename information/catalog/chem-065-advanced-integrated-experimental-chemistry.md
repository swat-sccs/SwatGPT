# CHEM 065. Advanced Integrated Experimental Chemistry

This course will consist of advanced experimental projects incorporating a range of chemistry subdisciplines.  
Prerequisite: C- or better in four semesters of chemistry, one of which must be 40 or higher. At least three of the four chemistry courses must have a lab.   
Natural sciences and engineering practicum.  
1 credit.  
Spring 2027. Staff.

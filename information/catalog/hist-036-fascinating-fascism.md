# HIST 036. Fascinating Fascism

This course explores the various manifestations of fascism as an ideological, cultural, and political movement in Europe from 1919 to 1945. Special attention will be paid to Spain, Italy, Germany, Romania, and England.  
Social sciences.  
1 credit.  
Eligible for GMST, PEAC, GLBL-Core

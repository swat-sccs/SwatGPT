# GMST 006. German Conversation

Through discussion of German films and presentations on student-selected topics, this class develops students' speaking skills. Content changes every semester and students can enroll in this class multiple times.  
Prerequisite: Completion of [GMST 001](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110317).   
0.5 credit.  
Spring 2027. Burri.  
Catalog chapter: [Modern Languages and Literatures: German Studies](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=32&poid=2569)

# MUSI 048. Individual Instruction

Please consult the [MUSI 048 guidelines and video](https://www.swarthmore.edu/music/private-lessons) on the Music Department website for information on auditions, subsidies, and other requirements of the private instruction program.  
Audition required. Repeatable course. Course is open to Swarthmore students only (not Tri-Co).   
Corequisite: Participation in an approved performance ensemble.  
0.5 credit.

Spring 2027. Milewski.

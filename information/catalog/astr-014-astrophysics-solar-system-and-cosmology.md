# ASTR 014. Astrophysics: Solar System and Cosmology

This course assumes no prior knowledge of astronomy, but knowledge of some basic physics as well as elementary calculus. It focuses on two major topics of current interest in astrophysics: (1) Solar System and planetary science and (2) cosmology, the large-scale study of the universe, its history and content.   
Prerequisite: Prerequisite: [MATH 015](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110598) and [PHYS 003](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110758) (or placement out). Interested students who have not met these prerequisites should consult with the instructor. This course should be accessible to some students who have completed [ASTR 001](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109880).

# ENGR 041. Thermofluid Mechanics

This course introduces macroscopic thermodynamics: first and second laws, properties of pure substances, and applications using system and control volume formulation. Also introduced is fluid mechanics: development of conservation theorems, hydrostatics, and the dynamics of one-dimensional fluid motion with and without friction.   
Prerequisite: C- or better in all of [ENGR 006](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110227), [ENGR 012](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110233), and [MATH 033](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110607), [MATH 034](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110608), or [MATH 035](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110609).  
Lab required.

# MATH 027. Linear Algebra

This course covers systems of linear equations, matrices, vector spaces, linear transformations, determinants, and eigenvalues. Applications to other disciplines are presented. This course is a step up from calculus: it includes more abstract reasoning, proofs, and structures. Students may take only one of MATH 027 and [MATH 028](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110604) for credit.  
Prerequisite: Either (1) a grade of C- or better in (or placement out of) [MATH 025](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110601); or (2) a grade of C- or better in [MATH 039](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110606).

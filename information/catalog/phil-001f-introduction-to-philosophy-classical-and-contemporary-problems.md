# PHIL 001F. Introduction to Philosophy: Classical and Contemporary Problems

Classical and current readings by Plato, Aristotle, Aquinas, Descartes, Kant, Mill, Russell, and Lewis '62, amoung others introduce the traditions of Western Philosophy. Topics may include: God and Evil, Knowledge and Belief, Life and Mind, Morality and Interests, Taste and Aesthetic Judgment, Personal and Bodily Identity.  
Humanities  
1 credit.

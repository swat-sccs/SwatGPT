# ENGL 098. Senior Thesis

Course majors may pursue a thesis for 1 (40-50 pages) or 2 (80-100 pages) credits. A proposal for the project must be submitted in April of the junior year. Before submitting this proposal, course majors must consult with a prospective faculty supervisor. This work does not replace [ENGL 099](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110211), required of every course major. Available only if a professor is available to supervise the project.  
1 - 2 credits.

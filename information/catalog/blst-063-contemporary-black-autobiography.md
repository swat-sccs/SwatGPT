# BLST 063. Contemporary Black Autobiography

(Cross-listed as [ENGL 063](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/search_advanced.php?cur_cat_oid=29&ecpage=1&cpage=1&ppage=1&pcpage=1&spage=1&tpage=1&search_database=Search&filter%5Bkeyword%5D=ENGL+063&filter%5Bexact_match%5D=1&filter%5B3%5D=1&filter%5B31%5D=1#/usr/local/webroot/acalog-legacy/shared/htdocs_gateway/ajax/preview_course.php))  
How does the Black subject become the source and site of intersectional theory? This course examines the complexities of Black self-presentation in relation to gender, sexuality, class, place, and history, with a particular focus on developments within the last decade, the era of Black Lives Matter.  
1 credit.

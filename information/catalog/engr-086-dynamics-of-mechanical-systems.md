# ENGR 086. Dynamics of Mechanical Systems

Rigid-body kinematics and dynamics in plane and three-dimensional motion using fixed as well as moving reference frames. The course will use both Newtonian (i.e. force-based) and Lagrangian (i.e., variational) methods to derive the (differential) equations of motion for mechanical systems. The resulting equations will be solved analytically when possible and numerically using MATLAB or Python when appropriate.

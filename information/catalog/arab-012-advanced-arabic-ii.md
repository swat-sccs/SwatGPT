# ARAB 012. Advanced Arabic II

This course is a continuation of [ARAB 011](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109829) and all previous course in the sequence. This course will begin with a quick review of advanced grammatical structures and vocabulary. Students will continue to encounter a wide range of authentic texts and audiovisual materials to enhance their competency in reading, writing, listening, and speaking, with a special emphasis on vocabulary building.  
Prerequisite: Successful completion of [ARAB 011](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109829) and permission of the instructor.

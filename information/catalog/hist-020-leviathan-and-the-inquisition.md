# HIST 020. Leviathan and the Inquisition

Centered on _Leviathan_ by Thomas Hobbes (1651), this course will trace a counter-history of Western political thought starting with the Ancients and ending in the present moment when Hobbes' famous mythical entity-at once machine, monster, and idol-continues to inspire paradigm shifts in the humanities and social sciences.  
Method/Theory. Concentrations: Domination and Resistance; Empires and Nations

# ECON 122. Financial Economics

This seminar analyzes the ways that firms finance their operations. It discusses the organization and regulation of financial markets and institutions. It examines theories explaining asset prices and returns, and it discusses the function and pricing of options and futures contracts.  
Prerequisite: [ECON 011](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110091), [ECON 031](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110096) or [ECON 035](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110099), and [MATH 025](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110601) or higher calculus.

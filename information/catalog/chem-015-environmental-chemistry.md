# CHEM 015. Environmental Chemistry

(Cross-listed as [ENVS 060](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=111680)) The course covers selected aspects of atmospheric chemistry, aquatic chemistry, and soil chemistry. There will be a specific focus on the environmentally important element cycles for C, N, O, P, and S in the absence and presence of current human activity. The chemistry of organic pollutants across the three zones will also be examined. The course content will involve a discussion of relevant current events.  
Prerequisite: C- or better in [CHEM 010](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109946) or [CHEM 011](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109947) ; or discretion of the instructor.  
Natural sciences and engineering practicum.  
One laboratory period weekly.  
1 credit.  
Eligible for ENVS, GLBL-core

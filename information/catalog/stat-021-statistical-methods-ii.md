# STAT 021. Statistical Methods II

This is a second course in applied statistics that extends methods taught in [STAT 011](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=111041). Topics include multiple linear regression, analysis of variance, and logistic regression.  
Prerequisite: Credit for AP Statistics; or a grade of C- or better in [STAT 011](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=111041) or [ECON 031](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110096); or a grade of B or better in [STAT 001](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=111040) with departmental approval.

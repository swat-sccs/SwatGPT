# ENGR 029. Embedded Systems

Connected systems that used embedded microcontrollers are becoming more and more pervasive, with applications in the car, home, and body. This course will explore how to design embedded systems. Topics will include biomedical signal acquisition and processing, numerical computation, and audio/video signal processing. This course includes a laboratory.  
Prerequisite: [ENGR 021](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112597), or permission of the instructor.  
Natural Science and engineering practicum.  
Lab required.  
1 credit.

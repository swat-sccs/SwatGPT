# ENGR 078. Communication Systems

This course expolres theory and design principles of analog and digital communication systems. Topics include frequency domain analysis of signals; signal transmission and filtering; random signals and noise; AM, PM, and FM signals; sampling and pulse modulation; digital signal transmission; Pulse Code Modulation; coding; and information theory. Applications to practical systems such as television and data communications are covered.  
Offered in the spring semester of alternate years.  
Prerequisite: [ENGR 012](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110233) with a grade of C- or better or permission of the instructor.  
Natural sciences and engineering practicum.  
Lab included.  
1 credit.

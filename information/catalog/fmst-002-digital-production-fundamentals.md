# FMST 002. Digital Production Fundamentals

This course introduces students to the expressive possibilities and rigors of the film medium while offering a sound technical foundation in digital production and post-production. We will explore documentary, experimental, and narrative approaches and also consider the opportunities and limitations-conceptual, practical and aesthetic- of exhibiting work through different venues and platforms. Emphasis will be on using the formal and conceptual palette introduced in the course to develop one's own artistic vision. Coursework includes short assignments, readings, discussions, screenings, and a final project.  
Prerequisite: [FMST 001](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110275) or Instructor Permission.

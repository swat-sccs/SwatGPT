# ENGL 001D. Writing Tutorial

Students currently enrolled or have completed an academic writing course, in consultation with the professor of these courses, may enroll in the tutorial. Students will set up an individual program to work with the professor on writing for the course or other courses.  
Humanities.  
0.5 credit.

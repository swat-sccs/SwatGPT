# MATH 057. Topics in Algebra

Course content varies each year, depending on student and faculty interest. Recent offerings have included coding theory, groups and representations, finite reflection groups, and advanced matrix theory. See also [MATH 077](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110625).  
Prerequisite: A grade of C or better in [MATH 027](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110603) or [MATH 028](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110604) , and any proof-based math class, such as Math 28, 35, 39, etc., or permission of the instructor.

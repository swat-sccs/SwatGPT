# MATH 065. Introduction to Geometry

The course content varies from year to year but is often either the differential geometry of curves and surfaces or the algebraic geometry of conics and cubics.  
Alternate years.  
Prerequisite: Credit for, or placement out of, [MATH 027](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110603) or [MATH 028](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110604) and one of [MATH 033](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110607), [MATH 034](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110608), or [MATH 035](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110609), with a grade of C- or better in at least one of these courses.

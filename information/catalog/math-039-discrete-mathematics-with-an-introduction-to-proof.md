# MATH 039. Discrete Mathematics with an Introduction to Proof

An introduction to noncontinuous mathematics. Topics will include mathematical induction and other methods of proof, basic set theory, bijections, counting, and graph theory. Additional topics may include algorithms, recurrence relations, probability, voting methods, and other topics at the discretion of the instructor. While it does not use any calculus, MATH 039 is a more sophisticated course than [MATH 015](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110598) or [MATH 025](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110601); thus success in a calculus course demonstrates the mathematical maturity needed for MATH 039. This course is an  _introduction_ to proof; thus, students who have taken two or more upper-level proof-based courses need the permission of the instructor to enroll in Math 039. Previously called Math 029.  
Prerequisite: Either (1) a grade of B or better in, or placement out of [MATH 015](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110598); or (2) credit for [MATH 025](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110601).

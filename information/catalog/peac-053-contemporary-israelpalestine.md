# PEAC 053. Contemporary Israel/Palestine

(Cross-listed as [ANTH 051](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112576))  
_This course examines the past, present, and future of Palestine/Israel, including the trajectory of the conflict, competing narratives, and movements aimed at peace and justice. We also emphasize the heterogeneity of Palestinian and Israeli societies, pursuing an ethnographic approach to the study of the region._  
Social Sciences.  
1 credit.  
Eligible for ISLM

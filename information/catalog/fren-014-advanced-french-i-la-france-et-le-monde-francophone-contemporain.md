# FREN 014. Advanced French I: La France et le monde francophone contemporain

This course gives students the opportunity to develop French language skills through explorations of culture and society in France and the Francophone World. Particular attention will be paid to oral communication, grammar review, and analytical skills in written French. [[FREN 003](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110301)](http://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course.php?catoid=7&coid=8158#tt8731) or placement required.  
This class is the first part of the two-part sequence: French 14. Advanced French I - French 15. Advanced French II.

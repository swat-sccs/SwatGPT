# GREK 012. Homer’s Iliad

This course examines the literary, historical, and linguistic significance of Homer's _Iliad_. Selections from the poem are read in Greek and the entire poem is read in translation.  
Arts and Humanities.  
Writing course.  
1 credit.  
Eligible for CPLT.  
Spring 2027. Lefkowitz.  
Spring 2028 Munson.

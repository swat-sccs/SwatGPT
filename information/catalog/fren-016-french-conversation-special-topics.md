# FREN 016. French Conversation: Special Topics

An intermediate to advanced-level conversation course concentrating on improving students' ability to speak French through the discovery of topics relevant to the contemporary Francophone world. May be taken twice with a different instructor, but may only count once to fulfill major/minor credit requirements.  
Prerequisite: For students previously enrolled in [FREN 014](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110302) or above.  
0.5 credit.

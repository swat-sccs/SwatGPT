# MUSI 052. Class Study of Guitar

Come, Shred, and Strum! This course focuses on the essential elements of guitar playing: melodic playing (guitar solos) and harmonic playing (chord strumming). Students will learn solo and ensemble pieces and rudimentary music theory; culminating in an ensemble performance. 

Prior experience is optional; guitars are provided for students by the college.  
0.5 credits.

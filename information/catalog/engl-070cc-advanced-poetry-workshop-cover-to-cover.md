# ENGL 070CC. Advanced Poetry Workshop: Cover to Cover

In this poetry workshop, we'll engage not only individual poems but whole titles, studying book-length poems, thematically- and formally- linked collections, hybrid texts, and other examples, to investigate the entire project of a poetry book. This course culminates in the creation of an original poetry chapbook. Limited to 12 students.

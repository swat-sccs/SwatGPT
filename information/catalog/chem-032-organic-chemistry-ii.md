# CHEM 032. Organic Chemistry II

A continuation of [CHEM 022](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109948) with emphasis on more advanced aspects of the chemistry of monofunctional and polyfunctional organic compounds, multistep methods of synthesis, and an introduction to bio-organic chemistry.  
Prerequisite: C- or better in [CHEM 022](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109948).  
Natural sciences and engineering practicum.  
One laboratory period weekly.  
1 credit.  
Fall 2026. Rablen.  
Fall 2027 Staff.

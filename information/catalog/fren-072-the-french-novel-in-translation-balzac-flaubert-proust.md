# FREN 072. The French Novel in Translation: Balzac, Flaubert, Proust

(Cross-listed as [LITR 072F](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110586))  
This course is designed to provide students with a broad knowledge of French literature, from before the Revolution to the present. Among the authors included on the syllabus are: Molière, Voltaire, Balzac, Baudelaire, Proust, Camus and Sartre. Students will read works in their entirety, discuss their significance in class, and listen to short lectures to situate the readings in a historical and cultural context. There is a 0.5 credit French Attachment for students reading in French ([FREN 072A](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=111784)).

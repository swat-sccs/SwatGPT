# HIST 065A. Issues and Debates in Modern Latin America

Cross listed with LALS 065  
Explores major problems and challenges Latin American nations have been confronting since the last third of the 19th century onward. Topics include the neocolonial condition of the region, nation and state builing process, urbanization, industrialization, popular and elite cultures, modernities in the periphery, and race, class and gender conflicts.   
Cross listed with LALS 065. Concentration: Culture and Identity.  
Social sciences.  
1 credit

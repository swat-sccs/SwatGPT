# ARAB 004. Intermediate Modern Standard Arabic II

This course is a continuation of [ARAB 003](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109827). Because the material covered in this course relies heavily on the previous course, students are expected to review and be familiar with the previous work in [ARAB 001](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109825), [ARAB 002](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109826) and [ARAB 003](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109827).  
Prerequisite: [ARAB 003](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109827) or equivalent or permission of the department.

# DANC 041. Ballet I

An introduction to the fundamentals of classical ballet vocabulary with a focus on anatomically correct alignment, movement quality, and musicality. If taken for academic credit, concert attendance, two short papers, and a vocabulary test are required.   
Graded CR/NC.  
0.5 credit or P.E.

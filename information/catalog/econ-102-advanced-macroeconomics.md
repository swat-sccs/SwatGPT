# ECON 102. Advanced Macroeconomics

Subjects covered include microfoundations of macroeconomics, growth theory, rational expectations, and New Classical and New Keynesian macroeconomics. Extensive problem solving, with an emphasis on the qualitative analysis of dynamic systems.  
Prerequisite: [ECON 011](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110091), [ECON 021](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110094), and multivariable calculus ([MATH 033](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110607), [MATH 034](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110608), or [MATH 035](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110609) or with permission of the instructor).  
Recommended: [MATH 043](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110610) or [MATH 044](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110611).

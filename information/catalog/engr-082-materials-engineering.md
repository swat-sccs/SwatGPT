# ENGR 082. Materials Engineering

We interact with myriad materials in our everyday lives, from plastic packaging, to metals in our laptops, to ceramic bowls. This course will teach you the fundamentals of materials science and engineering, covering mechanical, electronic, and optical properties of polymers, ceramics, metals, semiconductors, and more. We'll question what sustainability means in the context of materials production, consumption, and recycling. Class activities include materials characterization labs, structured debates, case studies, and a final project.  
Prerequisite: C- or better in [ENGR 006](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110227) AND [MATH 015](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110598) AND [PHYS 004](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110759) or [CHEM 010](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109946); or permission of instructor.  
Lab included.  
Spring 2027. Loh.

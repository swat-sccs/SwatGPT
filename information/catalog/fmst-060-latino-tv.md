# FMST 060. Latino TV

(Cross-listed as [LALS 060](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112926))  
This course uses television programs to trace the histories and experiences of Latiné migrants living in the United States. By analyzing television shows with a predominantly Latiné cast, we will study the historical, political, and economic processes that have led to these migrations, as well as the varying ways in which race/ethnicity, class, gender/sexuality, and citizenship affect Latiné migrants' lived experiences in the U.S.  
Eligible for LALS.  
Spring 2028 Almeida.

# ANCH 056. Pagans and Christians in the Roman Empire

This course considers the rise of Christianity and its encounters with the religious and political institutions of the Roman Empire. It examines Christianity in the second and third centuries of the Common Era and its relationship with Judaism, Hellenistic philosophies, state cults, and mystery religions and concentrates on the various pagan responses to Christianity from conversion to persecution. Ancient texts may include Apuleius, Lucian, Marcus Aurelius, Porphyry, Justin, Origen, Lactantius, Tertullian, and the _Acts of the Christian Martyrs_.  
[ANCH 044](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109799) (The Early Roman Empire) provides useful background.

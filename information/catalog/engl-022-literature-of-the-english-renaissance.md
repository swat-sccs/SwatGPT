# ENGL 022. Literature of the English Renaissance

This course will begin with More's _Utopia_ and end with selections from _Paradise Lost_ , paying particular attention to literature's political contexts, gender, genre, and the relation of women's writing to the male canon. Among the other writers included will be Wyatt, Surrey, Philip Sidney, Mary Herbert, Mary Wroth, Spenser, Elizabeth Cary, Jonson, Bacon, Donne, Herrick, George Herbert, and Marvell.  
Med/Ren.  
Humanities.  
1 credit.

# ENGL 046. Tolkien and Pullman and Their Literary Roots

A study of Tolkien's _Lord of the Rings_ and Pullman's _His Dark Materials_ in the context of their early English sources. For Tolkien, this will include _Beowulf_ , Old English riddles and elegies, and _Sir Gawain and the Green Knight_. For Pullman, this will include Biblical stories of the Creation and Fall, Milton's _Paradise Lost_ , and selected Blake poems. Some film versions will be included.

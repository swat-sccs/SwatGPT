# DANC 043. African Diaspora Traditions I

DANC 043. Dance Technique: African Diaspora dance traditions l is an exploration of African  
Diaspora dance and drum traditions through kinesthetic engagement and selected philosophical  
and aesthetic perspectives. This course will explore selected dance and drum traditions and  
their associated cultural functions as a way to enter an embodied dialogue in African Diaspora  
dance traditions. Primary focus will be placed on dance and drum traditions from the Mali  
Empire, Haiti, Brazil and the United States. The Philadelphia African Diaspora dance and drum  
community is part of this rich legacy for over 60 years, while, the Swarthmore College Dance  
Department commemorates 30 years of African Diaspora dance and drum traditions. Join us  
and be part of the legacy. Students enrolled in DANC 043 are required to write several detailed  
journals, a dance concert analysis and a short final reflection paper.

# LITR 037G. The Holocaust: History, Representation, and Culture

(Cross-listed as [GMST 037](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112027) and [HIST 037](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110394))  
Seventy-five years after the Holocaust, and despite an enormous amount of research and testimony, the genocide of European Jewry continues to generate compelling interpretive questions. This course is a multidisciplinary exploration of the Holocaust with special attention paid to forms of memory, commemoration, and artistic representations through the study of fiction, poetry, film, memoirs, and historical scholarship.  
Humanities.  
1 credit.

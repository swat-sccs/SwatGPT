# PHIL 042. Descartes in Contemporary Philosophy

Responses to Descartes's early modern philosophical innovations include basic contemporary work in philosophy of mind, epistemology, philosophy of religion, and metaphysics. Readings from authors who may include Frege, Russell, Moore, Husserl, Ryle, Wittgenstein, Kripke, Plantinga, Stroud and others.  
PHIL distribution - (M)H  
Prerequisite: First- and second-year students must complete one introductory level PHIL course before enrolling in this course.

# ENVS 040. Religion and Ecology

(Cross-listed as [RELG 022](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110933))  
Is the environmental crisis a scientific or spiritual problem? Is the crisis a matter of the head or the heart? Can spiritual worldviews - Deep Ecology, Green Christianity, Indigenous Animism, Earth-based Judaism, Eco-Buddhism, etc. - aid the development of Earth-centered philosophies of life?  
Humanities.  
1 credit.  
Eligible for ENVS, ESCH, GLBL-Core, PEAC  
Fall 2026. Wallace.  
Catalog chapter: [Environmental Studies](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=32&poid=2555)

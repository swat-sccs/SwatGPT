# ENVS 132. Wildlife Conservation

Cross-listed with [BIOL 132 ](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=30&coid=99971)  
 _" We protect wildlife for many reasons. It is integral to the balance of nature. It is a source of inspiration. It nurtures a sense of wonder." -World Wildlife Fund_

Students will explore the field of wildlife conservation in this course. The seminar will include 1) a student-driven discussion of primary literature, 2) hands-on experience about how to study wildlife in the Crum Woods, and 3) practice on how to use wildlife data to promote conservation initiatives.   
Prerequisite: Prerequisite: [BIOL 002](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=30&coid=97363) or [ENVS 001](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=30&coid=97739), and [BIOL 036](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=30&coid=97379) or [BIOL 037](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=30&coid=97380); or permission of the instructor.

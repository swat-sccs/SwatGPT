# ENGR 016. Biomedical Modeling and Simulation

(Cross-listed as [BIOL 061](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=112761))  
This course covers the fundamentals of modeling biomedical systems. Topics include: unit analysis, exponential growth/decay, half-life, exponential vs. age-structured death models, sigmoidal growth models, sigmoidal effect models, mass-action reaction rates, and experimental measurement noise models. Students gain hands-on experience developing and analyzing mathmatical models and performing simulations using MATLAB.   
Prerequisite: [MATH 025](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110601)   
Natural sciences and engineering practicum.  
Lab required.  
1 credit.

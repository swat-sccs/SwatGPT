# ENGL 070V. Intermediate Poetry Workshop: Movements & Schools

An incomplete survey of 20th-21st century poetry movements and schools of thought, this workshop will encourage students to envision their own writing within a broader landscape and begin forming their own poetic theories. Previous poetry workshop experience is preferred but not required.  
Graded CR/NC  
Humanities.  
1 credit.  
Fall 2026. Parker.

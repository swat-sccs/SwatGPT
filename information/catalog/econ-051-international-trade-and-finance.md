# ECON 051. International Trade and Finance

This course surveys the theory of international trade and of the balance of payments and exchange rates (international finance). We will primarily touch upon contemporary theories of international trade and open-economy macroeconomics. The theories are used to analyze topics such as trade patterns, trade barriers, international migration and capital flows, contemporary trade disputes, tariffs and protectionism, effects of economic and financial integration on growth and inequality, and the rise of multinational firms.

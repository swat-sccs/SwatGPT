# HIST 001J. First-Year Seminar: London Beyond Control: From the Plague Year to the Public Sphere

The Great Plague of London (1665), Daniel Defoe's _Journal of the Plague Year_ (1722), and the avalanche of imitations inspired by the latter in 2020 will all serve as points of entry into plague as a cultural crisis of modernity that has spawned (and continues to spawn) a vast corpus of new imaginaries of the relationship between self and society, risk and immunity, fact and fiction, private and public, law and justice, freedom and sovereignty.  
Concentration: Migration, Diaspora, and Space  
Social sciences.  
Writing course.

# CHEM 048. Biological Chemistry II

A continuation of [CHEM 038](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109950). More advanced aspects of proteins, nucleic acids, and metabolism will be covered along with an introduction to the structure, function and chemistry of carbohydrates and lipids. Additional topics include the transport of molecules and signals across and within membranes.  
Prerequisite: C- or better in [CHEM 038](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=109950)

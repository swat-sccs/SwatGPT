# MATH 025. Single-Variable Calculus II

The continuation of [MATH 015](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110598), this course covers techniques and applications of integration, convergence properties of infinite sequences and series, the approximation of functions by Taylor polynomials, and an introduction to differential equations.  
Prerequisite: A grade of C+ or better in (or placement out of) [MATH 015](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110598).

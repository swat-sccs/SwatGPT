# PHYS 107. Quantum Mechanics.

An introductory course in quantum mechanics. Topics include waves, photons, the Schrodinger equation, Dirac notation, one-dimensional potentials, quantized angular momentum, and central potentials.   
Prerequisite: [PHYS 005](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110761), [PHYS 008](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110763), multivariable calculus ([[MATH 033](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110607)](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course.php?catoid=7&coid=8628#tt9424), [[MATH 034](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110608)](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course.php?catoid=7&coid=8628#tt5375), or [[MATH 035](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110609)](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course.php?catoid=7&coid=8628#tt7352))

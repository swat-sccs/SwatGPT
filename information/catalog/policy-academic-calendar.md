# Academic Calendar

Academic Calendar

Fall 2026 | August 25 | Residence halls open for new students  
---|---  
August 21-24 | International Student Orientation  
August 25-30 | New Student Orientation.  
August 27 | Advising begins. All-advisor meeting in morning; individual advising begins in afternoon.  
August 28 | Residence halls open for returning students. Computer preregistration for first-year and transfer students only.  
August 30 | Meal plan starts at dinner.  
August 31 | Classes begin.  
September 7 | Labor Day. No classes.  
September 14 | Drop/add ends. Last day to delete a course from or add to permanent registration. Last day to uncover a shadow grade for a CR course from the previous semester.  
October 1  | Final examination schedule available online.  
October 5 | All outstanding incompletes from Spring semester must be complete and graded.  
October 9 | Fall Break begins after last class.  
October 19 | Fall classes resume at 8:30 a.m.  
October 28 | Schedule of courses and seminars for next semester available online.  
November 6 | Last day to declare CR/NC grading option. Last day to withdraw from a course and receive the grade notation "W."  
November 2- November 13 | Advising period.  
November 17-19 | Pre-enrollment for spring semester. Pre-enrollment ends at 4 p.m.  
November 25 | Thanksgiving Break begins after last class.  
November 30 | Fall classes resume at 8:30 a.m.  
December 1 | Note: All accounts must show a zero or positive balance to enroll or select a room for spring semester.  
December 9 | Classes end  
December 13-19 | Finals exam period  
December 18 | 100-level seminars end. The fourteenth meetings of Fall 100-level seminars may meet in either the seminar format or for a final exam, up to the discretion of the professor.  
December 19 | Finals end at noon. Residence halls close at 6 p.m. Meal plan ends at lunch.  
|   
  
Spring 2027

Jan 16 | Residence halls open at noon  
---|---  
Jan 17 | Meal plan starts at dinner  
Jan 18 | Martin Luther King Jr. (MLK) Day Holiday. No classes on MLK Day.  
Jan 19 | Classes begin  
Feb 1 | Drop/add ends. Last day to delete a course from or add one to permanent registration. Last day to uncover a shadow grade for a CR course from the previous semester.  
Feb 22 |  All outstanding incompletes from Fall semester must be complete and graded  
Mar 5 | Final examination schedule available on-line  
Mar 5 | Spring Break Begins after last class  
Mar 15 | Spring Classes Resume  
Mar 24 | Schedule of courses and seminars for next semester available on-line  
Mar 26 | Last day to declare CR/NC grading option. Last day to withdraw from a course and receive the grade "W"  
Apr 1 | Note: All accounts must show a $0 or positive balance to enroll and select a room for the fall semester  
Mar 29 - Apr 9 | Advising period  
Apr 13 - 15 | Pre-enrollment for fall semester  
Apr 15 | Pre-enrollment ends at 4 p.m.  
Apr 30 | Classes and seminars end, as originally scheduled.   
May 6 - 13 | Course Final & Honors written examinations. If you must make travel arrangements before the examination schedule is published, do not plan to leave until after finals  
May 14 | Meal plan ends at dinner for all but seniors  
May 14 | Residence halls close to all but seniors . (Non-seniors are expected to leave the college within 24 hours after their last examination)  
May 16 - 18 | Honors Orals  
May 18 | Faculty Deadline for Senior Grades Due, Tuesday noon.  
May 18 | Senior Deadline for CR Shadow Grade Uncovering, Tuesday 11:59PM. No extensions.  
May 22 | Baccalaureate  
May 23 | Commencement  
May 24 | Residence halls close  
May 27 - 30 | Alumni weekend  
May 27 | Faculty Deadline for All Spring Grades Due

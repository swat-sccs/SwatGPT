# MATH 034. Several-Variable Calculus

Same topics as [MATH 033](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110607) except in more depth using the concepts of linear algebra. The department strongly recommends that students take linear algebra first so that they are eligible for this course. Students may take only one of [MATH 033](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110607), MATH 034, and [MATH 035](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110609) for credit.  
Prerequisite: Both (1) a grade of C- or better in, or placement out of, [MATH 025](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110601); and (2) a grade of C- or better in, or placement out of a [MATH 027](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110603) or [MATH 028](https://catalog.swarthmore.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=32&coid=110604).

# Making the gradebook visible to students

By default, students cannot see the gradebook.  To let students see their own grades follow the steps below. Students cannot see grades for other students. 

  1. Go to your course
  2. Click **Settings **(under course title)
  3. In the **Appearance** section, change **Show gradebook to students** to "**Yes**."
  4. Scroll down and click **Save and display**

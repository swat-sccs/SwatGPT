# Record Your Name Pronunciation in Moodle

## Recording your name

Recording how to pronounce your name will make it easier for professors and students to learn how to say it. To start, log into [Moodle](https://moodle.swarthmore.edu). 

  1. Click on your name (top right) and select **Profile**
  2. Under **User details** , select **Edit profile**
  3. Above the **Description** text box, select the microphone icon and record your name
  4. Select **Attach recording**
  5. Go to the bottom of the page and select **Update profile**



To update the pronunciation, edit the Description section. Delete the original recording and make a new recording. 

## Listening to others' name pronunciations

To hear how to pronounce other people’s names in Moodle: 

  1. In your Moodle course, select **Participants** from the left-hand navigation column
  2. Click on a name
  3. Play the audio located under their name and photo



### Faculty Services

For professors, student name pronunciations are available in Faculty Services in the following locations: 

  * Each student’s individual record
  * Course rosters
  * Class photo list (linked from course roster page)

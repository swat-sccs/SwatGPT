# Printing for Students

Printers are available in public computing areas and in the residence halls for student use. There is no charge for printing but we ask students to refrain from printing multiple copies or wasting paper. Large print jobs such as multi-copy color printing, binding, and posters for student organizations are handled by [Print Services](https://www.swarthmore.edu/print-services). 

To connect your personal device to student printers, visit this webpage: <http://restech.swarthmore.edu:9163/setup> (Link only available on campus) The Mobility Print application will guide you through installing **GarnetPrint** and one or more of the available dorm printers below. 

**GarnetPrint** \- A shared queue for all of the student Canon printers equipped with release stations. Send B&W jobs to **GarnetPrint** and swipe to release your pages at any of the high volume Canon printers across campus. Print jobs will be removed from the queue if not released within three hours.

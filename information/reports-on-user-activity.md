# Reports on user activity

Moodle keeps tracks of user activity in Moodle.  To view information from the Moodle logs, click on** Reports** at the top of the course page.  There are a variety of reports on Moodle activity that are available. 

  
**Dates** \- lists all of the course activities that have a date associated with them (ie. course start date, due dates, etc.) 

**Logs** \- reports on all activity within the course 

**Live Logs** \- quick report on course activity in the last hour 

**Activity report** \- number of views for each activity in the course.  Does not include any user information. 

**Course participation** \- report on if students view or post to a specific activity within the course over the past week. 

**Activity completion** \- check boxes for each course activity for each participant 

**Roster** \- course participants with photos (if available) 

**Statistics** \- Nothing currently

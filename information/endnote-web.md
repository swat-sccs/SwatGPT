## About EndNote Basic

EndNote Basic (formerly EndNote Web) is a free online Reference Manager. This makes accessing your information easier because it is available to any computer with a web connection. You can also share selected parts of your library with other EndNote users.

## Installation Instructions

  1. Go to the EndNote website at [www.myendnoteweb.com](https://www.myendnoteweb.com) and register for an account with your Swarthmore email address
  2. Sign into the EndNote website
  3. Go to **Downloads**
  4. To capture websites, add **Capture Reference** button to your browser bookmarks toolbar 
     1. Captures references from web pages
     2. Some databases, such as Google Scholar and PubMed, allow you to capture an entire page of references all at once
  5. To add to Microsoft Word, download **Cite While You Write** for Windows or Mac
  6. Open the installer and follow the **Installation Instructions** presented

# Giving Honors Examiners access to my Moodle course

Although it is technically possible to give a guest access to a Moodle course (see [Making a course available to students](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Making_a_course_available_to_students "Making a course available to students")), the [Honors Program](https://www.swarthmore.edu/honors-program) does not permit Honors Examiners to see the contents of a Moodle course in order to ensure that the examiner is not prejudiced by any student work that may be available in Moodle.  Contact the [Director of the Honors Program](https://www.swarthmore.edu/honors-program) for more details. 

To share important readings and files from your course, you can download the files from Moodle then upload them to a Google Drive folder and share the folder with the Honors Examiner. For more information on downloading files from Moodle in bulk, see [Export or backup a course from Moodle](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Export_or_backup_a_course_from_Moodle "Export or backup a course from Moodle").

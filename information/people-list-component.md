# People List Component

[← Return to the Drupal User Guide](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Drupal_Home "Drupal Home")

**People Lists** , commonly referred to as faculty/staff lists or directories, are components that display a curated list of College community members associated with a department, organization, or group. These lists include useful contact information such as job title, email address, phone number, and office location. 

While this component can only be created by ITS administrators, the individuals included in a **People List** can be managed by users who have attended the Introductory Drupal training session. Additional editing options are available for users who have completed the Advanced Drupal training session. 

To request that a **People List** component be added to your site, email [support@swarthmore.edu](mailto:support@swarthmore.edu).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why doesn't our new faculty/staff member show up as an option in the _Add People_ list?

Drupal synchronizes user information nightly. If a new employee has only recently received a College account, they may not appear in the system until after the next synchronization. 

If the account still does not appear after one business day, contact [web-update@swarthmore.edu](mailto:web-update@swarthmore.edu). 

### I've noticed that some individuals have additional information listed along with their contact information (website link, interests, office hours, etc.). How do I add this information?

If you would like to include additional information with a **People List** entry, see [User Contact Information and Profiles](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/index.php?title=User_Contact_Information_and_Profiles&action=edit&redlink=1 "User Contact Information and Profiles \(page does not exist\)"). 

If you're attempting to edit another person's profile, see [Profiles](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Profiles "Profiles"). Editing another user's profile requires Advanced Drupal editing privileges.

## Browser Extensions

There is robust support for [Read&Write as a browser extension](https://support.texthelp.com/help/readwrite-for-google-chrome-readwrite-software-downloads). As part of our license, we also have access to the free version of [OrbitNote's browser extension](https://academy.texthelp.com/orbitnote/howtos/how-to-get-the-orbitnote-extension/) and to Screenshot Reader. You will need to download these for your browser to make use of them.

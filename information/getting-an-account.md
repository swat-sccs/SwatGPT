## Faculty & Staff

All employees of Swarthmore College are provided an employee ID and username after their employment requirements are completed by Human Resources (staff) or the Provost's Office (faculty). Most staff will receive their account information during new employee orientation, otherwise your hiring manager will contact you with instructions. 

If you are an employee and have not received your account username, notify Human Resources for staff (**610-328-8397**) or the Provost's Office for faculty (**610-328-8319**). 

### Account Usage

Your username and password can be used to access most campus digital services, such as: 

  * The campus network (Wi-Fi)
  * Google Workspace, including Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets
  * [mySwarthmore](http://myswat.swarthmore.edu)
  * Banner administrative software
  * [Moodle](https://moodle.swarthmore.edu), Swarthmore's learning management system



For guidelines on responsible conduct while using Swarthmore accounts and services, please review our [Acceptable Use Policy for Swarthmore College computer systems and networks](https://www.swarthmore.edu/its/acceptable-use). 

## Students

All students registered for a Swarthmore class are assigned a student ID and username. Accounts are created automatically for new students in the fall and spring. You will be sent instructions for accessing your account upon initial enrollment. 

If you do not have a username please contact the Registrar's office ([registrar@swarthmore.edu](mailto:registrar@swarthmore.edu)). 

### Bryn Mawr College, Haverford College, University of Pennsylvania, and Strath Haven High School students who need to log into Moodle

You must be registered for a Swarthmore course to log into Moodle, and you'll need to use a Swarthmore Account to do so.  After registering for a course, you will receive an email with your username and instructions for setting up your Swarthmore password. If you have not yet registered for a course, but need access to the content, ask your professor about guest access

## Setting or Changing Your Password

In order to use your Swarthmore account, you will need to establish your password. You may be required to change your password periodically based on length and complexity. You may choose to change your password more often. 

To set or change your Swarthmore password, please visit [password.swarthmore.edu](https://password.swarthmore.edu). For step-by-step help visit our [password change instructions](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Resetting_Email_and_Network_Passwords "Resetting Email and Network Passwords"). For help choosing a password visit our [password tips page](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Password_Tips "Password Tips"). Remember to also check our page on [what to update after changing your password](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/What_to_Update_After_Changing_Your_Password "What to Update After Changing Your Password"). 

## Guest Accounts

### SwatGuest Wireless Accounts

College visitors who do not have Swarthmore IDs or belong to an eduroam institution may use the wireless network called SwatGuest. General guest accounts may be attained by following the [self-service guest Wi-Fi instructions](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Self-Service_Guest_Wireless "Self-Service Guest Wireless"). 

If you are a Tri-Co student enrolled in a Swarthmore course, review [how to log in to a Moodle course](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Log_in_to_Moodle_at_Swarthmore "Log in to Moodle at Swarthmore"). 

### Sponsored Guest Accounts

Swarthmore Faculty and Staff can request a sponsored guest account for non-Swarthmore employees that need access to a system that requires a Swarthmore login. To request an account, follow the link to the ITS Service Portal (requires login): [service account request form](https://support.swarthmore.edu/support/catalog/items/136).

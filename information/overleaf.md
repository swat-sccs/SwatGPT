## Description

Overleaf is an online, collaborative LaTeX editor. In many cases, the free version is sufficient, but the paid license includes the ability to add more collaborators and integrate with other services such as GitHub, Dropbox, and Zotero.

Website: [www.overleaf.com](https://www.overleaf.com)

## Licensing

ITS maintains a limited number of Overleaf licenses for employees. Submit a help ticket to request a license. 

## Version

Swarthmore has the "Standard" license that includes unlimited projects with up to 10 collaborators per project. 

## Account Setup

Use your Swarthmore email as the primary email address. You can add a non-Swarthmore email as a [secondary email address](https://docs.overleaf.com/accounts-and-security/email-address-and-login-options/managing-your-email-addresses#secondary-email-addresses-are-helpful) .

# Learning Spaces

ITS supports technology in all classrooms as well as providing specialized learning spaces. 

## Classroom Technology Support

[The Classroom and Conferencing Technologies (CCT)](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Classroom_and_Conferencing_Technology_Support "Classroom and Conferencing Technology Support") group supports A/V systems for academic classrooms. They can assist with classroom projector systems, lecture capture, video web-conferencing, and other digital media technologies. 

## Specialized Learning Spaces

ITS supports a number of spaces for specialized uses. 

  * [Audio Recording & Podcast Studio](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Audio_Recording_%26_Podcast_Studio "Audio Recording & Podcast Studio")
  * [Creative Media Commons](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Creative_Media_Commons "Creative Media Commons")
  * [Language Center](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Language_Center "Language Center")
  * [MakerSpace](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/MakerSpace "MakerSpace")

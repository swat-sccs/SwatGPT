# FMST Equipment Checkout

Digital cinema cameras, sound recording equipment, and production peripherals are available for checkout in Martin Hall room 138. Priority for most equipment is given to students who are actively enrolled in FMST production classes. Some equipment (DSLR cameras, sound recorders, tripods) are available to the community. Checkout hours are 12:30pm-7:30pm M-F during the academic year. A complete list of equipment can be [found here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/17HqmQXpZKXF3D8fDqpDfDZDetPxjuCKU4mo3VG0P0Rw/edit?usp=sharing).

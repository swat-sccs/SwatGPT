# Log in to Moodle at Swarthmore

## Contents

  * 1 Swarthmore Faculty, Staff, and Students
  * 2 Bryn Mawr College, Haverford College, University of Pennsylvania, and Strath Haven High School students
  * 3 Visitors
  * 4 Guests



## Swarthmore Faculty, Staff, and Students

Go to [moodle.swarthmore.edu](https://moodle.swarthmore.edu), and click the **Login via Swarthmore** button under the **Swarthmore Users** tab.  If you are already logged into another Swarthmore service (such as Gmail), you may be logged in automatically without being prompted to enter a password. 

## Bryn Mawr College, Haverford College, University of Pennsylvania, and Strath Haven High School students

You must be registered for a Swarthmore course to log into Moodle.  After registering for a course, you will receive an email with your username and instructions for setting up your Swarthmore password. For more information see the [Tri-Co Students](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Tri-Co_Students "Tri-Co Students") help page. 

Once you have your Swarthmore credentials set up, go to [moodle.swarthmore.edu](https://moodle.swarthmore.edu/), and click the **Login via Swarthmore** button under the **Swarthmore Users** tab 

If you have not yet registered for a course, but need access to the content, ask your professor about guest access. See [Making a course available to students](https://kb.swarthmore.edu/wiki/Making_a_course_available_to_students "Making a course available to students"). 

## Visitors

ITS creates special manual accounts for visiting users who are not officially registered for a course. 

For these users, visit [moodle.swarthmore.edu,](https://moodle.swarthmore.edu) click on the **Visitor** tab on the login screen, then enter the username and password provided.  If you do not know your username or password, click on the link labeled **Forgotten your username or password?** and enter your username or email. 

## Guests

Moodle guest access is provided for some courses. Guests are provided with a course url and password. Guests do not have a username. To access your course, go to the course url, then enter the guest password.
